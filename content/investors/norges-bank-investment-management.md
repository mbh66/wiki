Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM), which manages about US\$2 trillion for Norway, is on the register's watch list: it has published nature expectations for companies, and no nature performance bond names it [IC].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags. No nature performance deal names NBIM in the sources checked, so the register lists it on the watch list for the policies, research or products below.

NBIM's March 2026 nature expectations ask companies to assess their dependencies and impacts on nature with methods tied to TNFD and ISSB, to set targets, and to give boards oversight of critical land, water and ocean habitats. They do not mention nature bonds [IC]. Pictet Asset Management names NBIM among investors embedding nature risk [MS].
