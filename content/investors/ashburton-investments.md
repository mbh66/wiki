Ashburton Investments, part of the FirstRand group, invested in the Cape Water Performance-Based Bond [MS].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB) | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Investor | Not disclosed | [MS] |

Ashburton is a related party to the issuer, [[investors/firstrand-bank|FirstRand Bank]]. With the [[investors/eskom-pension-and-provident-fund|Eskom Pension and Provident Fund]], [[investors/optimum-investment-group|Optimum Investment Group]] and [[investors/sanlam-life|Sanlam Life]], it holds roughly R316 million of the bond between them, on the disclosed figures for the three co-anchors.
