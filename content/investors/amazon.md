Amazon buys the carbon removal units generated by the spekboom restoration behind the World Bank's 2026 Spekboom Restoration Outcome Bond, under a fixed-price offtake of more than a decade; those purchases fund the bond's variable coupon [IC].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| World Bank Spekboom Restoration Outcome Bond | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Carbon removal unit offtaker funding the variable coupon | Not disclosed | [IC] |

The project, run by Imperative in the Eastern Cape, aims to restore 50,000 hectares [IC]. The register includes Amazon because its purchases are the economic source of the outcome payment.
