Coface says it is one of 11 private reinsurers of the US DFC's US$656 million political-risk insurance on the 2023 Galápagos Marine Bond, committed for 10 years [MS].

Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. ## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Galápagos Marine Bond (Ecuador) | 2023 | Debt-for-nature conversion | Reinsurer of US DFC political-risk cover | Not disclosed | [MS] |

The other ten reinsurers on the Galápagos panel are not named in any source found. The register places Coface in Tier 1 as a credit insurer that operates internationally.

## What it has said

Coface says its participation helps Ecuador work toward 30% marine protection by 2030, reduce its debt, protect Galápagos biodiversity and mobilize private capital [MS].
