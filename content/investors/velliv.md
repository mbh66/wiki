Velliv, a Danish pension and life insurance company, was a lead investor in the World Bank's 2024 Amazon Reforestation-Linked Outcome Bond [IC].

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

Velliv has bought more World Bank outcome bonds than any insurer in the register.

## What it has said

"Velliv is very pleased to participate in the Amazon Reforestation-Linked Bond" (Thor Schultz Christensen, Deputy CIO). Velliv tied the deal to its carbon-reduction, social and biodiversity priorities and to its "pension savers" [IC].
