IFC made the largest disclosed single allocation in the register outside the debt conversions: about R1.6 billion of the R2.5 billion Cape Water Performance-Based Bond, as lead co-anchor investor [IC].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB) | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Lead co-anchor investor | About R1.6bn | [IC] |

The JSE names IFC, [[investors/fsd-africa-investments|FSD Africa Investments]] and [[investors/aluwani-capital-partners|Aluwani Capital Partners]] as the bond's co-anchor investors [IC]. IFC funded its position through a new R1.6 billion borrowing facility with [[investors/citi|Citi]]; the World Bank's release of 14 April 2026 says the facility "has supported IFC's anchor investment into the Cape Water outcome-based bond" [IC]. With FSD Africa Investments, IFC supplied about R1.83 billion of the R2.5 billion issue on disclosed figures. The bond's mechanics, and IFC's stated reasons for anchoring it, are set out in [[research/cape-water-bond-co-anchor-investors|Why Three Investors Anchored the Cape Water Bond]].
