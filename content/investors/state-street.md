State Street is on the register's watch list: it has published an insight piece on nature-linked bonds, and no nature performance deal names it [TBV].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags. No nature performance deal names State Street in the sources checked, so the register lists it on the watch list for the policies, research or products below.

The piece is titled "EMEA asset owners turn to nature-linked bonds for positive outcomes and returns"; only its landing page was retrieved, so its content and date are unverified [TBV]. State Street quit Climate Action 100+ in February 2024, and its US arm stayed out of the relaunched Net Zero Asset Managers initiative while its EU and UK entities joined [IC]. It remains a defendant in the Texas antitrust case [IC].

## What it has said

State Street called the Texas suit "baseless and without merit" [MS].
