CSAA Insurance Group invested in the first two Forest Resilience Bonds, Yuba I and Yuba II, which finance forest restoration on the Tahoe National Forest in California [MS].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes CSAA Insurance Group only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. CSAA Insurance Group has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. ## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yuba I Forest Resilience Bond | 2018 | Impact or resilience bond | Investor | Share of US$4.6m | [MS] |
| Yuba II Forest Resilience Bond | 2021 | Impact or resilience bond | Market-rate investor | Share of US$25m | [MS] |

CSAA is the only insurer in the register that has invested in a nature performance instrument in its own underwriting region. No source quotes it linking the restoration to its own wildfire losses. The register places it in Tier 2 as a regional insurer; its only documented deals are domestic, which would also support Tier 3.

## What it has said

"We earn a good return while helping the environment and our policyholders" (Linc Walworth, 2018) [MS]. WRI described the investment as "a way to diversify its portfolio while supporting the communities in which it operates" [MS]. "We recognize the importance of investing in solutions that help reduce risk and protect our communities" (Gretchen Tai, 2021) [MS].
