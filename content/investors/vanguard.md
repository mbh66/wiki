## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags. No nature performance deal names Vanguard in the sources checked, so the register lists it on the watch list for the policies, research or products below.

Vanguard settled on 26 February 2026 for US$29.5 million and accepted "Passivity Commitments", under which it will not use divestment, threats of divestment or opposition to directors as levers on environmental and social conduct; its US entities agreed not to join initiatives such as the Net Zero Asset Managers initiative [IC]. Texas Attorney General Paxton called the settlement "historic, industry-changing"; Ropes & Gray said Vanguard had agreed only to current industry norms; the NYU Stern author argued it gives up effective stewardship tools [IC]. The settlement governs stewardship and coalition membership. It does not bar the purchase of any particular bond.

## What it has said

Vanguard said it was "disappointed" when the court denied most of the motions to dismiss in August 2025 [MS].
