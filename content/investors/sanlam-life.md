Sanlam Life, the life insurance company of the Sanlam group, invested in the Cape Water Performance-Based Bond [MS].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB) | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Investor | Not disclosed | [MS] |

FSD Africa's release and Africa Global Funds name Sanlam Life among the bond's investors; RMB's release names no investors [MS]. The register places Sanlam Life in Tier 3 for this domestic role, although the Sanlam group operates across Africa.
