CIBC Caribbean, formerly CIBC FirstCaribbean, was domestic lead arranger of Barbados's 2022 Blue Bonds conversion and lead arranger of the country's 2024 debt-for-climate-resilience operation [MS].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes CIBC Caribbean only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. CIBC Caribbean has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. On register pages, [MS] marks a claim that rests only on the institution's own statement or on statements by parties to the same deal.

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barbados Blue Bonds conversion | 2022 | Debt-for-nature conversion | Domestic lead arranger | About US$150m | [MS] |
| Barbados debt-for-climate-resilience conversion | 2024 | Adjacent | Lead arranger | Freed US$125m | [MS] |

The 2024 operation funds a sewage plant and water reclamation, with sustainability-linked targets and penalties paid into the Barbados Environmental Sustainability Fund, so the register treats it as adjacent [MS]. The register places CIBC Caribbean in Tier 3 as a regional bank arranging deals at home; its parent is Canadian.
