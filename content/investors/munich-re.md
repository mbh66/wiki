Munich Re writes parametric insurance for coral reefs: it joined the Mesoamerican Reef program at the July 2022 renewal and insured The Nature Conservancy's Hawaii reefs from 2022 [MS].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Munich Re only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. Munich Re has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. On register pages, [MS] marks a claim that rests only on the institution's own statement or on statements by parties to the same deal.

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mesoamerican Reef parametric insurance (MAR Fund) | 2022 onward | Adjacent | Parametric insurer | Shared program | [MS] |
| The Nature Conservancy's Hawaii reef insurance | 2022 to 2024 | Adjacent | Parametric insurer, chosen from seven bids | Up to US\$1m per storm, US\$2m per year (2024) | [MS] |

Parametric reef cover is adjacent to the register's scope: it protects a natural asset and pays on a wind-speed trigger, and no investor return depends on ecological performance. The 2024 Hawaii policy reportedly cost about US\$106,000 and was arranged by [[investors/wtw|WTW]] [MS]. Munich Re was searched for and not found in any debt-for-nature deal.
