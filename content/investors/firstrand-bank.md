FirstRand Bank issued the R2.5 billion Cape Water Performance-Based Bond in April 2026, the first nature outcome bond in the register issued by a commercial bank [IC].

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB) | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Issuer | R2.5bn, five-year senior unsecured, due 30 June 2031 | [IC] |

The bond listed on the JSE on 17 April 2026 [IC]. Part of what investors earn depends on independently verified clearing of invasive alien plants in the Boland Groot Winterhoek [[strategic-water-source-area-swsa|strategic water source area]], which feeds Cape Town's dams [IC]. In a Business Day column, Michael Avery, summarizing RMB's Martin Potgieter, writes that if the project falls short investors earn less, "but, crucially, their capital remains protected" [MS]. The FirstRand group holds several roles in the deal: [[investors/rand-merchant-bank|Rand Merchant Bank]] arranged it, the [[investors/firstrand-foundation|FirstRand Foundation]] anchored the outcome funding, and [[investors/ashburton-investments|Ashburton Investments]] is among the buyers [MS].
