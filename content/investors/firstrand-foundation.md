The FirstRand Foundation was the anchor outcome funder of the Cape Water Performance-Based Bond, committing R50 million and coordinating the other philanthropic partners [IC].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes the FirstRand Foundation only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. The FirstRand Foundation has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. ## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB) | 2026 | Outcome-linked bond | Anchor outcome funder and coordinator | R50m | [IC] |

Outcome funders pay the bond's success premium only when an independent verifier confirms the cleared hectares. The other outcome funders are the [[investors/development-bank-of-southern-africa|DBSA]] (R50 million through its Green Fund), [[investors/remgro|Remgro]], the [[investors/rupert-nature-foundation|Rupert Nature Foundation]] and the [[investors/lewis-foundation|Lewis Foundation]] [IC for DBSA; MS for the others].
