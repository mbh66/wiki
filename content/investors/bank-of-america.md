Bank of America arranged two sovereign debt-for-nature conversions, Gabon in 2023 and the Ecuador Amazon Biocorridor in 2024, more than any other bank after Credit Suisse [IC].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Bank of America only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. Bank of America has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. On register pages, [MS] marks a claim that rests only on the institution's own statement or on statements by parties to the same deal.

## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gabon Blue Bond | 2023 | Debt-for-nature conversion | Arranger | US$500m | [IC] |
| Ecuador Amazon Biocorridor conversion | 2024 | Debt-for-nature conversion | Sole structuring agent, dealer manager, arranger, initial purchaser | US$1bn | [IC] |

Both deals carried [[investors/us-international-development-finance-corporation|US DFC]] political-risk insurance; the Ecuador deal also carried an [[investors/inter-american-development-bank|IDB]] liquidity guarantee [IC]. The Ecuador conversion refinanced about US$1.53 billion of bonds and is expected to provide about US$400 million in new conservation funding over 17 years [IC].
