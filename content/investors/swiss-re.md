Swiss Re Corporate Solutions reinsured part of the US DFC's political-risk cover on the 2023 Gabon Blue Bond, and Swiss Re co-developed and reinsured the first parametric insurance for a coral reef, in Quintana Roo, Mexico [MS].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Swiss Re only through its published statements and the public records of the deals listed. Swiss Re has not yet been invited to comment. Responses and corrections will be recorded here with their date. ## In the register

The register follows instruments whose terms depend on a verified ecological outcome, the broad family of the [[performance-based-bond|performance-based bond]]. The [[investors/index|register index]] explains its tiers, roles and claim tags.

| Deal | Year | Family | Role | Amount | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gabon Blue Bond | 2023 | Debt-for-nature conversion | Reinsurer of US DFC political-risk cover (Swiss Re Corporate Solutions) | Share of about US$250m private panel | [MS] |
| Quintana Roo reef parametric insurance | 2019 | Adjacent | Reinsurer and co-developer | US$850,000 payout after Hurricane Delta (2020) | [MS] |

Swiss Re and DFC each state the Gabon panel [MS]. The Quintana Roo policy pays when wind speeds of 96 knots or more pass within 60 kilometers of the reef; premiums come from the state and a hotel levy [MS]. Swiss Re's own research positions on nature and losses were not researched for the register.

## What it has said

Swiss Re ties the Quintana Roo cover to the reef's role in the state's tourism economy and in protecting the coast from disasters and erosion [MS].

## Open questions

Dialogue Earth puts the 2020 payout at "almost US$800,000" against Swiss Re's US$850,000 [TBV]. Accounts differ on whether Seguros Banorte or Seguros Afirme was the primary insurer [TBV].
