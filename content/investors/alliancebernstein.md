## What it has said

On debt conversions, AllianceBernstein wrote: "Our analysis persuaded us to consider Ecuador's Galápagos bond and avoid the Gabon issue," and "Our engagement also helped secure a significantly higher yield than the average." The article does not state a purchase [MS].
