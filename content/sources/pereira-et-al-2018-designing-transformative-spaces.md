Pereira, L., Karpouzoglou, T., Frantzeskaki, N. and Olsson, P. (2018). Designing transformative spaces for sustainability in social-ecological systems. *Ecology and Society* 23(4): 32.

The paper names and describes transformative spaces: settings, often convened by researchers, where people with different stakes can try new ways of living with land and water away from everyday pressures. It discusses how such spaces are designed and opened. The wiki treats a transformative space as one kind of protected space and keeps its evidence separate from that on niches and incubators.
