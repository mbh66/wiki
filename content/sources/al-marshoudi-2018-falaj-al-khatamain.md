Al-Marshoudi, A. S. (2018). "Water Institutional Arrangements of Falaj Al Khatamain in the Sultanate of Oman." *Journal of Earth Science and Engineering* 8: 51-65. https://doi.org/10.17265/2159-581X/2018.02.001

Using interviews with the falaj's *wakils*, *areefs* and the *bayt al-mal* supervisor, and the ministry's flow records, the author describes how Falaj al-Khatmeen's shares are divided between its upper and lower channels, how its common right is rented on annual contracts to fixed renters, and why it has no auction market: its flow varies less than that of neighboring aflaj that auction their common rights. The journal's publisher is often listed as having weak peer review, so the wiki treats the flow analysis as unconfirmed and uses the paper alongside Al-Marshoudi and Sulong (2024), which reports the same institutional difference.
