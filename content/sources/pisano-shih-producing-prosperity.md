Pisano, G. P., & Shih, W. C. (2012). *Producing Prosperity: Why America Needs a Manufacturing Renaissance*. Boston: Harvard Business Review Press.

Pisano and Shih argue that manufacturing and innovation share an industrial commons of skills, suppliers and know-how rooted in particular places, and that offshoring production erodes the commons and with it the capacity to innovate. The wiki takes the concept as the regional form of Weber's agglomeration factor, and as the reason a bioregion that wants to make things must rebuild shared capability before it can expect the gains of a cluster.
