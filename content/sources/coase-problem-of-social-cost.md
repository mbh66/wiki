Coase, R. H. (1960). "The Problem of Social Cost." *Journal of Law and Economics*, 3, 1–44.

Coase replied to Pigou that harm is reciprocal and that, where property rights are clear and bargaining costs little, the parties will reach the efficient outcome whoever bears the liability. Where transaction costs are high, as with diffuse pollution, the allocation of rights and the choice of institution decide the result. The wiki uses the paper beside Pigou: it explains why external costs carried by many dispersed people, and by ecosystems, go unsettled by bargaining.
