Liodaki, D., Sattler, M., Papadimitropoulos, V. and Lang, T. (2025). *Multiple Geographies of Commons-Based Peer Production*. Urban Planning 10, article 10172. Published 15 October 2025. DOI: [10.17645/up.10172](https://doi.org/10.17645/up.10172). Open access (CC BY 4.0).

The authors review research on commons-based peer production and its cosmo-local form [TBV]. They treat cosmolocalism as promising, and fault the research for neglecting the material side of production, the cost of hardware, exclusions in the Global South and the play of power across scales [TBV]. They describe design and making as concentrated in the Global North [TBV].

The article is the main independent critique of cosmo-localism in this research, and the source through which several earlier readings of the design global, manufacture local papers were made.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
