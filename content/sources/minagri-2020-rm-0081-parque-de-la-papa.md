Ministerio de Agricultura y Riego (2020). *Resolución Ministerial N.° 0081-2020-MINAGRI*. Reconocen la Zona de Agrobiodiversidad denominada "Parque de la Papa". Lima, 5 March 2020.

The resolution recognizes the Zona de Agrobiodiversidad Parque de la Papa in Pisac district, covering 7,238.33 hectares across four comunidades campesinas: Chahuaytire, Amaru, Paru Paru and Pampallacta. It directs INIA to prepare a master plan in agreement with the communities. The wiki cites it as the official record of the park's territory, which is smaller than the 12,000 hectares given in partner accounts.
