Reed, J., van Vianen, J., Barlow, J., & Sunderland, T. (2017). Have integrated landscape approaches reconciled societal and environmental issues in the tropics? *Land Use Policy*, 63, 481-492.

The authors reviewed the implementation of landscape approaches across the tropics and did not find a single applied example in the scientific literature that demonstrated, with reliable and in-depth data, the effective balancing of social and environmental trade-offs. Their assessment of 150 grey-literature cases and 24 peer-reviewed studies shows potential gains in social capital, income, employment and resource conservation, and associates multi-level or [[polycentricity|polycentric]] governance with success. They conclude that the approaches remain nascent in concept and practice. The wiki cites it as the benchmark for honest claims about [[glossary/f-l/integrated-landscape-management|integrated landscape management]] outcomes, and as a reason for BioHubs to build verification into their services from the start.
