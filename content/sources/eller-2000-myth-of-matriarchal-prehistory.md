Cynthia Eller (2000). *The Myth of Matriarchal Prehistory: Why an Invented Past Will Not Give Women a Future*. Beacon Press. ISBN 978-0-8070-6792-5. [Beacon Press](https://www.beacon.org/The-Myth-of-Matriarchal-Prehistory-P363.aspx).

Eller, a scholar of religion, traces how the story of a peaceful, woman-centred and goddess-worshipping prehistory took shape in feminist spirituality [IC]. She then tests the story against the archaeological and ethnographic record [IC]. She questions whether the archaeology of Marija Gimbutas supports claims of matriarchal societies [IC]. She concludes that the evidence does not show such a prehistory, and that the myth is at best unnecessary and at worst harmful to feminism [IC].

Publishers Weekly gave the book a starred review [IC]. Natalie Angier's review praised how it "carefully clips every thread" of the myth [TBV]. Max Dashu answered the book in *Feminist Theology* (2005) [TBV]. The chapters were not read for this record; the coding rests on the publisher's description and reviews [TBV].

The wiki takes from it the main published critique of a lost partnership or goddess age [TBV].
