Smith, S. M. and Andersson, K. (2025). "Conserving Groundwater: Why Irrigators Chose to Tax Themselves." *Elementa: Science of the Anthropocene* 13(1): 00081. https://doi.org/10.1525/elementa.2024.00081

The authors trace Subdistrict 1 from its formation in 2006 and the fee's rise from US\$45 to US\$150 an acre-foot, find pumping about 30% below the counterfactual through 2021, report that farm values recovered most of their post-2006 loss, and conclude that the fee has not stabilized or recovered the aquifer, which stood 1.21 million acre-feet below its 2000 level in 2023. They read the 2024 decision to raise the fee to US\$500 as a shift toward a cap with tradeable allowances. The wiki uses it as the fullest account of the case.
