Ned Block (1981). *Review of The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind*. Cognition and Brain Theory 4(1): 81-83.

The philosopher Ned Block's short review in *Cognition and Brain Theory* (1981) allows that people's concept of mind may have changed in the ancient world [TBV], and argues that such a change is a different thing from a change in experience [IC]. On Block's reading, Jaynes slides from the first to the second. The Julian Jaynes Society has published replies [IC]. The wiki takes the review as the standard statement of the line between content and structure, which the grid keeps square by square, and as the main critique of step A3 as Jaynes states it.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a critic in philosophy of mind, with evidence type E4 and claim codes A3.

Glossary: [[glossary/a-e/bicameral-mind|Bicameral Mind]].
