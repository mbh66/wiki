UNCTAD. (2026, January). *Global Investment Trends Monitor*, No. 50. Geneva: United Nations Conference on Trade and Development.

UNCTAD's first estimate of foreign direct investment in 2025: flows to developed economies up 43 percent, flows to developing economies down 2 percent, Mexico up 16 percent to US\$44 billion with reinvested earnings a significant share, India up 73 percent, US greenfield announcements of about US\$360 billion led by semiconductors and data centers, and manufacturing projects in middle-income economies down sharply. The wiki uses it as its main official record of where new capacity went and who owns it.
