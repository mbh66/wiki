Cai, F., & Du, Y. (2011). "Wage Increases, Wage Convergence, and the Lewis Turning Point in China." *China Economic Review*, 22(4), 601–610.

Cai and Du report rising wages for migrant and unskilled workers in China and the convergence of wages across sectors and regions, which they read as evidence that China reached its Lewis turning point. The wiki cites the paper as independent corroboration of Zhang, Yang and Wang, from economists at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences.
