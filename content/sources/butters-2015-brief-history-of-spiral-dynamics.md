Butters, A. M. (2015). A brief history of Spiral Dynamics. *Approaching Religion* 5(2): 67-78.

Albion Butters traces Spiral Dynamics to Clare Graves's eight-level system of 1974, with six levels of subsistence in a first tier and two levels of being in a second [IC]. He reports criticism that Graves's data collection would be controversial today, that the model is hierarchical and can be used to rank people, and that it imposes fixed categories on moving processes [IC]. He also records the disputes among Don Beck, Chris Cowan and Ken Wilber over the model and its use [IC]. The wiki takes the article as a qualifier of the Spiral Dynamics leg of C4: the tier distinction has a documented origin, and its claim that a first tier resists a second remains contested.
