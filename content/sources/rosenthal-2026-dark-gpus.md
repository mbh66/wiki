Rosenthal, D. (2026, 15 October). Dark GPUs. *DSHR's Blog*. https://blog.dshr.org/2026/10/dark-gpus.html

Rosenthal tests the claim that an AI crash would leave useful surplus, as the telecom crash left dark fibre. He estimates that about 40 per cent of an AI data centre's cost is buildings, power connections and cooling, and about 60 per cent is racks of accelerators that depreciate faster than companies' accounts assume. Fibre's surplus was long-lived and cheap; an idle data centre is neither. The wiki treats the post as the strongest published criticism of the analogy behind the term [[glossary/a-e/dark-ai|dark AI]], and the figures are graded as a commentator's estimate.
