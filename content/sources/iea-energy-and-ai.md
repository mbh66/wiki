International Energy Agency (2025). *Energy and AI*. Paris: IEA.

The IEA estimates that data centers used about 415 terawatt hours of electricity in 2024, about 1.5 percent of global consumption, with the United States accounting for 45 percent, China 25 percent and Europe 15 percent, and projects demand in its Base Case to reach about 945 TWh by 2030, with AI the largest driver. The wiki uses the report to measure the energy cost of the Data Center, the infrastructure the Market form owns, and the energy the Emergent form draws through infrastructure it does not govern.
