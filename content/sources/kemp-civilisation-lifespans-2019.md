Kemp, L. (2019). "Are we on the road to civilisation collapse?" *BBC Future*, 19 February 2019.

Kemp, of the Cambridge Centre for the Study of Existential Risk, surveys collapse across 83 civilizations spanning 4,000 years and reports an average lifespan of 336 years, with very wide variation. He develops the argument in *[[sources/kemp-goliaths-curse|Goliath's Curse]]* (2025). The wiki uses the figure in place of unsourced cycle lengths, as one of three quantitative findings on duration.
