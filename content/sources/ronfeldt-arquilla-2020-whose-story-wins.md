Ronfeldt, D. and Arquilla, J. (2020). *Whose Story Wins: Rise of the Noosphere, Noopolitik, and Information-Age Statecraft*. RAND Corporation, PE-A237-1, 27 July 2020. 116 pp. DOI: [10.7249/PEA237-1](https://doi.org/10.7249/PEA237-1). [Read online](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PEA237-1.html).

David Ronfeldt and John Arquilla describe a noosphere circling the globe [TBV]. They argue that statecraft is moving towards noopolitik, which works through ideas, values and narratives, and they tie the noosphere's future to global commons [TBV]. They also document its dark uses, such as weaponised narratives [TBV].

David Ronfeldt's 2026 paper, [[sources/ronfeldt-ballooning-noosphere|Our Planet's Ballooning Noosphere]], is said to cite Michael Haupt's work.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
