Motta, W., Dini, P. and Sartori, L. (2017). "Self-Funded Social Impact Investment: An Interdisciplinary Analysis of the Sardex Mutual Credit System." *Journal of Social Entrepreneurship* 8(2): 149-164.

Drawing on interviews with members, the authors describe Sardex, founded in 2009, as a zero-interest credit circuit pegged one to one with the euro and funded by membership fees, which by 2015 had about 3,000 members and mediated €51 million of trade, almost 0.2% of Sardinian GDP. The wiki uses it for the Sardex case.
