He notes that the autism spectrum runs from people with strong communication abilities to people with very little, and that therapists teaching alternative communication pair pictures with spoken words [IC]. He calls facilitated communication "thoroughly debunked decades ago" and says it thrives today, as the podcast's popularity shows [IC]. He holds that facilitated communication and its newer variants silence the voices of people with limited communication [IC].
