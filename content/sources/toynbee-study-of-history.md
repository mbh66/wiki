Toynbee, A. J. (1934 to 1961). *A Study of History*, 12 vols. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

Civilizations grow by responding creatively to challenges and break down when a creative minority becomes a dominant minority; disintegration passes through a time of troubles and a universal state. Quigley's universal empire echoes it. The wiki lists it in the Cycle of Civilizations lineage.
