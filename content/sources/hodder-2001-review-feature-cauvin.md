Hodder, I., Rollefson, G. O. and Bar-Yosef, O., with a response by Watkins, T. (2001). Review Feature on *The Birth of the Gods and the Origins of Agriculture*, by Jacques Cauvin. Cambridge Archaeological Journal 11(1): 105-121. [Open copy, Harvard DASH](https://dash.lib.harvard.edu/handle/1/12220716).

Three archaeologists of the Near Eastern Neolithic review [[sources/cauvin-2000-birth-of-the-gods|Cauvin's book]] [TBV]. They accept that a symbolic change took place in the early Neolithic, and dispute his causal sequence from new symbols to domestication [TBV]. Ian Hodder grants that cultural and symbolic factors matter and holds that Cauvin gives them too much weight [TBV]. Trevor Watkins, the translator, replies [TBV].
