Arquilla, J. and Ronfeldt, D. (1999). *The Emergence of Noopolitik: Toward an American Information Strategy*. RAND Corporation, Santa Monica, MR-1033-OSD. 102 pp. DOI: [10.7249/MR1033](https://doi.org/10.7249/MR1033). [Read online](https://www.rand.org/pubs/monograph_reports/MR1033.html).

John Arquilla and David Ronfeldt propose noopolitik, a statecraft for the information age that relies mainly on soft power and stresses the sharing of ideas and values worldwide [IC]. They borrow the word noosphere for a globe-spanning realm of the mind in which that statecraft works [IC]. Ronfeldt and Arquilla's [[sources/ronfeldt-arquilla-2020-whose-story-wins|Whose Story Wins]] (2020) builds on the report. This entry rests on RAND's abstract and summaries; the chapters were not read [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in strategic studies, with evidence type E4 and claim code C3. It takes a position on one step, so it stays in the library outside the grid.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T8; it qualifies T9.
