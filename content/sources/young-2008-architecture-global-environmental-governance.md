Young, O. R. (2008). "The Architecture of Global Environmental Governance: Bringing Science to Bear on Policy." *Global Environmental Politics* 8(1): 14-32. https://doi.org/10.1162/glep.2008.8.1.14

Young argues that reforming international environmental organizations matters less than agreeing on the right structure of rights, rules and decision-making procedures, and that such arrangements must be "well-matched to the defining features of the problems they address." He sorts environmental problems into kinds, such as the depletion of living resources, reliance on free environmental services and externalities, each calling for its own incentive mechanisms. The wiki takes from it the statement of institutional fit that Cox (2012) quotes and formalizes.
