Adaba, K. A., and Boio, D. (2024, 31 October). *Across Africa, public trust in key institutions and leaders is weakening*. Afrobarometer Dispatch No. 891.

The dispatch compares two survey periods in 30 countries and finds trust in key institutions and leaders declining in most of them. Trust in the president fell by 34 percentage points in South Africa, the fourth-largest fall after Botswana, Eswatini and Sudan. The wiki uses the South African figure in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as a household reading that GDP does not record, set beside a decade of flat income per person.
