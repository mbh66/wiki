Chmiel, Z. and Bhattacharyya, S.C. (2015). "Analysis of off-grid electricity system at Isle of Eigg (Scotland): lessons for developing countries." *Renewable Energy* 81.

The authors model the Eigg system after its first years of operation and report a renewable fraction of 0.89 in one configuration, noting that the island had received little academic attention. They draw lessons for off-grid systems in developing countries. The wiki takes from it independent confirmation of how the community trust's grid performs.
