Tetakawi. (2026). "Manufacturing Wages: Mexico vs China." Tetakawi Insights.

A comparison of fully loaded manufacturing labor costs by a firm that provides shelter and manufacturing services in Mexico, putting China at about US\$6.50 to US\$6.69 an hour in 2024 and Mexico at about US\$4.90, based on national statistics. The wiki uses the figures to show the labor deviation moving from China to the next labor reserve, and grades them [MS] because the publisher sells relocation to Mexico.
