National Telecommunications and Information Administration (2024, 30 July). *Dual-Use Foundation Models with Widely Available Model Weights*. US Department of Commerce. https://www.ntia.gov/issues/artificial-intelligence/open-model-weights-report

Prepared under a 2023 executive order, the report weighs the marginal risks and benefits of foundation models whose weights anyone can download, measured against closed models and other technologies. It concludes that the government should build the capacity to monitor those risks and should not restrict the availability of open weights at present. It also lists benefits that matter for small users: more actors can build on the models, and users can run them without sharing data with a third party. The wiki cites it for its working definition of open-weight models.
