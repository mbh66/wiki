Cauvin, J. (2000). *The Birth of the Gods and the Origins of Agriculture*. Translated by Trevor Watkins. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, New Studies in Archaeology. ISBN 0-521-65135-2. First published in French in 1994 as *Naissance des divinités, naissance de l'agriculture*.

Jacques Cauvin argues that a Revolution of Symbols came before farming in the Near East [IC]. New religious ideas and divinities restructured mentality, and in his account this change helped bring the first farming villages about [IC].

A review feature in the Cambridge Archaeological Journal (2001) by Ian Hodder, Gary Rollefson and Ofer Bar-Yosef accepts a symbolic change and disputes the causal link to domestication; see [[sources/hodder-2001-review-feature-cauvin|the review feature]] [IC]. On step T7, run 7b codes the book as qualifying part (a): it speaks to the Neolithic, and the change it describes is religious and symbolic.
