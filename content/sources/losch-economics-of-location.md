Lösch, A. (1954). *The Economics of Location*. Trans. W. H. Woglom and W. F. Stolper. New Haven: Yale University Press. (Original work published 1940 as *Die räumliche Ordnung der Wirtschaft*.)

Lösch replaced Weber's search for least cost with the search for maximum profit over a market area, and derived hexagonal market areas and networks of central places from it. The wiki cites Lösch for the standard critique of Weber, whose model fixes demand at points and so has no market area, and for the reminder that where a plant sits depends on where its buyers are as well as where its costs are lowest.
