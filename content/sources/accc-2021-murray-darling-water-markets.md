Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (2021). *Murray-Darling Basin Water Markets Inquiry: Final Report*, 26 March 2021. https://www.accc.gov.au/publications/murray-darling-basin-water-markets-inquiry-final-report

The inquiry puts average annual trade above A\$1.8 billion and finds poor market information, manipulation and insider trading inadequately regulated, few conduct rules, no single overseeing agency, and trade rules that cause channel congestion with third-party and environmental impacts. It finds no evidence that investors used market power to raise prices in the Southern Connected Basin. The wiki uses it as an official record of a mature market in water rights.
