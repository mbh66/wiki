Corballis, M. C. (2014). Left brain, right brain: facts and fantasies. *PLoS Biology* 12(1): e1001767.

The neuropsychologist Michael Corballis reviews the evidence on hemispheric asymmetry. He finds asymmetries common across animals and likely to have evolved gradually alongside language and tool use, and he treats sharp popular contrasts between a left and a right brain as largely exaggerated [IC]. He names Iain McGilchrist's *The Master and His Emissary* as a recent example, noting that it makes the right hemisphere the master, and argues that its account of Western culture reaches well past the neurological facts [IC]. The wiki takes the review as the plainest dissent from the McGilchrist leg of step C4.
