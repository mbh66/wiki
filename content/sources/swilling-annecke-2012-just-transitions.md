Swilling, M. and Annecke, E. (2012). *Just Transitions: Explorations of Sustainability in an Unfair World*. Cape Town: UCT Press; Tokyo: United Nations University Press.

A reading of sustainability transitions from South Africa that puts inequality at the centre. Chapter 10, Pioneering Liveable Urbanism: Reflections on an Invisible Way, describes Lynedoch EcoVillage and the Sustainability Institute near Stellenbosch, which the authors co-founded in 1999. The chapter is an account by the founders of their own work. The wiki cites the book as the main South African text in its brief on protective space, graded as a practitioner account.
