International Cooperative Alliance. (2025, 3 November). *World Cooperative Monitor 2025*. Press release.

The annual ranking of the world's largest cooperatives reports that the 300 largest had a combined turnover of US\$2.79 trillion in 2023; the ICA counts more than a billion members in about three million cooperatives. The ICA is the sector's own federation. The wiki uses the figures as the largest measured scale of production owned by its members, and notes that most of it is in insurance, agriculture and retail and predates 2008.
