Von Muralt, A. (1934). "The Woergl Experiment with Depreciating Money." *Annals of Public and Cooperative Economics* 10(1): 48-57. Translated by G. Spiller from *Ständisches Leben* no. 6 (1933).

Von Muralt, a Swiss writer who visited Wörgl, records that 32,000 schillings of certificates were printed and 12,000 issued against an equal deposit, that about a third were kept by collectors, that prices did not rise, and that relief works worth about 100,000 schillings were built. He could not confirm the mayor's claims on tax arrears at the provincial offices, and he records the state bank's opposition and the suspension of issue in May 1933. The wiki uses it to correct the figures most often repeated about Wörgl.
