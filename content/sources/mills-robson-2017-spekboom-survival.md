Mills, A.J. and Robson, A. (2017). "Survivorship of spekboom (*Portulacaria afra*) planted within the Subtropical Thicket Restoration Programme." *South African Journal of Science* 113(1-2).

About 21.5 million spekboom cuttings were planted from 2004 to 2016 in Addo Elephant National Park, Great Fish River Nature Reserve and Baviaanskloof Nature Reserve. In 64 large-scale plots sampled in 2015 (in Addo and Great Fish River), survival ranged from 0 to 93% with a mean of 28%. The wiki takes from it an independent check on the restoration method used in the Baviaanskloof, measured outside Living Lands' sites.
