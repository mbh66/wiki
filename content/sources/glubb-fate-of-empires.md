Glubb, J. B. (1978). *The Fate of Empires and Search for Survival*. Edinburgh: William Blackwood & Sons. The two essays first appeared in *Blackwood's Magazine*.

A retired British general's essay naming six ages: Pioneers, Conquests, Commerce, Affluence, Intellect and Decadence, over an average of about ten generations. The essay was not peer reviewed, rests on eleven empires, and its author called his dates largely arbitrary. The wiki uses Glubb's age names as descriptions in the Cycle of Civilizations and does not treat his 250-year span as a finding.
