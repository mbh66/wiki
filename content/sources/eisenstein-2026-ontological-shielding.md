Eisenstein, C. (2026, 8 February). Ontological shielding in new-paradigm research. Substack. https://charleseisenstein.substack.com/p/ontological-shielding-in-new-paradigm

Eisenstein argues that some phenomena are not yet settled in consensus reality, and that sceptical scrutiny dilutes them. He proposes layers of shielding, from none to complete, so that research can be designed with chosen degrees of exposure, and likens the most shielded experiment to a baby in an incubator. The W2DF has kept the social reading of shielding for young initiatives and dropped any hidden steering. The wiki maps the essay beside transitions research without testing its claim. See [[people/theorists/eisenstein-charles|Charles Eisenstein]].
