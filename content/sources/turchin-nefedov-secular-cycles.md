Turchin, P., & Nefedov, S. A. (2009). *Secular Cycles*. Princeton: Princeton University Press.

The book sets out structural-demographic theory through case studies of England, France, Rome and Russia: secular cycles of expansion, stagflation, general crisis and depression, driven by population pressure, elite overproduction and state fiscal strain. It is the best-evidenced cycle model the wiki cites, and the source of the elite-overproduction mechanism in the late phases of the Cycle of Globalization.
