Shawn Wilson (2008). *Research Is Ceremony: Indigenous Research Methods*. Fernwood Publishing, Black Point, Nova Scotia [IC]. [Library of Congress contents](https://catdir.loc.gov/catdir/toc/fy1002/2008431436.html).

Wilson, an Opaskwayak Cree scholar, describes a research paradigm that Indigenous scholars in Canada and Australia share [IC]. The catalogue summary states: "Relationships don't just shape Indigenous reality, they are our reality." [IC]. He holds that Indigenous ontology and epistemology rest on relationality, and that research is a ceremony of accountability to those relations [IC]. He addresses the book partly to his own children, a choice reviewers link to its method [TBV].

A review in *The Qualitative Report* presents it as a guide for qualitative researchers [TBV]. The book was not read for this record; the coding rests on the Stanford and Library of Congress records and on reviews [TBV].

The wiki takes from it an Indigenous scholar's own account of a continuing way of knowing, in his own terms [TBV].
