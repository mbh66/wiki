Schwartz, M. (2013). A control group study of incubators' impact to promote firm survival. *Journal of Technology Transfer* 38(3), 302-331.

One of the few studies of protection with a counterfactual. Schwartz matches 371 firms that graduated from five German incubators with 371 comparable firms that were never incubated and follows both for ten years. At none of the five sites did incubated firms survive longer, and at three they survived significantly less well. The wiki treats this as the strongest evidence that shelter alone does not secure survival after exposure.
