Sunass (2022). "Se afianza sostenibilidad del servicio de agua potable en Arequipa con funcionamiento de dos microrrepresas." 14 October 2022.

The regulator reports the inauguration of two microdams in San Juan de Tarucani under Sedapar's MERESE program for ecosystem services, financed by Arequipa's drinking-water users through a tariff Sunass approved, with S/135,000 disbursed after the first year of work. Sedapar, Sernanp and Descosur carried out the works, and Sunass monitors them. The wiki cites it as the official record of how the qochas are paid for.
