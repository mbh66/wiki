Bolhuis, M. A., Chen, J., & Kett, B. (2023, June). "The Costs of Geoeconomic Fragmentation." *Finance & Development*. Washington, DC: International Monetary Fund.

IMF economists model a world split into two trading blocs and estimate the loss at about 2.3 percent of world GDP, larger for lower-income countries. The wiki cites the article with the IMF staff note as the quantified case that fragmentation into blocs, the pattern the evidence shows since 2018, has costs that a place-based reading of production must account for.
