World Bank. (2020). *World Development Report 2020: Trading for Development in the Age of Global Value Chains*. Washington, DC: World Bank.

The World Bank's survey of global value chains finds that trade through them grew fastest in the long 1990s, reached about half of world trade around 2008 and has stalled since, and that participation in them helped many countries grow. It also records China building its own supply chains and relying less on imported inputs. The wiki takes the plateau after 2008 as a documented fact, and the report's development case as part of the evidence its framing must face.
