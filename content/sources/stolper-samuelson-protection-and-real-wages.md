Stolper, W. F., & Samuelson, P. A. (1941). "Protection and Real Wages." *Review of Economic Studies*, 9(1), 58–73.

The Stolper-Samuelson theorem: within the Heckscher-Ohlin model, trade raises the real return to a country's abundant factor and lowers the real return to its scarce one. In a capital-rich country opening to labor-abundant partners, wages of less-skilled workers fall. The wiki cites the theorem as the standard account of why the gains of the steep part of the curve did not spread evenly inside rich economies.
