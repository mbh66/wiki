Smith, A., Fressoli, M., Abrol, D., Arond, E. and Ely, A. (2017). *Grassroots Innovation Movements*. London: Routledge. Open access.

A historical comparison of six movements: Socially Useful Production in the UK, Appropriate Technology in South America, the People's Science Movements and the Honey Bee Network in India, the Social Technology Network in Brazil, and hackerspaces and makerspaces. The authors show movements opening space for alternatives and contesting mainstream framings of innovation, and being absorbed or sidelined by more powerful actors. The wiki takes from it that empowerment on the regime's terms can cost a movement its purpose.
