Baldwin, R. (2019). *The Globotics Upheaval: Globalization, Robotics, and the Future of Work*. New York: Oxford University Press.

Baldwin's account of a third unbundling, in which telepresence and remote work separate the worker from the workplace, so that service tasks can be done across borders while automation reshapes the factory. The wiki tests his prediction against services trade since 2008, which grew faster than goods trade and reached its highest share of world trade since 2005 in 2025.
