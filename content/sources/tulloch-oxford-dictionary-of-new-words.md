Tulloch, S. (comp.) (1991). *The Oxford Dictionary of New Words: A Popular Guide to Words in the News*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

The dictionary's entry for glocal and glocalization, on page 134, records that the word was "formed by telescoping global and local to make a blend," and that it is modeled on the Japanese *dochakuka*, from *dochaku*, "living on one's own land": originally the agricultural principle of adapting farming technique to local conditions, later adopted in Japanese business. Robertson quotes the entry in his 1995 chapter. The wiki uses it to settle the word's origin.
