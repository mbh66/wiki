New York State Department of Health (2022). *Revised 2017 New York City Filtration Avoidance Determination*. Albany: NYSDOH, December 2022.

The determination sets the conditions under which New York City may keep drawing unfiltered water from its upstate watersheds. The 2022 revision followed a public comment period that was extended at the request of the Coalition of Watershed Towns, and it carries forward the city's obligations under the Watershed Agricultural Program, including a contract with the Watershed Agricultural Council. The wiki cites it as the official record that the upstream farm program remains a condition of the city's water supply.
