McKinsey Global Institute. (2026, 19 March). "Geopolitics and the Geometry of Global Trade: 2026 Update."

MGI's update finds that "trade in 2025 did not retrench, despite dire predictions," with AI hardware shipments up almost 40 percent and accounting for about a third of trade growth, trade moving further toward geopolitically aligned partners, goods traveling longer distances, and limited evidence of firms bringing production home or to near neighbors.
