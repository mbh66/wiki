Whaites, A. (2017). *The Beginner's Guide to Political Economy Analysis (PEA)*. London: National School of Government International. Crown copyright.

The guide is a thirteen-page entry point to political economy analysis for practitioners without formal training. It groups the issues a PEA examines into four elements (structural and contextual factors, bargaining processes, stakeholders, and incentives and ideas), offers a simple "one-hour conversation" approach adapted from the Effective States and Inclusive Development research center, and argues that analysis done once at the design stage tends to be ignored, so it should become a continuous part of the work in the manner of the Thinking and Working Politically community of practice. The wiki takes from it the four elements and the conversation method for place-level analysis by a [[glossary/a-e/biohub|BioHub]] cohort, and adds a reading of bargaining through the [[frameworks/time-framework|TIME]] forms.
