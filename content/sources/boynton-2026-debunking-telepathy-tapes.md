Boynton worked as a facilitator and took part in controlled authorship testing in 1993; she now writes publicly in her own name about facilitated communication [IC]. She uses FC for every method in which a facilitator supports a nonspeaker's letter selection, the Rapid Prompting Method included [IC]. From images and scenes in the podcast filed as [[sources/dickens-2024-telepathy-tapes|Dickens (2024)]], she points to touch, positioning and spoken cues during letter selection [IC]. She argues that the experiments shown in season one, episodes 1 and 2, are open to cueing [IC].
