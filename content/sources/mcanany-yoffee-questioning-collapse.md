McAnany, P. A., & Yoffee, N. (eds.) (2010). *Questioning Collapse: Human Resilience, Ecological Vulnerability, and the Aftermath of Empire*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

Archaeologists and anthropologists respond to popular collapse narratives, showing for cases such as the Maya and Easter Island that populations and cultures persisted through political breakdown. The wiki cites it in support of the overlap of decay and emergence in the last phase of a cycle, and as a caveat on collapse language.
