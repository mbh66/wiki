Latour, B. (1987). *Science in Action: How to Follow Scientists and Engineers through Society*. Harvard University Press, Cambridge, MA.

Bruno Latour follows science as it is made [IC]. He argues that its reach is built from local practice through networks: inscriptions made mobile, stable and combinable, and gathered at centres of calculation [IC]. He holds that the difference between scientific and other knowledge lies in the length of these networks, with no separate mode of thought behind it [TBV].

On step T7, run 6b codes the book as accepting a historical build-up of knowledge made to travel while rejecting a great divide in thought, and flags it as a judgement call.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T7.
