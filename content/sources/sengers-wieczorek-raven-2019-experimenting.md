Sengers, F., Wieczorek, A.J. and Raven, R. (2019). Experimenting for sustainability transitions: a systematic literature review. *Technological Forecasting and Social Change* 145, 153-164.

The authors map how the transitions field uses the idea of an experiment, and separate niche experiments, bounded socio-technical experiments, transition experiments, sustainability experiments and grassroots experiments. The review maps concepts and does not pool outcome data. The wiki uses it to keep experiments, incubation and protection apart when it weighs their evidence.
