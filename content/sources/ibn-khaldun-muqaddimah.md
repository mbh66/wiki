Ibn Khaldun (1377). *The Muqaddimah: An Introduction to History*. Trans. Franz Rosenthal. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1958.

Dynasties rise on *asabiyyah*, the solidarity of a group, and decline over about three to four generations as wealth and luxury erode it. The wiki reads it as the classical source closest to the cohesion-to-decay arc in Michael's seven-phase synthesis.
