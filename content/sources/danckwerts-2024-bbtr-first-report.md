Danckwerts, B. (2024). *First Six-Month Report on the Campaign to Bring back the Rains to Southern Africa*. Choma, Zambia, October 2024.

Danckwerts sets out the campaign's case: the Zambezi's flow peaked in the 1960s and 1970s, the decline does not track El Niño or sunspot cycles on his reading, and falling tree cover is the likelier cause. He widens the aim from trees to soils and groundwater, asks readers to record which species leaf before the rains, maps NGO coverage in Zambia, and proposes a dedicated mentor for every chiefdom. The wiki cites it for the campaign's founding argument and for the mentor idea.
