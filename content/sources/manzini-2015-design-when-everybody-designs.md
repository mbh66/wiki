Manzini, E. (2015). *Design, When Everybody Designs: An Introduction to Design for Social Innovation*. Translated by Rachel Coad. MIT Press, Cambridge, MA, Design Thinking, Design Theory series.

Ezio Manzini describes social innovation as people redesigning how they live and work, with design experts in support [TBV]. He sets out a scenario of small, local, open and connected initiatives, and names the culture behind it cosmopolitan localism, which joins the local and the global [TBV]. He warns against a closed, insular localism [TBV].

Liodaki et al. (2025) name the book as the origin of the concept. Gideon Kossoff (2019) credits Wolfgang Sachs with the term in the 1990s; Sachs's own text has not been checked [TBV]. On step T9, run 7b codes the book as one of two works independent of the network that affirm part (a). Much of it is a design scenario, and the reading rests on one review.
