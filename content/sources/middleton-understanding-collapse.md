Middleton, G. D. (2017). *Understanding Collapse: Ancient History and Modern Myths*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

A survey of the archaeology of collapse which separates political fragmentation from the death of a society and questions explanations by moral decline. The wiki cites it among the critiques of cyclical theories on the Cycle of Civilizations page.
