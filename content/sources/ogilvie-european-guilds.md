Ogilvie, S. (2019). *The European Guilds: An Economic Analysis*. Princeton: Princeton University Press.

Ogilvie surveys guilds across Europe from 1000 to 1880 and tests the claims made for them: that they guaranteed quality, trained skill and spread innovation. She finds that guild privileges raised prices, excluded women and outsiders from trades, and that industry grew where guilds decayed. Defenders of guilds, S. R. Epstein among them, dispute her reading, and the debate is open. The wiki uses the book for the Institutional form of production and the costs it pushed onto consumers and excluded workers.
