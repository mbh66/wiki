African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights (2025). *African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights v. Republic of Kenya*. Application No. 006/2012. Judgment on compliance, 4 December 2025. Arusha. Summary: https://www.african-court.org/wpafc/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/006-2012-SW-Judgment-Summary-ACHPR-v-KENYA-Compliance-1-December-2025.pdf

In its first ruling on compliance in the case, the Court found that Kenya had complied only partly with the 2017 merits judgment and had paid none of the reparations ordered in 2022 (KES 57,850,000 for material loss and KES 100,000,000 for moral prejudice). Identification, demarcation and titling of Ogiek land were incomplete, consultations on concessions and leases had not begun, and the community development fund had not been set up. The Court ordered immediate implementation. The wiki cites it as the official record of what happened after a community won recognition of its claim to a water tower in court.
