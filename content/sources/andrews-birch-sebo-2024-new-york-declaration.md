Kristin Andrews, Jonathan Birch, Jeff Sebo et al. (2024). *The New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness*. New York University (19 April 2024).

Launched at New York University on 19 April 2024, the declaration was drafted by Kristin Andrews, Jonathan Birch and Jeff Sebo and signed by researchers in animal cognition, neuroscience and philosophy [IC]. It finds strong scientific support for conscious experience in mammals and birds, and a realistic possibility of it in all vertebrates and many invertebrates, at minimum cephalopod molluscs, decapod crustaceans and insects; it holds that a realistic possibility is enough to warrant attention to welfare [IC]. Accounts of the first signatory count differ, and later counts run into the hundreds [TBV]. The wiki takes it as the broadest statement of current scientific opinion on step A1.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as an evidence work in comparative cognition, with evidence type E3 and claim codes A1.


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T3; it is silent on T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|claim grid]] codes its positions on the claim chain: it affirms A1.
