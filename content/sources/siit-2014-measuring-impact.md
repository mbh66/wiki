Impact Measurement Working Group. (2014). *Measuring Impact: Subject Paper of the Impact Measurement Working Group*. Social Impact Investment Taskforce, established under the UK's presidency of the G8. September 2014. Now hosted by the Global Steering Group for Impact Investment.

The paper gives impact investors seven guidelines for measurement (set goals; develop a framework and select metrics; collect and store data; validate data; analyze data; report data; make data-driven management decisions), grouped under the phases Plan, Do, Assess and Review. It includes cases of investors applying them, names three trends expected to shape the field, and describes an ideal of material, reliable, comparable, additional and universal impact data supported by a shared convention. The wiki takes from it the measurement sequence a [[glossary/a-e/biohub|BioHub]] can show funders, and reads its investor-centered framing against [[glossary/t-z/transvestment|transvestment]].
