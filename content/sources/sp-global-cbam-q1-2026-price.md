S&P Global Commodity Insights. (2026, 7 April). "Brussels confirms first CBAM certificate price for Q1 2026 at EUR 75.36/mtCO2e."

Reports the European Commission's first quarterly price for certificates under the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, which entered its definitive phase on 1 January 2026 for iron and steel, cement, aluminum, fertilizers, electricity and hydrogen. The wiki reads CBAM as a return of part of the sink cost that globalization left unpriced to the delivered price of heavy goods, arriving through policy.
