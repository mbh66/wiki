Wehling, P. and Garthwaite, B. (2015). *Designing Warehouse Receipt Legislation: Regulatory Options and Recent Trends*. FAO Legal Office, Rome.

The study sets out the legal choices behind warehouse receipt systems: licensing, inspection, negotiability and creditor rights. It records Ethiopia's 2003 warehouse receipt law and its commodity exchange's electronic receipts, and Tanzania's path from a 1998 coffee pilot and a 2002 cereals pilot to the 2005 Warehouse Receipts Act and its public-private licensing board. The wiki uses it for the legal structure behind those two cases.
