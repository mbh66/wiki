South African San Council (2017). *San Code of Research Ethics*. South African San Council (launched Cape Town March 2017).

Launched in Cape Town in March 2017, the code was developed by the South African San Council with San communities and partners, and is described as the first research ethics code from an Indigenous people in Africa [IC]. It asks researchers to commit to four values (respect, honesty, justice and fairness, and care) and sets a process through which San communities approve research and its outputs [IC]. It is listed among the codes affiliated with the Global Code of Conduct for Equitable Research Partnerships [IC]. The wiki follows it in how it reports San knowledge: through San institutions and published San testimony, never translated into the network's structure names.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in Indigenous knowledge, with evidence type E5 and claim codes C3.
