Kleppmann, M., Wiggins, A., van Hardenberg, P. and McGranaghan, M. (2019). Local-first software: you own your data, in spite of the cloud. *Proceedings of the 2019 ACM SIGPLAN International Symposium on New Ideas, New Paradigms, and Reflections on Programming and Software (Onward! 2019)*: 154-178. https://doi.org/10.1145/3359591.3359737

Written by researchers at Ink & Switch, the essay sets cloud apps, where every access to data runs through the provider's server, against software that treats the copy on the user's own device as the main one. It proposes seven ideals, among them speed, working offline, collaboration, longevity and user control, and points to conflict-free replicated data types as the technique that makes them possible. The wiki draws on it for communities with slow or intermittent connections and for keeping a community's records on devices the community holds.
