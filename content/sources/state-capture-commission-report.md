Judicial Commission of Inquiry into Allegations of State Capture (2022). *Report of the Judicial Commission of Inquiry into Allegations of State Capture, Corruption and Fraud in the Public Sector including Organs of State*. Pretoria. Summary of findings via the Presidency.

The commission found that state capture took place "on an extensive scale." The summary published by the Presidency puts state spending tainted by state capture at about R57 billion, more than 97 percent of it at Transnet and Eskom. The wiki cites the report in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as the official record of governance failure in the 2009 to 2018 period, and uses the Commission's figure in preference to wider estimates made in testimony and in the media. The wiki page names offices and leaves out individuals.
