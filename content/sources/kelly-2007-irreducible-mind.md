In brief: Case material shows mind is not produced by the brain alone [TBV].

How its field receives it: Outside mainstream psychology [TBV].

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in psychology, with evidence type E4 and claim codes H, H2.


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it affirms T1; it affirms T2; it affirms T3; it is silent on T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.
