Haile, M. and Gebregziabher, D. (2020). "Successful landscape restoration in Abreha We Atsbeha watershed, Tigray, Ethiopia." *ETFRN News* 60: 43-49. Wageningen: Tropenbos International.

The article reports that 4,644 hectares (69%) of the watershed were restored with exclosures, that 55 check dams were built, and that about 650 hand-dug wells now irrigate fields. Citing Gebregziabher et al. (2016), it states that groundwater was once found at about 50 meters and is now about 5 meters below the surface. It is a practitioner synthesis and draws its figures from other studies. The wiki takes from it the watershed's physical record and the source chain behind the groundwater figure.
