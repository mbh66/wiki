Quigley, C. (1979). *The Evolution of Civilizations: An Introduction to Historical Analysis*. 2nd ed. Indianapolis: Liberty Fund. First published New York: Macmillan, 1961.

Quigley proposes seven stages: mixture, gestation, expansion, age of conflict, universal empire, decay and invasion. Expansion depends on an organization that accumulates surplus and invests it; when that organization becomes an institution serving its own members, expansion slows and conflict follows. Reform can restart expansion. The wiki takes Quigley's sequence as the phase structure of the Cycle of Globalization, and notes that Quigley did not apply it to a global civilization.
