Ervin Laszlo (2004). *Science and the Akashic Field: An Integral Theory of Everything*. Inner Traditions, Rochester, Vermont; updated second edition 2007.

Ervin Laszlo argues that modern physics has rediscovered the Akashic field of perennial philosophy: a field of information in the quantum vacuum that conserves the record of everything that happens and connects all things, from atoms to minds [IC]. On this basis he sets out an integral theory of everything and reads evolution in nature and in mind as a movement toward greater coherence [TBV]. The book sits outside mainstream physics; library catalogues class it under Akashic records and parapsychology [IC]. Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* draws on Laszlo for its claim that complexity has a direction. The wiki takes it as a statement of that claim, and leaves aside the book's arguments for non-local mind, which the grid treats as outside its scope.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a canon work in systems philosophy, with evidence type E4 and claim codes B3, H.


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T3.
