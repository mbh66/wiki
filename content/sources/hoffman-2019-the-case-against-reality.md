Donald D. Hoffman (2019). *The Case Against Reality: Why Evolution Hid the Truth from Our Eyes*. W. W. Norton, New York.

Donald Hoffman argues that evolution selects perception for fitness to reproduce and gives no access to the truth, so space, time and objects are an interface (his interface theory of perception) [IC]. His ultimate ontology, conscious realism, holds that the objective world consists of conscious agents and their experiences [IC]. Reviewers have found the argument provocative and, in *Publishers Weekly*'s reading, given too little room to be fully developed [IC]. Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* cites Hoffman's interface theory for its claim that the brain filters consciousness. The wiki takes it as one of the main contemporary arguments that consciousness is fundamental.
