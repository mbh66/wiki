Sharpe, B. (2013). *Three Horizons: The Patterning of Hope*. Axminster: Triarchy Press. Second edition 2020.

Sharpe describes three horizons present at once: the dominant pattern in decline, the emerging pattern, and the transitional zone where the two contend. He calls emerging practices pockets of the future in the present, with case studies in community development, education and health. The book is written from facilitation practice. It is the one framework in the transitions field that invites a reading against the network's [[concepts/major-evolutionary-transitions|Major Evolutionary Transitions]] frame, since it holds the declining and the emerging together; that reading is the wiki's own.
