Monod, J. (1970). *Le hasard et la nécessité: Essai sur la philosophie naturelle de la biologie moderne*. Éditions du Seuil, Paris. English: *Chance and Necessity: An Essay on the Natural Philosophy of Modern Biology*, translated by Austryn Wainhouse, Alfred A. Knopf, New York, 1971. The publishers are recorded from general knowledge and await a catalogue check [TBV].

Jacques Monod, a Nobel laureate in medicine, argues that science rests on a postulate of objectivity, which excludes any project or purpose in nature [IC]. Evolution, in his account, rests on chance mutation and the necessity of natural selection [IC]. He groups doctrines of a progressive purpose in nature, Teilhard's among them, as animisms [IC].
