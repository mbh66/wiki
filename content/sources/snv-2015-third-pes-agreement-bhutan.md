SNV (2015). "Third PES agreement in Bhutan." SNV news update.

SNV, which facilitated the agreement, reports that it was signed on 29 October 2015 for 2016 to 2020 between the Namey Nichu Watershed Management Group (29 households) and the drinking water users: five hotels and the Satsham Water Association. It states that the providers "shall be paid Nu.142,000 only upon compliance to the four agreed activities and after verification by the PES Verification Team." As the facilitator's account, it is cited on the wiki for the payment terms; the scheme's existence rests on the World Bank and IUCN records.
