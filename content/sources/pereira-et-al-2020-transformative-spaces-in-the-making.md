Pereira, L. and others (2020). Transformative spaces in the making: key lessons from nine cases in the Global South. *Sustainability Science* 15, 161-178.

Fifteen authors draw five lessons from nine transformative spaces they convened or facilitated, mainly in the Global South and including Southern African cases. The lessons concern how such spaces are designed, convened and opened to wider influence. Because the authors ran the spaces, the wiki grades the paper as an account of their own work. It is the strongest Southern evidence on convened protected spaces in [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
