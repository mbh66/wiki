Aglietta, M. (1979). *A Theory of Capitalist Regulation: The US Experience*. Trans. D. Fernbach. London: New Left Books. (Original work published 1976 as *Régulation et crises du capitalisme*.)

The founding work of the regulation school. Aglietta describes Fordism as a regime of accumulation joining mass production to mass consumption under a mode of regulation, and its crisis from the late 1960s. The wiki reports the periodization of Fordism and post-Fordism as a framework without adopting it, and notes that its account of crisis comes close to TIME's property that each form externalizes costs the next must organize.
