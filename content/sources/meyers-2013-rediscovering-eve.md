Carol Meyers (2013). *Rediscovering Eve: Ancient Israelite Women in Context*. Oxford University Press, New York. ISBN 9780199734627 [IC]. [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/rediscovering-eve-9780199734627).

The book follows her *Discovering Eve* (1988); one reviewer notes that she does not present it as a revision [TBV]. Meyers sets the Eve of Genesis 2-3 beside the ordinary woman of Iron Age Israel, read through the Hebrew text, archaeology and ethnography [IC]. In chapter 4 she traces how ideas absent from the Hebrew text, such as the Fall and Eve's temptation of Adam, became attached to Eve, and she faults translations that carry them [IC]. In chapter 5 she reads Genesis 3:16 against the farm and household life of the period, when men and women both faced hard labour and women faced many pregnancies [IC].

The H-Net review praises her joint command of the Bible and archaeology [TBV]. A 2015 review for the Council on Biblical Manhood and Womanhood calls her translation of 3:16 forced [IC]. No lawful preview was read, and the coding rests on the reviews [TBV].

The wiki takes from it a feminist and archaeological reading of Eden, in Meyers's own terms [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it dissents on T7.
