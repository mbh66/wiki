Badgley, G. and Cullenward, D. (2022). "Zombies on the Blockchain." CarbonPlan, 7 April 2022. https://carbonplan.org/research/toucan-crypto-offsets

The authors count over 21.6 million Verra credits moved on chain since October 2021 and find that about 28%, some 6.0 million tons of CO2-equivalent, came from "zombie" projects with no public retirement in the prior two years or with nearly all retirements made through Toucan. They also track the fall of KlimaDAO's KLIMA token from over US\$1,000 to near US\$20. The wiki uses it as the independent test of the carbon tokens' backing.
