Danny Whitty (2025). "On Telepathy Tapes". *Danny With Words*, 27 January 2025 [IC]. [Author's site](https://dannywithwords.com/2025/01/27/on-telepathy-tapes/).

Whitty describes himself as a nonspeaking autistic writer and advocate who communicates by spelling [IC]. He writes that he is angry about the podcast, and that the spellers he has spoken with share his feelings [IC]. He argues that the show adds to doubt about his method of communication and risks presenting his community as a spectacle [IC]. On the information claim he writes: "If a speller says they do, I trust them." [IC]. He says he is not among those who report telepathy, and he likens his work with a communication partner to a shared rhythm [IC].

This is his own account, testimony that cannot be checked further [TBV]. [[sources/des-roches-rosa-2026-telepathy-tapes-hot-mess|Shannon Des Roches Rosa]] quotes his objection in a 2026 article [TBV]. The post was read in full on his site [IC].

The wiki takes from it a nonspeaking adult's published view, in his own words [TBV].
