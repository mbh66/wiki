Kemp, R., Schot, J. and Hoogma, R. (1998). Regime shifts to sustainability through processes of niche formation: the approach of strategic niche management. *Technology Analysis and Strategic Management* 10(2), 175-198.

The founding text of strategic niche management. Kemp, Schot and Hoogma argue that governments and firms can create protected spaces for promising technologies, where users, producers and regulators learn together before the technology meets full market selection. Later reviews read these niches as temporary, with protection meant to fall away as the technology matures. The wiki takes from it the idea that protection can be designed on purpose, and the starting vocabulary of niche and regime used in [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
