Al-Marshoudi, A. S. and Sulong, J. (2024). "Water Market Auctioning Method in the Sultanate of Oman: Islamic Principles of Three Aflaj Case Study in Northern Oman." *Sustainable Water Resources Management* 10: 14. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40899-023-00971-7

From interviews with *wakils*, focus groups held at the auctions and the recorders' handwritten books, the authors document how three aflaj in northern Oman divide their flow into time units (*baddah* and *athar*) and four kinds of right, and how a full day of flow is set aside and rented to pay for maintenance: at weekly ascending auctions in Falaj Daris and Falaj al-Malki, where prices rise in summer and for daytime turns, and on annual contracts in Falaj al-Khatmeen. The wiki uses it as its main source on the aflaj as a market in water shares bounded by the community that owns the channel.
