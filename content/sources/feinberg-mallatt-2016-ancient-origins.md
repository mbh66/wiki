Todd E. Feinberg, Jon M. Mallatt (2016). *The Ancient Origins of Consciousness*. MIT Press.

Todd Feinberg, a neurologist and psychiatrist, and Jon Mallatt, an evolutionary biologist, set out criteria for sensory consciousness and trace them through the fossil and comparative record [IC]. They conclude that it arose in the Cambrian, more than 500 million years ago, in at least three lineages, and explain it as a natural product of neural hierarchies, a view they call neurobiological naturalism [IC]. The book is widely cited in comparative consciousness research [TBV]. The wiki takes it as core evidence for step A1, and as a naturalist voice on the hinge.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as an evidence work in neurobiology, with evidence type E3 and claim codes A1, B3, H.



The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it dissents on T1; it dissents on T3; it qualifies T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|claim grid]] codes its positions on the claim chain: it affirms A1; it qualifies B3; it dissents from H.
