Stallman, R. M. (1985). "The GNU Manifesto." *Dr. Dobb's Journal*, March 1985.

Stallman explains the GNU project, announced in 1983, as a refusal of proprietary software licenses: "Software sellers want to divide the users and conquer them." He asks programmers and firms to contribute to a free system that anyone may copy, study and change. The wiki uses the Manifesto as a founding text of peer production, and as evidence of what its founders said they were responding to: license restrictions and the sharing of software, with no reference to the costs of the industrial economy.
