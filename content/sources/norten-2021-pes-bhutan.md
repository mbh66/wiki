Norten, U. (2021). "Impact of Water Management strategies: Payment for Ecosystem Services (PES) in Bhutan." *International Journal of Environmental Science and Innovative Research* 2(8): 109-144.

The study surveys the Yakpugang scheme, in which Mongar town pays the village's community forest group to protect the springs that supply it. It reports payments of Nu 178,200 a year from town users and Nu 50,000 a year from the Mongar Regional Referral Hospital, and gives the watershed protection zone as 373.129 acres, though other passages give 538, 638 and 1,176.22 acres. It finds Yakpugang residents more aware of the payment than town dwellers, about 20% of whom knew of it. The wiki uses it for current payment levels and notes its inconsistent area figures.
