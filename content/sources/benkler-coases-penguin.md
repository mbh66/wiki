Benkler, Y. (2002). "Coase's Penguin, or, Linux and The Nature of the Firm." *Yale Law Journal*, 112(3), 369–446.

Benkler argues that networked information technology makes possible a third mode of production beside firms and markets: commons-based peer production, in which many people contribute to a shared project without prices or managerial orders, as in free software and Wikipedia. It works best where tasks are modular and fine-grained. The wiki reads peer production as the production counterpart of TIME's Emergent form, and as the base of cosmo-local production, while noting that its physical output remains small.
