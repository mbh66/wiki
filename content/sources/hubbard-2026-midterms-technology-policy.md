Hubbard, S. (2026, 10 November). What the 2026 midterm results mean for technology policy. Ash Center, Harvard Kennedy School. https://ash.harvard.edu/articles/what-the-2026-midterm-results-mean-for-technology-policy/

Hubbard, of the Allen Lab for Democracy Renovation, reviews the technology questions on the November 2026 ballots. She records that 40 of 41 local data centre measures favoured tighter limits, 30 of them by more than 70 per cent, lists local measures on licence plate readers and surveillance technology, and reports US\$55.7 million spent by AI-focused super PACs by late September. She names affordability, electricity bills, data centres and AI's effect on jobs as the central concerns. The wiki relies on it to keep votes about power, water and land apart from votes about surveillance.
