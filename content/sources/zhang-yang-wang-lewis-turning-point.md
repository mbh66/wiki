Zhang, X., Yang, J., & Wang, S. (2011). "China Has Reached the Lewis Turning Point." *China Economic Review*, 22(4), 542–554.

Using primary surveys of wage rates, which the authors judge more reliable than Chinese employment statistics, the paper finds "a clear rising trend of real wages rate since 2003," with wages rising even in slack seasons, and concludes that "the era of surplus labor is over." The wiki uses it to date the erosion of China's labor saving to the 2000s, well before the tariffs of 2018, which bears on whether the shift in production location is structural or political.
