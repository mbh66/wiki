Geels, F.W. (2002). Technological transitions as evolutionary reconfiguration processes: a multi-level perspective and a case-study. *Research Policy* 31(8-9), 1257-1274.

Geels reads the shift from sailing ships to steamships through three levels. Niches are spaces where new things are tried; the regime is the settled set of rules, technologies and practices in a sector; the landscape is the wider context neither controls. New technologies break through when landscape pressure loosens the regime. The wiki uses the three levels as the frame for its brief on protective space, and keeps the term landscape only in this technical sense.
