Botha, L., Grobbelaar, S. and Bam, W. (2016). Towards a framework to guide the evaluation of inclusive innovation systems. *South African Journal of Industrial Engineering* 27(3), 64-78.

The authors set out a framework for evaluating innovation systems meant to include people usually left out of them. The wiki cites it beside reflexive monitoring and developmental evaluation as a South African contribution to evaluating young initiatives, and records that the search behind its brief found no evaluation of a grassroots niche on communal or mission-station land.
