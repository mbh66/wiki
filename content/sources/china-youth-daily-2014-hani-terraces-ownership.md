中国青年报 (2014). 「申遗成功后的哈尼梯田 谁是主人」 [Who owns the Hani terraces after World Heritage listing?]. Republished by China Daily, 18 February 2014.

The county tourism bureau director said the Shibo tourism company gives 10% of ticket income to residents through village committees, and Shibo's office director said it pays 10% of pre-tax ticket revenue to the county government and does not know how it is distributed. Villagers said they had never seen the money, a village committee staff member had not heard of it, and the Hani scholar who led the nomination experts argued that residents should receive at least half of ticket revenue. The wiki cites it for the contested revenue flow in the main scenic area.
