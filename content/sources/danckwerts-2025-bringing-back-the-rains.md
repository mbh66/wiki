Danckwerts, B. (2025). Bringing back the Rains to Southern Africa. *Biotic Regulation and Biotic Pump*, guest post, 25 April 2025.

Published with an introduction by Anastassia Makarieva, the report covers a small regional WhatsApp network, about US$600 raised toward a US$20,000 radio budget, the Moto waMvula small-stick stove, failed approaches to ministers, and the hope that climate models could be run with different tree cover. It closes by asking readers to reconcile a record Limpopo flow with the weakened-pump story. The wiki cites it as evidence that awareness without income did not produce uptake, and for the attribution problem a rainfall outcome carries.
