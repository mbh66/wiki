Department for International Development. (1999). *Sustainable Livelihoods Guidance Sheets*. London: DFID. Section 2, *Framework*, sheets 2.1 to 2.6 (April 1999). A copy of the full set is also held by the [Livelihoods Centre](https://www2.cruzroja.es/web/livelihoods-centre/-/sustainable-livelihoods-guidance-sheets).

The sheets set out the sustainable livelihoods framework DFID adopted in the late 1990s, building on work at the Institute of Development Studies and others. Section 2 explains the framework's five parts: the vulnerability context of trends, shocks and seasonality; the five capitals (human, social, natural, physical, financial) of the asset pentagon; the transforming structures and processes that govern access, terms of exchange and returns; livelihood strategies; and livelihood outcomes. The sheets present the framework as a flexible, people-centered tool and acknowledge that methods for analyzing structures and processes were poorly developed. The wiki takes from it the five capitals as a settlement-level inventory and pairs it with [[frameworks/political-economy-analysis|political economy analysis]] to cover that gap.
