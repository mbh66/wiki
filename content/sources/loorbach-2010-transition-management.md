Loorbach, D. (2010). Transition management for sustainable development: a prescriptive, complexity-based governance framework. *Governance* 23(1), 161-183.

Loorbach describes a cycle for steering long-term change: a small transition arena of front-runners, a shared agenda, experiments, and reflexive evaluation that feeds back into the cycle. The framework draws on the Dutch energy transition programme, which the author's institute advised, so it is graded here as an account by people involved in the work. The wiki reads the arena as a closed space for early learning that needs planned links outward, and cites it in [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
