Kemp, L. (2025). *Goliath's Curse: The History and Future of Societal Collapse*. New York: Alfred A. Knopf; London: Viking.

Kemp, a researcher on existential risk at Cambridge, calls hierarchical states "Goliaths" and traces their collapse to rising inequality, elite extraction and capture of the state. He finds that collapse often left ordinary people better off, and argues that the present world forms a single global Goliath with no outside. The wiki draws on the book for two steps of the inference in the Cycle of Globalization: that a global civilization has no outside successor, and that the new can emerge from those the late phases left out.
