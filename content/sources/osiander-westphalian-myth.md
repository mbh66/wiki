Osiander, A. (2001). "Sovereignty, International Relations, and the Westphalian Myth." *International Organization* 55(2): 251 to 287.

Osiander reads the 1648 treaties closely and finds that they confirmed the constitution of the Holy Roman Empire, with no doctrine of sovereign equality. The founding story of international relations is largely a later construction. The wiki cites it as the main caveat on the 1648 start of the Cycle of Globalization.
