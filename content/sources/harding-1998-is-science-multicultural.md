Harding, S. (1998). *Is Science Multicultural? Postcolonialisms, Feminisms, and Epistemologies*. Indiana University Press, Bloomington and Indianapolis. x + 242 pp.

Sandra Harding argues that modern European science is one local knowledge system among many [IC]. It spread with European expansion from the early modern period, and it drew on other knowledge traditions as it went [TBV].

Ingrid Bartsch's review in Hypatia confirms this reading [IC]. On step T7 the book accepts that a portable science travelled, and holds that it remains a local system.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T7.
