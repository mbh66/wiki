Norberg-Hodge, H. (2019). *Local Is Our Future: Steps to an Economics of Happiness*. Local Futures. [Publisher page](https://www.localfutures.org/publications/local-is-our-future-book-helena-norberg-hodge/).

Helena Norberg-Hodge argues for a shift from global to localised economies, starting with food [IC]. She holds that localisation eases several crises at once, ecological, social and personal, and she treats economic globalisation as their main cause [TBV].

Emily Duncan reviewed the book in the Journal of Agriculture, Food Systems, and Community Development [IC]. No open copy was found. EarthDance draws on the author's earlier work in Ladakh.
