Gershenfeld, N. (2025, February). "The Band Began to Play: The History and Future of Fab Labs." *Make:*.

The founder of the fab lab network recounts its growth from a first lab at MIT to about 3,000 labs in some 150 countries, and sets out a path from labs that make prototypes to networks that make products. The wiki cites the count as the author's own, and uses it to show that distributed fabrication is widespread in number and small in output.
