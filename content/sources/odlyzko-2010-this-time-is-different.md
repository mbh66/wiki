Odlyzko, A. (2010). This time is different: an example of a giant, wildly speculative, and successful investment mania. Working paper, University of Minnesota, revised 21 June 2010. https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/mania01.pdf

The paper studies the British railway boom of the 1830s, which committed capital comparable as a share of GDP to about US\$2 trillion in the United States today, and at its peak more than 8 per cent of GDP. It was wildly speculative, and it paid. Odlyzko uses it to argue that manias are not all failures. For the wiki it is the strongest historical counter-case to the claim that every infrastructure boom ends in investor losses, and the [[research/dark-ai-and-the-place-based-inheritance|Dark AI brief]] grades that claim false on its evidence.
