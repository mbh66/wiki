Grossman, G. M., & Rossi-Hansberg, E. (2008). "Trading Tasks: A Simple Theory of Offshoring." *American Economic Review*, 98(5), 1978–1997.

The paper that moved trade theory from goods to tasks. As the cost of coordinating work across borders falls, firms offshore tasks one by one, and offshoring can raise the wages of the workers whose tasks stay home. It connects the firm's decision to outsource with trade between countries. The wiki uses it to keep two theories apart: comparative advantage explains trade between nations, and trade in tasks explains why firms split production across them.
