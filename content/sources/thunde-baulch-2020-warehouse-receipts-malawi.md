Thunde, J. and Baulch, B. (2020). *Who Uses and Who Benefits From Warehouse Receipt Systems? An Examination of Contract Level Transactions on the Agricultural Commodity Exchange for Africa, 2011-2018*. MaSSP Working Paper 35, IFPRI.

Across 710 receipts issued from 2011 to 2018, large traders took out the most receipts and stored the largest volumes, farmers the fewest; 48% of receipts made a profit, and maize depositors who borrowed against their receipts lost about 7.4% on average. The paper's table of African warehouse receipt systems gives volumes in tonnes, including Ghana's 47,000 tonnes in 2016 and South Africa's 3.0 million in 2019/20. The wiki uses it for the commodity receipt cases.
