Hummels, D. L., & Schaur, G. (2013). "Time as a Trade Barrier." *American Economic Review*, 103(7), 2935–2959.

Using US import data on the choice between air and ocean shipping, the authors estimate that "each day in transit is equivalent to an ad-valorem tariff of 0.6 to 2.1 percent." The wiki uses the estimate to explain why delay and unreliability, such as diversions around the Cape during the 2026 Hormuz closure, act as a tax on long supply chains even when freight rates are low.
