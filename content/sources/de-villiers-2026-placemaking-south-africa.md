de Villiers, A., Esler, K.J., Cockburn, J. and Spierenburg, M. (2026). "Remaking place relations for social-ecological restoration: reflections on placemaking in South Africa." *People and Nature*.

Written as a collaborative reflection on practice with Living Lands, the article draws on interviews with 12 Baviaanskloof and 6 Langkloof residents and reports that farmers remained willing to keep working with the organization after a disappointment. Because it is co-produced with the implementing organization, the wiki cites it as peer-reviewed and partner co-produced, and keeps the case at [MS]. The second enrichment run dated it 2025; the publisher's record gives 2026.
