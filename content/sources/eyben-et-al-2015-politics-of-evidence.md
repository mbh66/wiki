Eyben, R., Guijt, I., Roche, C. and Shutt, C. (eds) (2015). *The Politics of Evidence and Results in International Development: Playing the Game to Change the Rules?* Rugby: Practical Action Publishing.

An edited collection in which people working in aid describe how results frameworks and evidence demands from funders shape what programmes do, and how staff respond. The cases describe work drifting toward what can be counted. The wiki uses it on how premature measurement can steer a young initiative, and on why a forum should agree what it will measure before a funder asks.
