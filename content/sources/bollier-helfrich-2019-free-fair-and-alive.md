Bollier, D. and Helfrich, S. (2019). *Free, Fair, and Alive: The Insurgent Power of the Commons*. New Society Publishers, Gabriola Island, September 2019. [Read online](https://freefairandalive.org/).

David Bollier and Silke Helfrich describe commoning as a social form in its own right [TBV]. They set out patterns of social life, peer governance and provisioning, and argue that commons can grow beside market and state and in tension with them [TBV].

Run 6a read the book through reviews and found none showing it take up place-based and globally shared knowledge working together; the square on T9 records that silence [TBV]. A reading of the full text could change it.
