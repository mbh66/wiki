State of Delaware (2013). *Delaware General Corporation Law*, Title 8, Chapter 1, Subchapter XV: Public Benefit Corporations, sections 361 to 368. As in force on 7 November 2026.

The subchapter creates the [[public-benefit-corporation|public benefit corporation]]: a for-profit company "intended to produce a public benefit or public benefits and to operate in a responsible and sustainable manner" (section 362). Its directors must balance "the pecuniary interests of the stockholders, the best interests of those materially affected by the corporation's conduct, and the specific public benefit" named in the charter (section 365), report to stockholders on that benefit at least every two years (section 366), and answer to suits brought only by holders of at least 2 percent of the shares or, for a listed company, shares worth US\$2 million (section 367). The wiki reads the statute as a test of what a mission written into a charter can bind: it obliges directors to weigh the benefit without setting a weight, and leaves enforcement with the shareholders who elect the board.
