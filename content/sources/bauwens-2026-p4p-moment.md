Bauwens, M. (2026). "Understanding the importance of the P4P moment in the long evolution of the commons." *Fourth Generation Civilization* (Substack), 16 August 2026. [Open copy](https://4thgenerationcivilization.substack.com/p/understanding-the-importance-of-the).

Michel Bauwens defines P4P as Peer FOR Peer (collective) infrastructure. He names the Archipelago of Regenerative Projects as a third attractor, an alternative both to Western private platforms and to China's fusion of state planning and markets [TBV]. He places it in the long evolution of the commons, with knowledge shared globally, production regenerative in place, and coordination through bioregions and federation [TBV]. He describes the seed forms as still marginal [TBV]. The term P4P is Jeff Emmett's coinage, which Bauwens adopted; see [[p4p-peer-for-peer|P4P (Peer FOR Peer)]].

Michel Bauwens is close to the BioConomy network, and the essay names Michael Haupt's account of nodes of coordination and his TIMN-based account of a fifth, mycelial form of coordination. The network first recorded this account under the working title [[sources/bauwens-peer-for-peer|"Peer FOR Peer: The Emerging Third Attractor"]]; this essay is its published text.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T2; it is silent on T3; it is silent on T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it affirms T9.
