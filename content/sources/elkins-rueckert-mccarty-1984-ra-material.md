Don Elkins, Carla Rueckert and James Allen McCarty (1984). *The Ra Material: The Law of One, Book I*. Whitford Press; reissued by L/L Research, Louisville, KY. ISBN: 089865260X.

In the authors' own description, the book is an exact transcript of 26 recorded sessions of an experiment in communication [IC]. Elkins, a physics professor, asked the questions; Rueckert spoke as the instrument; McCarty kept the record [IC]. In all, 106 sessions were held from 1981 to 1984 [IC]. The authors report that a source calling itself Ra spoke through Rueckert [IC].

This is testimony (E5), and E5 carries no lower worth [MS]. The source the authors report cannot be checked by any independent means [IC]. According to the L/L Research summary, the material teaches a Law of One, an infinite Creator, seven densities of consciousness and free will [TBV]. Readers in New Age communities treat it as a spiritual teaching, and it has no scientific reception [TBV].

The wiki takes from it one of Tom Morgan's sources, described as its authors present it [TBV]. T5 is left uncoded because no passage on it was located.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it affirms T1; it affirms T2; it affirms T6.
