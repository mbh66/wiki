Aboagye, A. (2023). "Structuring African Warehouse Receipt Systems to Succeed." *Athens Journal of Business & Economics* 9: 1-18.

Aboagye finds that certified systems handle small shares of national output (about 1.2% of Ghana's maize in 2015), that large traders, processors and larger farmers receive most receipts, and that grading, minimum deposits and high interest often cancel the price gains. Community warehouses in northern Ghana did better for smallholders. The wiki uses it on who backed money with stored grain benefits in practice.
