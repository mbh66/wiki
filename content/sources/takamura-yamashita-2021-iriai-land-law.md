高村学人・山下詠子 (2021). 「表題部所有者不明土地適正化法の入会地へのインパクトと求められる探索的調査」 [The impact on iriai land of the act on land with unclear registered owners, and the exploratory research required]. *入会林野研究* 41: 2-15.

The authors examine how a recent law on land whose registered owners are unclear bears on iriai land, much of which is still registered in the names of long-dead members or groups. As cited in the second enrichment run, they put the area reorganized at about a quarter of the iriai forest that existed at enactment, which differs from the Forestry Agency's 31% to 48%. The wiki records the difference.
