Singapore Ministry of Transport. (2026, 4 August). "Increases in War Risk Insurance Premiums, Marine Insurance Costs and Freight Rates due to US-Iran Conflict." Written reply by Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow to a parliamentary question.

The minister reported industry estimates that war-risk cover for Strait of Hormuz transits had risen to 3.5 to 7.5 percent of hull and machinery value per transit by July 2026, from 0.10 to 0.25 percent before the conflict, and that Asia to North Europe container rates were about 120 percent above pre-conflict levels while still about 40 percent below the 2024 Red Sea peak. The wiki uses the reply as an official record of transport risk returning as a priced input to the location of production.
