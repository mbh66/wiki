Wightman, A. (2025, August). Gresham House is now Scotland's 2nd largest landowner. *Land Matters*. https://andywightman.scot/2025/08/gresham-house-is-now-scotlands-2nd-largest-landowner/

Following Gresham House's purchase of the Griffin forestry estate in July 2025, Wightman revises his count and finds at least 66,879 hectares held through Gresham House partnerships, which passes Buccleuch Estates' roughly 65,000. He notes that the holdings are split across many separate parcels. Gresham House's position, recorded by others, is that the land belongs to its funds' investors. The wiki cites the post beside Gresham House's own figure of about 120,000 hectares registered to it or its funds.
