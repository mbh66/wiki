Wightman, A. (2024, March). Who owns Scotland 2024 (a preliminary analysis). *Land Matters*. https://andywightman.scot/2024/03/who-owns-scotland-2024-a-preliminary-analysis/

Wightman, a land reform researcher and former member of the Scottish Parliament, updates his long-running count of who holds Scotland's land, using Land Register records. His 2024 table puts Wildland (Anders Povlsen) first at 88,296 hectares, Buccleuch Estates second at 66,345, and Gresham House third at 53,775, held through partnerships; Gresham House owned no land in Scotland in 2012. The wiki uses his counts as an independent measure of how fast investment funds are acquiring land, set beside the funds' own figures.
