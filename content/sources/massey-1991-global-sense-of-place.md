Massey, D. (1991). *A Global Sense of Place*. Marxism Today, June 1991, pp. 24-29. Reprinted in Massey, *Space, Place and Gender* (University of Minnesota Press, 1994).

Doreen Massey argues that a place is a meeting point of social relations that stretch well beyond it [TBV]. A sense of place built on a bounded, rooted identity can turn reactionary, she holds, and a progressive sense of place is open and looks outward [TBV]. She names the power geometry of these connections: some groups direct the flows of people, money and goods, and others are moved by them [TBV].

The essay is a standard reference for a relational view of place in human geography [IC]. One annotation cites it as Marxism Today 38; the magazine's archive and a 2026 encyclopedia entry give the June 1991 issue, pp. 24-29 [IC]. The wiki reads it as local and global joined in each place, with a warning about who controls the joining (inference).

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
