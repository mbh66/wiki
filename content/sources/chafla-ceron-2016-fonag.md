Chafla, P. and Cerón, P. (2016). "Pago por servicios ambientales en el sector del agua: el Fondo para la Protección del Agua." *Tecnología y Ciencias del Agua* 7(6): 25-40.

The authors analyze FONAG as a payment scheme for water. They state that each contributor sits on the steering committee "y recibe un porcentaje de voto proporcional a su contribución," put the trust's capital at about US\$12 million, and note the fund's heavy dependence on the city's water and electricity utilities. The wiki takes from it, with FONAG's own records, the finding that the fund's votes belong to its institutional contributors and that páramo communities hold none.
