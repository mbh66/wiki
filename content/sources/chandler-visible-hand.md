Chandler, A. D., Jr. (1977). *The Visible Hand: The Managerial Revolution in American Business*. Cambridge, MA: Belknap Press of Harvard University Press.

Chandler shows how the railroad and the telegraph made possible the large, multiunit, managerially run corporation, whose administrative coordination replaced market coordination across much of the US economy from the late nineteenth century. The wiki uses the book to tie one stage in a possible evolution of production, the national corporation, to a communications revolution, one of the properties TIME requires.
