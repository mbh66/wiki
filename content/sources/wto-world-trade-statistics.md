World Trade Organization. *World Trade Statistics*. Geneva: WTO. Accessed 8 November 2026.

The WTO's statistics page reports that services reached their highest share of world trade since 2005 in 2025. The page gives the share as 27.5 percent in its body and 27.6 percent in its summary, and states goods growth in two ways, so the wiki uses the body figure and records the inconsistency. The series is the wiki's measure of light trade outgrowing heavy trade.
