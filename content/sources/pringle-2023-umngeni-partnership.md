Pringle, C.B., Meissner, R., Biggs, R., Pahl-Wostl, C., Stuart-Hill, S. and Sitas, N. (2023). "Exploring social processes in transformation: the case of a collaborative water partnership in South Africa." *Ecosystems and People* 19(1): 2213780.

The study traces how the uMngeni partnership, coordinated by SANBI with eThekwini Municipality, built relationships among government, business, academic and civil society members. It finds that the partnership was a leading voice in drafting the catchment management strategy, yet the concept of ecological infrastructure did not appear in the final document. The wiki takes from it the partnership's structure and the limit of its influence, and notes that no rural community or land-rights holder appears among the members.
