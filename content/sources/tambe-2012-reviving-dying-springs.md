Tambe, S., Kharel, G., Arrawatia, M.L., Kulkarni, H., Mahamuni, K. and Ganeriwala, A.K. (2012). "Reviving Dying Springs: Climate Change Adaptation Experiments From the Sikkim Himalaya." *Mountain Research and Development* 32(1): 62-72.

The paper reports the start of Sikkim's Dhara Vikas program, which maps the recharge area of a spring and builds trenches and ponds there under India's rural employment scheme. As cited on this wiki, it records that about 80% of rural households in Sikkim depend on springs, and that lean-season discharge in five treated springs rose from 4.4 to 14.4 liters per minute, in a before-after design without controls. The lead author worked in the state department that ran the program. The wiki takes from it the most explicit naming of a mountain's retention function as something a public program can rebuild.
