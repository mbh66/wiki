McKinsey Global Institute. (2025, 27 January). "Geopolitics and the Geometry of Global Trade: 2025 Update."

MGI measures the geopolitical distance of trade, using countries' voting at the United Nations, and finds that it fell about 7 percent between 2017 and 2024, while the average geographic distance a dollar of trade travels rose by about 10 kilometers a year to about 5,200 kilometers. It concludes that nearshoring is not yet happening on a global scale. The wiki treats the finding as the clearest sign that trade is regrouping into blocs, graded as one consultancy's construction.
