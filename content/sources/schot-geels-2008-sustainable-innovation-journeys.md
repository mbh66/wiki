Schot, J. and Geels, F.W. (2008). Strategic niche management and sustainable innovation journeys: theory, findings, research agenda, and policy. *Technology Analysis and Strategic Management* 20(5), 537-554.

Schot and Geels review a decade of strategic niche management research. They name three internal processes that help a niche develop: shaping expectations, building networks and learning. They conclude that these must be studied alongside what happens outside the niche. The wiki takes the three processes as the nurturing codes (N1 to N3) used to compare cases in [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
