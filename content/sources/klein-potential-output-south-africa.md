Klein, N. (2011). *Measuring the Potential Output of South Africa*. IMF African Department Paper No. 11/2. Washington: International Monetary Fund.

Klein estimates potential growth by several methods and finds it "very modest, at slightly below 1 percent" in 1985 to 1994, which he attributes to "the high economic costs associated with isolation of the apartheid regime." Estimated potential growth rises after 1994. The wiki cites the estimate in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as the main quantitative reading of governance costs in the late National Party period.
