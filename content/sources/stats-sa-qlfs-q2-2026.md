Statistics South Africa (2026, August). *Quarterly Labour Force Survey, Quarter 2: 2026*. Statistical release P0211. Pretoria: Stats SA.

The survey puts the official unemployment rate at 33.6 percent and the expanded rate, which adds the potential labor force, at 43.8 percent. The wiki uses both in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as the latest reading of work, the household measure that has tracked GDP most closely in the South African record. The link is to the release's media statement.
