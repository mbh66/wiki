Seitz, N., Buratti, M., Lehmann, E.E. and Eberle, N. (2026). Do startup incubators enhance new venture performance? Evidence from a meta-analysis. *Review of Managerial Science* 20, 4693-4745.

A second 2026 meta-analysis, smaller than Murillo-Rojas and others, finds a statistically significant positive effect of incubation on venture performance across financial, operational and strategic measures. Programme specialisation, public sponsorship and regional context change the size of the effect. The wiki sets it beside the survival evidence: incubation can help ventures perform while they are young, and that is a different outcome from surviving once protection ends.
