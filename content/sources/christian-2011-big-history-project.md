David Christian (2011). *Big History Project*. Online high-school course.

The Big History Project is a high-school course created by the historian David Christian of Macquarie University [IC]. It teaches the history of the universe from the Big Bang in a single course [IC]. Liana Loewus reports in *Education Week* (8 September 2014) that Bill Gates personally invested 10 million US dollars in it, and that it began in five high schools in 2011 [TBV]. Andrew Ross Sorkin reports in *The New York Times Magazine* (September 2014) that it then reached more than 15,000 students in some 1,200 schools [TBV]. *Business Insider* gave the same figure from the project's organisers, so the two reports share one source [TBV]. Sorkin also reports that the University of California accepts a version of the course toward its world history requirement [TBV].

Sam Wineburg, a Stanford historian, has said in the press that the course does not teach students how to read a text [TBV]. [[sources/conrad-2019-big-history-project-colonizing-knowledges|Conrad (2019)]] gives the first scholarly critique of it as curriculum [TBV].

The wiki takes from it a measure of how far a shared cosmic story has reached schools [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T5.
