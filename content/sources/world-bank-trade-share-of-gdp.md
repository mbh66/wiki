World Bank. *Trade (% of GDP), World* (indicator NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS). World Bank national accounts data and OECD National Accounts data files. Accessed 7 November 2026.

The series shows world trade rising from about 25 percent of world GDP in 1970 to about 61 percent in 2008, falling in 2009 and standing at about 56 percent in 2019. The wiki uses it as the plainest measure of globalization's rise through the acceleration phase and of the plateau after 2008. A single ratio hides much: it says nothing about who owns the trade or where its gains settle, which is why the wiki reads it alongside the [[concepts/deceleration-indicators|deceleration indicators]].
