Morgan, T. (2024, November 10). Thinking the Unthinkable: Chaos at the consciousness crossroads. *The Leading Edge*.

Morgan argues that a left-hemisphere-dominant culture is cut off from "the attractor," a force drawing each person toward the niche where what only they can do meets what the world needs, and that the Tao names the path of integration it opens. He rephrases Brian Whetten's "the lower cannot see the higher" as "the disconnected cannot see the connected." The wiki takes that rephrasing as the step toward its own: an economy abstracted from place cannot see place-based coordination. It does not adopt the essay's claims about psi.
