Lee R. Berger, Tebogo V. Makhubela, Keneiloe Molopyane, John Hawks et al. (2025). *Evidence for deliberate burial of the dead by Homo naledi*. eLife 12: RP89106. DOI: [10.7554/eLife.89106](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.89106).

Working in the Rising Star cave system in the Cradle of Humankind, Lee Berger and colleagues report pits holding articulated *Homo naledi* remains, which they interpret as deliberate burials by a hominin with a brain about a third the size of a modern human's [IC]. *Homo naledi* remains from the Dinaledi Chamber have been dated to between about 335,000 and 236,000 years ago [IC]. The work went through *eLife*'s public review: its 2023 version was assessed as inadequate, and in 2025 one reviewer judged burial convincingly shown while another judged the evidence incomplete [IC]. Martinón-Torres and colleagues dispute it. The wiki takes it as an open question on step A2, and gives no site location beyond the published heritage record.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as an evidence work in palaeoanthropology, with evidence type E2 and claim codes A2, A3.
