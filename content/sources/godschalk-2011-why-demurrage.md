Godschalk, H. (2011). "Why Demurrage?" Paper presented in Lyon, 16 February 2011. Not peer-reviewed.

Godschalk reviews the case for demurrage and concludes that its rate "seems to be not crucial for the economic results," that empirical data do not yet allow statements on its effect on velocity, and that WIR, without demurrage, is the most successful complementary currency. He estimates the Chiemgauer's velocity at 10.6 in 2009. The wiki uses it as the main independent caution on demurrage's claimed effect.
