Siegler, R. S. (1996). *Emerging Minds: The Process of Change in Children's Thinking*. Oxford University Press.

The developmental psychologist Robert Siegler argues from repeated microgenetic studies that children's thinking changes gradually: several strategies coexist, each rising and falling in use, as new ones are built and less effective ones fade or are dropped [IC]. He offers this overlapping waves model as an alternative to Piaget's staircase of stages [IC]. The wiki takes it as a measured qualifier of step C1. It grants that older and newer ways of thinking coexist for long periods, and shows that older ones decline and some are abandoned, which is weaker than every earlier structure staying active in everyone (inference).
