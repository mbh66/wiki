Smith, A., Stirling, A. and Berkhout, F. (2005). The governance of sustainable socio-technical transitions. *Research Policy* 34(10), 1491-1510.

The authors show that the direction of a transition depends on how the pressures on a regime are articulated and on the resources regime members can draw on to respond. They argue that steering a transition is political. The wiki cites it for the point that protection is granted by people with power over a sector, and that the terms they set shape which initiatives survive.
