Popper, K. (1957). *The Poverty of Historicism*. London: Routledge & Kegan Paul.

Popper holds that trends are not laws and that the growth of knowledge makes the course of history unpredictable. The wiki applies the argument to the dated projections of the Cycle of Globalization, which it holds as forecasts. On the Anabranch layer, run 9 codes it as dissenting from T4: it denies any law of evolution and treats belief in unconditional trends as historicism's central mistake, which counts as a denial of striving (inference).


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T3; it dissents on T4; it is silent on T5; it qualifies T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|claim grid]] codes its positions on the claim chain: it dissents from B3 and C2.
