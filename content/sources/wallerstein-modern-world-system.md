Wallerstein, I. (1974 to 2011). *The Modern World-System*, 4 vols. New York: Academic Press; Berkeley: University of California Press.

Wallerstein dates the capitalist world-economy from the long sixteenth century and identifies brief periods of hegemony by the Dutch Republic, Britain and the United States within it. The wiki cites the work for the succession pattern, and notes that it starts the system well before the 1648 anchor used in the Cycle of Globalization.
