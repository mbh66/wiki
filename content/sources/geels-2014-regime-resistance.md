Geels, F.W. (2014). Regime resistance against low-carbon transitions: introducing politics and power into the multi-level perspective. *Theory, Culture and Society* 31(5), 21-40.

Geels answers the power critique from inside the framework. He describes how incumbent actors in the UK electricity sector resist low-carbon change through instrumental, discursive, material and institutional forms of power. He keeps the multi-level perspective and adds power to it. The wiki treats this as a partial reply: it answers the charge that power is missing, and leaves open the critics' wish for a different starting point.
