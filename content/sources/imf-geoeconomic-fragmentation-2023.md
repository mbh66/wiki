Aiyar, S., Chen, J., Ebeke, C., Garcia-Saltos, R., Gudmundsson, T., Ilyina, A., Kangur, A., Kunaratskul, T., Rodriguez, S., Ruta, M., Schulze, T., Soderberg, G., & Trevino, J. P. (2023). *Geoeconomic Fragmentation and the Future of Multilateralism*. IMF Staff Discussion Note SDN/2023/001. Washington, DC: International Monetary Fund.

IMF staff estimate the long-run cost of trade fragmentation at between 0.2 and 7 percent of world GDP, depending on its severity, and up to 8 to 12 percent in some countries if technology also decouples, with emerging and low-income economies hit hardest. The wiki cites the note as the strongest statement of the comparative-advantage objection to relocalizing production, and keeps its estimate beside the evidence for glocalization.
