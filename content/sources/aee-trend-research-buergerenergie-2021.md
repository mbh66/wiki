Agentur für Erneuerbare Energien. (2021, 15 January). "Studie: Bürgerenergie bleibt zentrale Säule der Energiewende." Reporting trend:research, *Eigentümerstruktur: Erneuerbare Energien*.

Reports a trend:research study of who owns Germany's renewable generating capacity: individuals and farmers held 40.4 percent in 2019, down from 42 percent in 2016, and about a quarter of the capacity added in 2019. The wiki uses it as the one measured trend in place-based ownership of production found for its glocalization brief, and records that the trend is downward.
