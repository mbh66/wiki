Maria Montessori (1948). *To Educate the Human Potential*. Kalakshetra Publications, Adyar, Madras [IC].

Montessori wrote the book during her years in India, and it sets out Cosmic Education for children of about six to twelve years old [IC]. It offers great stories of the universe, the Earth, life and early civilisations, tied to her idea of a Cosmic Plan [IC]. She asks what task people have in so wonderful a universe (p. 10, as quoted in an ERIC article) [TBV].

Montessori writing treats the book as the founding text of Cosmic Education [TBV]. A 2024 American Montessori Society essay warns that giving natural forces agency may confuse children about science [TBV]. No figure was found for how many schools teach Cosmic Education [TBV]. The book was not read for this record; it is confirmed in the montessori.org bibliography [IC].

The wiki takes from it the closest statement in print of step T5. Her Introduction places all beings, knowingly or not, within a cosmic plan, and Frierson (2018) confirms that she gives each person a cosmic task [IC]. Anabranch run 9 moved the book from qualifying to affirming T5 on 25 November 2026.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T2; it is silent on T3; it is silent on T4; it affirms T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8.
