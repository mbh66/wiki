Central Statistics Office Ireland (n.d.). *Trust Survey 2023: International Comparisons*. Cork: CSO. Accessed 22 October 2026.

The release sets Ireland's results beside those of other countries in the [[sources/oecd-trust-survey-2024|OECD trust survey]], where respondents rate trust on a scale of 0 to 10 and a score of 6 or more counts as high or moderately high. It reports that 39.3 percent of people across the 30 participating countries (unweighted average) had high or moderately high trust in their national government, with 26.7 percent in the United Kingdom and 48.5 percent in Canada. The wiki cites it in [[research/what-gdp-leaves-out|What GDP Leaves Out]] for country figures that the OECD country notes did not make retrievable.
