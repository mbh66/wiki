Arrighi, G. (1994). *The Long Twentieth Century: Money, Power and the Origins of Our Times*. London: Verso. 2nd ed. 2010.

Each systemic cycle of accumulation passes from material expansion to financial expansion, marked by a signal crisis and ended by a terminal crisis, after which a new center leads. Arrighi places the signal crisis of US hegemony in the Vietnam War years. The wiki uses the book as the main evidence for hegemonic succession inside one world-system, the inner rhythm of the Cycle of Globalization.
