Cox, M., Arnold, G. and Villamayor Tomás, S. (2010). "A Review of Design Principles for Community-based Natural Resource Management." *Ecology and Society* 15(4): 38.

The review codes 91 studies against Elinor Ostrom's eight design principles and finds them well supported empirically. It splits the boundary and congruence principles into two parts each, and notes that the principles describe structure more than trust and legitimacy. The wiki takes from it the evidence base behind reading the long-lived mountain commons in the register (Törbel, the acequias, the Regole) through bounded membership and user-made rules.
