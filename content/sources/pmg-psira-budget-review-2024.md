Portfolio Committee on Police (2024, 23 October). *Budgetary Review and Recommendations Report on the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority, 2023/24*. Cape Town: Parliament of South Africa.

The report records 608,977 registered active private security officers at the end of March 2024. The wiki cites the figure in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as a measure of defensive spending: security that households and firms buy privately counts in GDP as output.
