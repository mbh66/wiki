IUCN (2019). "Nature-based solutions in practice: the example of the Namey Nichu watershed in Bhutan." 1 March 2019.

IUCN describes a community forest group in Paro that keeps a 50-meter buffer around springs and streams, bans night grazing, cleans the sources twice a year and patrols the forest, with compliance verified twice a year by a team from the group, the forest department and the water users. It reports that four hotels and water associations pay the group about US\$2,000 a year, which the group spends on community investments. Consultations for a second phase from December 2018 raised concerns about water allocation to hotels, equitable distribution and springs drying in the dry season. The wiki takes from it the scheme's terms and the first signs of strain as hotel demand grows. Its count of four hotels differs from SNV's five.
