Ronfeldt, D. (2026). *Our World's Ballooning Noosphere (3 of 3): Prospective Rise Of 'Axial Entities Of The Noosphere' (AEONs)*. Onward With TIMN (Substack), 18 May 2026. [Read online](https://davidronfeldt.substack.com/p/our-worlds-ballooning-noosphere-3).

David Ronfeldt closes a three-part series on his noosphere paper by asking what might steward the noosphere [TBV]. He proposes autonomous AI-powered entities, which he calls Axial Entities of the Noosphere (AEONs), holding knowledge from all civilisations [TBV]. They would sit as central nodes in holonic networks that knit a commons realm centred on care, from local to planetary scales [TBV]. He judges that earlier peer-to-peer and DAO forms seemed too small to scale that far [TBV]. He mentions mycorrhizal and mycelial networks as a possible model for how the noosphere spreads [TBV]. He counts three earlier double transitions, social and noospheric at once, each enabled by an information revolution from the oral to the electrical, and holds that a fourth is beginning [IC]. He expects state and business actors to resist a commons realm [IC].

The post is the author's own account on his own Substack, and no independent discussion has been found [TBV]. The wiki reads it beside his 2026 paper, [[sources/ronfeldt-ballooning-noosphere|Our Planet's Ballooning Noosphere]], as knowledge joined at planetary scale with no grounding in place (inference).

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in political science, with evidence type E4 and claim codes B3, C2, C3, C4. It gives the noosphere's lead to AI entities and claims no new structure of awareness in individuals, so every grid square it holds qualifies.
