Ronfeldt, D. (2026). *Our World's Ballooning Noosphere (3 of 3): Prospective Rise Of 'Axial Entities Of The Noosphere' (AEONs)*. Onward With TIMN (Substack), 18 May 2026. [Read online](https://davidronfeldt.substack.com/p/our-worlds-ballooning-noosphere-3).

David Ronfeldt closes a three-part series on his noosphere paper by asking what might steward the noosphere [TBV]. He proposes autonomous AI-powered entities, which he calls Axial Entities of the Noosphere (AEONs), holding knowledge from all civilisations [TBV]. They would sit as central nodes in holonic networks that knit a commons realm centred on care, from local to planetary scales [TBV]. He judges that earlier peer-to-peer and DAO forms seemed too small to scale that far [TBV]. He mentions mycorrhizal and mycelial networks as a possible model for how the noosphere spreads [TBV].

The post is the author's own account on his own Substack, and no independent discussion has been found [TBV]. The wiki reads it beside his 2026 paper, [[sources/ronfeldt-ballooning-noosphere|Our Planet's Ballooning Noosphere]], as knowledge joined at planetary scale with no grounding in place (inference).

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
