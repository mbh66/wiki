R. L. Carhart-Harris and K. J. Friston (2019). "REBUS and the Anarchic Brain: Toward a Unified Model of the Brain Action of Psychedelics". *Pharmacological Reviews* 71(3): 316-344. DOI: [10.1124/pr.118.017160](https://doi.org/10.1124/pr.118.017160).

REBUS stands for relaxed beliefs under psychedelics [IC]. The paper joins Carhart-Harris's entropic brain hypothesis to Friston's free-energy principle [IC]. In this model the brain predicts its inputs through a hierarchy of prior beliefs; psychedelics weaken the high-level priors, so signals from lower levels shape experience more freely [IC]. The authors use the model to account for the subjective effects of psychedelics and for their proposed use in therapy [IC].

The paper is widely cited in psychedelic science [IC]. A 2020 commentary places it beside Aldous Huxley's image of the brain as a reducing valve [IC]. The comparison has limits: in REBUS the brain generates experience, and the priors it relaxes are its own (inference).

The wiki takes from it the strongest naturalistic model that echoes the filter image. It qualifies the filter claim because it keeps a brain that produces experience (inference) [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T3.
