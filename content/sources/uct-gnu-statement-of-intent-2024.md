University of Cape Town Libraries (2024, 18 June). *Statement of Intent of the 2024 Government of National Unity*. Government Publications.

The statement sets out the principles and decision rules agreed by the parties to the Government of National Unity formed after the May 2024 election. The wiki cites it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] to date the start of the third governing arrangement in the period. The page records what the parties signed and makes no assessment of the coalition.
