Engineering News (2025, 19 March). *Eskom implements Stage 2 loadshedding after loss of five generation units*. Creamer Media.

The report records a short return of load-shedding about a year after the 2024 suspension, following the loss of five generating units. The wiki cites it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] to qualify the end of load-shedding: supply ran without scheduled cuts from April 2024 to the end of that year, with brief interruptions in early 2025. Journalism, cited for a recent event only.
