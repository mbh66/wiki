Seyfang, G. and Smith, A. (2007). Grassroots innovations for sustainable development: towards a new research and policy agenda. *Environmental Politics* 16(4), 584-603.

Seyfang and Smith carry niche thinking from technologies to community groups. They argue that grassroots innovations arise from social need and ideology, run on voluntary work and grants, and seek benefits markets do not price, so findings from firms cannot be carried over to them unchanged. The wiki takes from it the distinction between technological and grassroots niches that runs through [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
