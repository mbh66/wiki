Stodder, J. (2009). "Complementary Credit Networks and Macroeconomic Stability: Switzerland's Wirtschaftsring." *Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization* 72(1): 79-95.

Stodder shows with WIR data from 1948 to 2003 that WIR money is negatively correlated with GDP in the short run, because firms short of cash in a recession make more use of WIR credit. The paper also gives the bank's participant and turnover series and records that WIR stopped charging demurrage in 1948. The wiki uses it for the WIR case and as evidence that a mutual credit network can circulate without demurrage.
