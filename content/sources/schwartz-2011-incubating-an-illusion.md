Schwartz, M. (2011). Incubating an illusion? Long-term incubator firm performance after graduation. *Growth and Change* 42(4), 491-516.

Schwartz tracks employment and sales for 324 firms that graduated from five German incubators between 1990 and 2006, including firms that later closed. Graduates grew in the first years after leaving, then most stopped growing; a minority grew strongly. The wiki cites it with the other Schwartz studies as the clearest long-run evidence on what protection does for ventures after it ends.
