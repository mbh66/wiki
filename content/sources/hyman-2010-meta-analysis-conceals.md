Ray Hyman (2010). "Meta-Analysis That Conceals More Than It Reveals: Comment on Storm et al. (2010)". *Psychological Bulletin* 136(4): 486-490. DOI 10.1037/a0019676.

Hyman comments on the meta-analysis of free-response psi studies filed as [[sources/storm-tressoldi-di-risio-2010-meta-analysis-free-response|Storm, Tressoldi and Di Risio (2010)]] [IC]. In his abstract he holds that the authors produce apparent homogeneity by removing many outliers and by combining databases into arbitrary composites [IC]. Commentators report his further points: that significance runs against sample size where it should run with it, and that researchers outside parapsychology have not replicated the ganzfeld results [TBV].

Storm, Tressoldi and Di Risio replied in the same issue with "A Meta-Analysis With Nothing to Hide", DOI 10.1037/a0019840 [IC]. Jeffrey Rouder, Richard Morey and Jordan Province reanalysed the same data with Bayes factors in 2013, and found that corrections to the data set shrink the evidence the authors reported [TBV]. The full comment was not read for this record; the record is confirmed by Crossref [IC].

The wiki takes from it the main critical comment on a T2 meta-analysis already filed [TBV].
