Lance Storm, Patrizio E. Tressoldi and Lorenzo Di Risio (2010). "Meta-analysis of free-response studies, 1992-2008: Assessing the noise reduction model in parapsychology". *Psychological Bulletin* 136(4): 471-485. DOI: [10.1037/a0019457](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0019457).

The authors pool free-response studies, among them ganzfeld studies in which a receiver in mild sensory isolation tries to describe a target, and report a consistent effect above chance [IC]. They read the result as support for a noise reduction model of psi [IC].

Ray Hyman replied in the same issue (136(4): 486-490) that the authors "manufacture apparent homogeneity and consistency" by removing outliers and combining unlike databases [IC]. Jeffrey Rouder, Richard Morey and Jordan Province reanalysed the data with Bayes factors in *Psychological Bulletin* 139(1): 241-247 (2013), DOI 10.1037/a0029008 [IC]. They granted that an uncritical treatment of the full set favours an effect, and argued that problems in the data set weaken it [TBV]. Storm and colleagues answered Hyman in 2010, and answered Rouder's team with Jessica Utts in 2013 [IC].

The wiki takes from it the strongest meta-analytic claim on T2, read beside its replies [TBV]. Hyman (2010) and Rouder and colleagues (2013) are confirmed and could take records of their own.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it affirms T2.
