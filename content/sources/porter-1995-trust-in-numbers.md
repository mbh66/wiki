Porter, T. M. (1995). *Trust in Numbers: The Pursuit of Objectivity in Science and Public Life*. Princeton University Press, Princeton. Reissued with a new preface (ISBN 9780691208411).

Theodore M. Porter argues that quantification works as a technology of distance [TBV]. Numbers let knowledge reach beyond local communities and the personal trust that holds them, and officials and experts lean on them most where their authority is weakest [TBV]. He traces this in modern administration and science [TBV].

Run 6b read the book through the publisher's description and an essay that discusses it, so the T7 reading is graded [TBV].
