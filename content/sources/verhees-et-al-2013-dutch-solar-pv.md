Verhees, B., Raven, R., Veraart, F., Smith, A. and Kern, F. (2013). The development of solar PV in The Netherlands: a case of survival in unfriendly contexts. *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews* 19, 275-289. Working-paper version: Eindhoven Centre for Innovation Studies 12-04 (2012).

A four-decade history of one technology under protection. The authors follow Dutch solar photovoltaics through five protected spaces, including research programmes, off-grid uses and local solar co-operatives, and show protection being won, lost and rebuilt. The technology survived by shifting between spaces. The study is a historical reconstruction from documents and interviews. The wiki uses it as the clearest long case of protection in practice, and as evidence that survival without empowerment is possible for a technology.
