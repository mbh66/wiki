Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (2025, 24 September). *Planetary Health Check 2025*. Potsdam: PIK.

The check updates the assessment in [[sources/richardson-planetary-boundaries|Earth beyond six of nine planetary boundaries]] (2023). It finds ocean acidification across its boundary, bringing the number breached to seven of nine, while stratospheric ozone and aerosol loading remain within safe limits; the institute cites ozone recovery as evidence that coordinated action works. A 2026 check, reported in September 2026, found all seven breached boundaries at their highest recorded levels. The wiki cites the 2025 check in [[research/what-gdp-leaves-out|What GDP Leaves Out]] for the state of the ecological base that households draw on.
