Gereffi, G., Humphrey, J., & Sturgeon, T. (2005). "The Governance of Global Value Chains." *Review of International Political Economy*, 12(1), 78–104.

The standard typology of how lead firms govern global value chains: market, modular, relational, captive and hierarchical forms, set by the complexity of transactions, the ease of codifying them and the capability of suppliers. The wiki uses it because it treats governance of production as separate from location, the distinction on which the wiki's sense of glocalization depends, and because it shows that relocated production can remain governed from the same center.
