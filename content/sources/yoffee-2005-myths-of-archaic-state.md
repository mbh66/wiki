Yoffee, N. (2005). *Myths of the Archaic State: Evolution of the Earliest Cities, States, and Civilizations*. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge. ISBN 0-521-81837-0 (hardback), 0-521-52156-4 (paperback).

Norman Yoffee, an archaeologist, argues from the record of the first cities and states that the neo-evolutionary ladder of band, tribe, chiefdom and state is a myth [IC]. Reviewers report that he holds the ladder stifled the study of early states, and that he seeks to rebuild social evolutionary theory as a tool for comparing change [IC]. Sequences of social forms, TIMN among them, meet his critique wherever they treat the tribe as a stage (inference). This entry rests on the publisher's description and reviews; the book was not read [TBV].
