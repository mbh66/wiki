Swyngedouw, E. (1997). "Neither Global nor Local: 'Glocalization' and the Politics of Scale." In K. R. Cox (ed.), *Spaces of Globalization: Reasserting the Power of the Local* (pp. 137–166). New York: Guilford Press.

Swyngedouw's first statement of glocalization as a politics of scale: economic organization and state regulation are being rescaled at once, upward to supranational bodies and downward to cities and regions, and the outcome is decided by struggle over who governs at which scale. The wiki reads the chapter as the nearest academic precedent for its own governance sense of the word, alongside his 2004 article.
