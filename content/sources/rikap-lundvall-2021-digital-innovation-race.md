Rikap, C. and Lundvall, B.-Å. (2021). *The Digital Innovation Race: Conceptualizing the Emerging New World Order*. Palgrave Macmillan, Cham. 197 pp. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-030-89443-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-89443-6).

Cecilia Rikap and Bengt-Åke Lundvall argue that the largest technology firms act as intellectual monopolies [TBV]. Knowledge produced across wide networks of universities, firms and users ends up captured by a few corporations, mostly in core countries, and this curtails knowledge as a commons [TBV]. The book sets out degrees of intellectual monopoly and corporate innovation systems over its middle chapters [TBV].

A shorter statement is their article "Big tech, knowledge predation and the implications for development", Innovation and Development 12(3): 389-416 (online 2020, print 2022). Run 7b chose the book as the fuller statement. On step T9 it is the platform-capture critique of open knowledge sourcing.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it dissents on T9.
