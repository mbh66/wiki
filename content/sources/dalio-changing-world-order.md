Dalio, R. (2021). *Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order: Why Nations Succeed and Fail*. New York: Avid Reader Press.

An investor's synthesis: great empires last roughly 250 years, give or take 150, with debt and political cycles of 50 to 100 years inside them, and pass through a six-stage internal order ending in great disorder. Its sequence of reserve-currency empires (Dutch, British, American) parallels the hegemonies in the Cycle of Globalization. The wiki treats it as a practitioner's model; it has not been peer reviewed.
