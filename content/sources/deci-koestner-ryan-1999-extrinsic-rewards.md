Deci, E.L., Koestner, R. and Ryan, R.M. (1999). A meta-analytic review of experiments examining the effects of extrinsic rewards on intrinsic motivation. *Psychological Bulletin* 125(6), 627-668.

The authors pool 128 laboratory experiments and find that tangible rewards tied to doing or completing a task lower people's own wish to do it, while positive feedback tends to raise it. The W2DF register also holds this work. The wiki cites it on monitoring young initiatives: measures that work as rewards per act can wear away the motivation that keeps volunteer work going.
