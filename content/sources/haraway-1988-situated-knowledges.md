Haraway, D. (1988). *Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective*. Feminist Studies 14(3): 575-599.

Donna Haraway argues against the view from nowhere, the ideal of a knower who sees everything from no place [IC]. She holds that objectivity is situated and embodied knowledge, partial and accountable for where it stands [IC].

Run 6b read the essay through scholarly works that discuss it, so its basis is recorded as `review`. On step T7 it speaks to part (a): it treats the placeless view as an ideal of science and an illusion.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T7.
