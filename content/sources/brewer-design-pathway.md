Brewer, J. *The Design Pathway for Regenerating Earth*. Earth Regenerators Press. [Book page](https://earthregenerators.org/the-design-pathway/). The research brief dated it 2023; Joe Brewer's own account gives October 2021 [TBV].

Joe Brewer calls for a planetary network of bioregional learning centres [TBV]. Each would be rooted in its own place and learn from the others, and he frames the work as regeneration through a period of collapse [TBV].

Run 6a read the book through its publisher's page and related pages, so the reading is graded [TBV]. Benjamin Life's [[sources/life-bioregional-economics|bioregional economics]] draws on it, and the BioConomy network has adopted both. No open copy or independent review was found.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it affirms T9.
