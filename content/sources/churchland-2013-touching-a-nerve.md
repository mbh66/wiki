Patricia S. Churchland (2013). *Touching a Nerve: The Self as Brain*. W. W. Norton, New York [IC].

Library catalogues list chapters including Soul Searching, My Heavens and The Conscious Life Examined [IC]. Churchland, a philosopher of mind, argues that the self is the brain [IC]. A review in *Religion Dispatches* reports her view that as neuroscience explains more, there is "less ontological room for the soul to occupy" [IC]. A review in *Perspectives on Science and Christian Faith* reports that chapter 3 explains near-death experiences by low oxygen and the body's own opioids [IC]. The reviews and contents show no discussion of filter theory by name [TBV].

The book was not read for this record; the contents were confirmed in three library catalogues [IC]. Her earlier *Neurophilosophy* (1986), named in the Run 3d brief, was not coded in this run.

The wiki takes from it a philosopher's argument against a mind independent of the brain, which is the view the filter claim needs [TBV].
