Benkler, Y. (2006). *The Wealth of Networks: How Social Production Transforms Markets and Freedom*. Yale University Press, New Haven. xii + 515 pp. Released under a Creative Commons non-commercial share-alike licence [IC].

Yochai Benkler names commons-based peer production: information, knowledge and culture made by large numbers of people who cooperate over networks without firms or market prices to direct them [IC]. He argues that it changes markets and widens personal and political freedom [TBV].

Independent reviews on SSRN and in The Independent Review confirm this reading [IC]. The accounts read show the book working at global scale with no grounding in place (inference). The wiki's page on his earlier essay is [[sources/benkler-coases-penguin|Coase's Penguin]].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
