Verfassungsgerichtshof (2010). *B 639/10 ua*, 10 December 2010 (Unterlangkampfen). VfSlg 19.262/2010.

Following its 2008 Mieders ruling, the Court found no constitutional objection to Tyrol's 2010 amendment that gave municipalities the substance value of Gemeindegut agrarian communities, and held that atypical Gemeindegut requires former municipal ownership. The wiki cites it as part of the official record of how Tyrol's mountain commons and its municipalities divided the value of the land.
