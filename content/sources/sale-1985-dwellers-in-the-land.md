Sale, K. (1985). *Dwellers in the Land: The Bioregional Vision*. Sierra Club Books, San Francisco. Reissued by the University of Georgia Press, Athens, 2000 (ISBN 978-0820322056).

Kirkpatrick Sale carried the bioregional idea into economics, government and settlement [IC]. He argues for self-reliant communities at the scale of their regions, producing and consuming in harmony with the land [TBV]. In his account the best wisdom arises from the land itself, and he is doubtful of a cosmopolitan culture cut loose from place [TBV].

No open copy was found. Run 6a read the book through reviews, including the Kirkus review of 1985, so the reading here is graded [TBV]. The [[glossary/a-e/bioregionalism|bioregionalism]] entry gives the wider tradition.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
