Eskom (2024, 23 August). *Loadshedding suspension continues: 149 days of constant energy supply and R10.60 billion reduction in diesel expenditure achieved*. Media statement.

The statement reports uninterrupted supply since 26 March 2024 and a fall in diesel spending, and credits both to the generation recovery plan Eskom began in March 2023. It is the utility's account of its own performance, so the wiki marks claims resting on it alone as unconfirmed and pairs it with the CSIR's statistics. [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] uses it to date the start of the recovery to before the change of government in June 2024.
