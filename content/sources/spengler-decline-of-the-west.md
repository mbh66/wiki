Spengler, O. (1918 to 1922). *Der Untergang des Abendlandes*. Trans. C. F. Atkinson, *The Decline of the West*. New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 1926 to 1928.

Spengler treats each high culture as an organism of about a thousand years whose late, rigid stage he calls civilization, ending in Caesarism. It is a theory of cultures, and historians have largely rejected its determinism. The wiki lists it in the Cycle of Civilizations lineage and corrects the note that attributed a cycle of empire to it.
