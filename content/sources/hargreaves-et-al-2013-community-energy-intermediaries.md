Hargreaves, T., Hielscher, S., Seyfang, G. and Smith, A. (2013). Grassroots innovations in community energy: the role of intermediaries in niche development. *Global Environmental Change* 23(5), 868-880.

Starting from the observation that community energy groups struggle simply to survive, the authors study the intermediary bodies that gather and pass on lessons between them. Intermediaries helped the niche hold together. Toolkits carried general lessons; the knowledge groups most needed was local and hard to transfer. The wiki takes from it that a forum protecting young initiatives can act as an intermediary, and that it should expect local knowledge to travel by people more than by documents.
