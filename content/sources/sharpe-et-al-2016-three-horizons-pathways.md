Sharpe, B., Hodgson, A., Leicester, G., Lyon, A. and Fazey, I. (2016). Three horizons: a pathways practice for transformation. *Ecology and Society* 21(2): 47.

The peer-reviewed statement of the Three Horizons practice. The authors set out how groups map the first, second and third horizons and use the map to find pathways for change, and they state that the paper draws on their own facilitation. The wiki cites it as an account by practitioners of their own method, and as the source for reading the second horizon as the contested space where protection decisions are made.
