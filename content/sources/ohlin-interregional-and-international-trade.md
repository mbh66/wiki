Ohlin, B. (1933). *Interregional and International Trade*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press.

Ohlin developed Heckscher's argument into a general theory in which trade between regions and between nations follows from differences in factor endowments, and treated location and trade as one subject. The wiki takes from Ohlin the factor-endowment explanation of offshoring to labor-abundant countries, and his treatment of regions and nations in the same frame, which lets a bioregion be read as a trading unit.
