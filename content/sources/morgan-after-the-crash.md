Morgan, T. (2026, November 6). After the Crash: How to prepare for a civilizational shift. *The Leading Edge*.

Written the day after a $2.5 trillion sell-off in technology stocks, the essay holds that "the lower can't see the higher," describes the present system as an isolation economy with three traits (zero-sum, wealth as static and quantifiable, workers valued for spreadsheet skills), and predicts a phase shift that integrates an anomaly, which Morgan names as the Tao. Initiation carries people from the isolation economy to an integration economy that is positive-sum, treats money as a flow and pays people for their gifts. The wiki takes the essay's structure as a lens on the [[disruption/index|decade of disruption]] and restates each move in its own terms: abstraction against place, the flattening [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|S-curve]], the [[glossary/a-e/e-form-emergent|Emergent form]] and [[concepts/bildung|Bildung]].
