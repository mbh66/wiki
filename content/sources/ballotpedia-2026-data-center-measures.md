The article counts the local measures on data centres decided on 3 November 2026. Voters approved 27 measures restricting development and defeated 13 that supported it; the one exception was in Harwood, North Dakota. Ohio held 21 of the 41, and six earlier 2026 measures also favoured limits. The measures dealt with zoning, acreage, megawatts, water use and financial guarantees. The wiki uses Ballotpedia as the record of the votes, because it lists the measures and their results one by one.
