In brief: Symbol use and brain co-evolved [TBV].

How the work is received in its own field is still to be checked [TBV].

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as an evidence work in biological anthropology, with evidence type E3 and claim codes A1, A2, A3, H.


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it dissents on T3; it is silent on T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it qualifies T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|claim grid]] codes its positions on the claim chain: it affirms A2; it qualifies A1 and A3; it dissents from H.
