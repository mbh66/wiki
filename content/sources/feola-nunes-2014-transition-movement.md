Feola, G. and Nunes, R. (2014). Success and failure of grassroots innovations for addressing climate change: the case of the Transition Movement. *Global Environmental Change* 24, 232-250.

One of the few comparative studies of community initiatives that includes ones that stopped. An online survey reached 276 Transition initiatives in 23 countries, active and inactive. Most rated themselves successful, measured by social connection, empowerment and environmental impact. Less successful initiatives may underestimate context and material resources. The authors also find an incubation period in which an initiative consolidates. Success is self-reported. The wiki uses it to qualify the claim that nurturing matters more than money.
