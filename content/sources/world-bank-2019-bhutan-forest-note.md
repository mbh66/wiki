World Bank (2019). *Bhutan Forest Note: Pathways for Sustainable Forest Management and Socio-equitable Economic Development*. Washington, DC: World Bank.

The note reviews forest management and forest-based livelihoods in Bhutan and records the country's payment-for-ecosystem-services agreements. For Namey Nichu in Paro it names the parties (the community watershed group and the downstream hotels and water association) and the facilitators, the Watershed Management Division and the Paro Dzongkhag Forestry Office. The wiki uses it as one of two records, independent of the parties, that establish the Paro scheme.
