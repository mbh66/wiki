Arbesman, S. (2011). "The Life-Spans of Empires." *Historical Methods* 44(3): 127 to 129.

Fitting the durations of 41 empires across more than three millennia, Arbesman finds an exponential distribution with an expected lifetime of about 220 years. The distribution is memoryless: an empire's risk of ending does not depend on its age. The wiki cites it against any fixed cycle length.
