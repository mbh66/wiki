Kostakis, V. and Seitanidis, S. (2026). *Design global, manufacture local: operationalizing post-growth industrial policy through cosmolocalism*. Globalizations, published online 25 April 2026. DOI: [10.1080/14747731.2026.2660493](https://doi.org/10.1080/14747731.2026.2660493).

The authors argue that cosmolocalism may give post-growth industrial policy a working bridge [TBV]. They present it as one component of a broader transformation, and set out the conditions it needs and the risks it runs [TBV].

Run 6a read the article through its abstract and search text, so the reading is graded [TBV]. Vasilis Kostakis co-wrote the design global, manufacture local papers with Michel Bauwens, who is close to the BioConomy network.
