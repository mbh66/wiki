Molina, A., Govers, G., Van den Putte, A., Poesen, J. and Vanacker, V. (2009). "Assessing the reduction of the hydrological connectivity of gully systems through vegetation restoration: field experiments and numerical modelling." *Hydrology and Earth System Sciences*, 13(10), 1823-1836.

Concentrated-flow experiments on nine gullies in the southern Ecuadorian Andes. Gullies infiltrated 60% of inflow when dry and 24% when wet on average; those with more than 50% vegetation cover reached 81% and 34%. The wiki cites it for the mechanism behind gully plugging and leaky weirs, and notes that it measures infiltration at gully scale, leaving catchment yield open.
