W. I. Vernadsky (1945). "The Biosphere and the Noösphere". *American Scientist* 33(1): 1-12. Translated by George Vernadsky.

Vernadsky argues that human scientific thought and social labour have become a geological force, and that this force is turning the biosphere into a new state, the noosphere [IC]. Oldfield and Shaw (2006) quote him calling the noosphere "the last of many stages" in the evolution of the biosphere (p. 10), and note that "latest" renders his Russian better [IC]. He died on 6 January 1945, as the issue went to press [IC]. The wiki's page on [[sources/vernadsky-biosphere|The Biosphere]] (1926) names this essay as the source of its three-sphere reading [MS].

Historians of Russian environmental thought read his noosphere as the biosphere carried on in its full materiality [IC]. The essay names no growth curve and says nothing of an individual drawing on the noosphere (inference) [TBV]. The text was not read for this record; the page reference comes from Oldfield and Shaw [TBV].

The wiki takes from it the noosphere in its first, geochemical sense [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T8.
