Hansen, U.E. and others (2018). Sustainability transitions in developing countries: stocktaking, new contributions and a research agenda. *Environmental Science and Policy* 84, 198-203.

The introduction to a special issue on transitions in developing countries. The editors take stock of the field and argue that its frameworks must be adapted to settings with high inequality, large informal economies and uneven state reach. The wiki cites it beside Wieczorek (2018) on the fit of European-built frameworks to the South.
