International Labour Organization (2018). *Care Work and Care Jobs for the Future of Decent Work*. Geneva: International Labour Office.

The report measures care work from time-use surveys covering 66.9 percent of the world's working-age population. It finds 16.4 billion hours of unpaid care work done every day, equivalent to 2.0 billion people working eight hours without pay, with women doing 76.2 percent of it; valued at an hourly minimum wage, it comes to 9 percent of global GDP. The wiki uses it as the scale of household production, the Tribal form's production, and as the main body of what the wiki calls unrecognized contribution.
