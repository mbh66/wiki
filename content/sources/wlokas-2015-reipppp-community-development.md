Wlokas, H.L. (2015). *A review of the local community development requirements in South Africa's renewable energy procurement programme*. Cape Town: WWF-SA. Report (not peer-reviewed).

By May 2015 the programme had approved 79 wind, solar and hydro projects, each required to contribute to local development through socio-economic and enterprise development, local ownership and local jobs. Wlokas finds that companies mostly planned these duties in isolation, often through private service providers, and that communities were discussed mainly as beneficiaries. The wiki reads the community trusts as financial protection made by the state and run by firms, and cites the report on who sets the terms.
