Immerzeel, W.W., Lutz, A.F., Andrade, M., Bahl, A., Biemans, H., Bolch, T., et al. (2020). "Importance and vulnerability of the world's water towers." *Nature* 577(7790): 364-369.

The study defines 78 water tower units and their downstream basins, ranks them by how much water they store and supply and by how vulnerable they are, and estimates that their decline could affect 1.9 billion people living in mountain areas (0.3 billion) or directly downstream (1.6 billion). The wiki takes from it the physical case that a mountain is a water asset, and notes that the index says nothing about who holds the mountains it ranks.
