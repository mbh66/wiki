Murillo-Rojas, J.-V., Brinckmann, J. and van Essen, M. (2026). Hatching and fledging? A meta-analysis of the performance effects of business incubators. *Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal*. Open access.

The authors pool 39 empirical studies covering 55,219 firms and compare how incubators affect innovation, survival, growth, profitability and employment. Incubation shows clearer gains for innovation and growth than for survival, where effects are weak or inconsistent, and the type of incubator matters. The wiki cites it beside the Schwartz studies on what protection does for ventures, as business evidence that bears on community initiatives only by analogy.
