Geels, F.W. and Schot, J. (2007). Typology of sociotechnical transition pathways. *Research Policy* 36(3), 399-417.

Geels and Schot extend the multi-level perspective by naming distinct pathways a transition can take, depending on the timing and nature of landscape pressure and how ready niche innovations are. The research run that produced the wiki's brief recorded the work by its bibliographic record only, so its detail is cited here without a fresh reading. The wiki uses it to note that a niche's path to wider use depends on conditions outside the niche as much as on its own strength.
