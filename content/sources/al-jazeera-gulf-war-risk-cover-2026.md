Reports that marine insurers canceled war-risk cover for Gulf voyages in the first days after the strikes on Iran of 28 February 2026, which stopped most traffic through the Strait of Hormuz without a physical blockade. The wiki cites it with the Singapore Ministry of Transport's later figures to show how quickly insurance turned a security event into a cost of location.
