Ilya Prigogine (1996; edition cited 1997). *The End of Certainty: Time, Chaos, and the New Laws of Nature*. First published as *La Fin des certitudes: temps, chaos et les lois de la nature* by Éditions Odile Jacob, Paris, 1996, in collaboration with Isabelle Stengers; English edition, The Free Press, New York, 1997.

In brief: Irreversibility and self-organisation are basic to nature [TBV].

How the work is received in its own field is still to be checked [TBV].

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in complexity science, with evidence type E3 and claim codes B3.


The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T3; it qualifies T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it is silent on T9.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|claim grid]] codes its positions on the claim chain: it affirms B3.
