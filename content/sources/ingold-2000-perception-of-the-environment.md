Ingold, T. (2000). *The Perception of the Environment: Essays on Livelihood, Dwelling and Skill*. Routledge, London. Reissued by Routledge (ISBN 9781032052274).

Tim Ingold argues for a dwelling perspective: people come to know their environment by living and moving in it, and skill grows from that engagement [IC]. In the essay "Globes and spheres" he holds that the image of the earth as a globe sets humans apart from the world, while a perspective of the sphere, rooted in dwelling, persists beneath it [TBV].

On step T7, run 7b codes the book as qualifying parts (a) and (b), read through Jason Antrosio's account of the essay. The reading is graded [TBV] until the text is read.
