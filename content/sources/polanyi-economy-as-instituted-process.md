Polanyi, K. (1957). "The Economy as Instituted Process." In K. Polanyi, C. M. Arensberg & H. W. Pearson (eds.), *Trade and Market in the Early Empires* (pp. 243–270). Glencoe, IL: Free Press.

Polanyi's statement of the substantive meaning of economy: provision for material wants embedded in institutions, integrated through reciprocity, redistribution and exchange, each resting on a supporting social structure (symmetry, centricity and markets). Every economy combines them in different weights. The wiki maps reciprocity, redistribution and exchange onto TIME's Tribal, Institutional and Market forms, and notes that Polanyi offers no fourth form.
