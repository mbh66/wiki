Mathie, A. and Peters, B. (2014). "Joint (ad)ventures and (in)credible journeys evaluating innovation: asset-based community development in Ethiopia." *Development in Practice* 24(3): 405-419.

The authors, both of the Coady Institute that ran the program, discuss how several stakeholders with different goals evaluated an open-ended approach whose outcomes are hard to attribute, and argue that the backers' tolerance for risk allowed lessons from failure as well as success. The wiki cites it as peer-reviewed and partner-authored, and as the authors' own account of why the Ethiopian program's results are hard to measure.
