Jódar, J., Martos-Rosillo, S., Custodio, E., Mateos, L., Cabello, J., Casas, J., Salinas-Bonillo, M.J., Martín-Civantos, J.M., González-Ramón, A., Lambán, L.J., et al. (2022). "The Recharge Channels of the Sierra Nevada Range (Spain) and the Peruvian Andes as Ancient Nature-Based Solutions for the Ecological Transition." *Water* 14(19): 3130.

The authors describe careo channels and amunas as one family of practice: wet-season or snowmelt water diverted onto permeable slopes to recharge aquifers that feed springs and rivers in the dry season. In the Bérchules watershed in 2014 to 2015, channel recharge of 3.66 hm³ was 48% of total aquifer recharge (7.62 hm³), and the authors state that the system can double natural recharge rates. The paper is an opinion article that draws on the authors' field studies. The wiki takes from it the measured recharge share for the strongest commons case in the register, and the link between the Iberian and Andean forms of sowing and harvesting water.
