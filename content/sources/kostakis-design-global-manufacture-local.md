Kostakis, V., Niaros, V., Dafermos, G., & Bauwens, M. (2015). "Design Global, Manufacture Local: Exploring the Contours of an Emerging Productive Model." *Futures* 73: 126-135.

The foundational academic paper for the cosmo-local production framework. The authors argue that a new productive model is emerging in which knowledge, designs, and protocols are shared through global digital [[commons|commons]] while physical production stays local, using distributed fabrication technologies and cooperative governance. The paper draws on three cases: the RepRap 3D printer, Open Source Ecology and the Wikispeed car. Read in full for the Anabranch research, it calls peer production a proto-mode that cannot yet sustain itself outside capitalism, and Michael Haupt moved its T9 square from affirming to qualifying on 25 November 2026 [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T1; it is silent on T2; it is silent on T3; it is silent on T4; it is silent on T5; it is silent on T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8; it qualifies T9.
