Grobbelaar, S., Tijssen, R. and Dijksterhuis, M. (2017). University-driven inclusive innovations in the Western Cape of South Africa: towards a research framework of innovation regimes. *African Journal of Science, Technology, Innovation and Development* 9(1), 7-19.

The authors examine 15 inclusive innovation activities run from universities in the Western Cape and propose a framework of innovation regimes to compare them. It is the closest published work found from Stellenbosch's inclusive innovation group to the questions of protection and niches. The wiki cites it as Southern evidence on university-held protected spaces.
