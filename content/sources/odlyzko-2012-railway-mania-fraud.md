Odlyzko, A. (2012). The Railway Mania: fraud, disappointed expectations, and the modern economy. Working paper, University of Minnesota, revised 14 August 2012. https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/mania06.pdf

The paper examines the British railway mania of the mid-1840s, which Odlyzko calls a disaster for most investors. The best-known railway share index fell 64 per cent from the summer of 1845 to the end of 1849. He traces the losses to revenue forecasts that couldn't be met and to fraud, and draws lessons for later booms. The wiki uses it with its companion paper on the 1830s: one mania that paid and one that didn't, both of which left a working railway network behind.
