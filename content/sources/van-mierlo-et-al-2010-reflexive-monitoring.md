van Mierlo, B., Regeer, B., van Amstel, M., Arkesteijn, M., Beekman, V., Bunders, J., de Cock Buning, T., Elzen, B., Hoes, A.-C. and Leeuwis, C. (2010). *Reflexive Monitoring in Action: A Guide for Monitoring System Innovation Projects*. Wageningen and Amsterdam: Wageningen UR and VU University Amsterdam.

A practical guide to monitoring young projects that aim to change a system. The monitor sits inside the project and helps participants reflect on the system they are trying to change and adjust what they do as they go. The method is built for learning during the work and offers no outcome measure of its own. The wiki proposes it as one way to watch a protected initiative without scoring it, and notes that no comparative study has tested that advantage.
