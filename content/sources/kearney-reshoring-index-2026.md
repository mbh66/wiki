Kearney. (2026). *2026 Reshoring Index*. Press release 29 April 2026.

Kearney's annual index compares US manufacturing output with manufactured imports from 14 Asian low-cost countries. The 2026 edition reports the index at minus 86 for 2025 (minus 91 in the press release), still negative, with imports from mainland China down about US\$135 billion and those from 13 other Asian countries up about US\$193 billion, and a March 2026 survey in which 75 percent of executives had switched sourcing away from China to other low-cost countries. Kearney sells supply-chain advice. The wiki uses the index as its measure of realized reshoring, graded as an interested party's.
