Meyerhoff, J. (2010). *Bald Ambition: A Critique of Ken Wilber's Theory of Everything*. Inside the Curtain Press.

Jeff Meyerhoff tests Ken Wilber's twenty tenets against the sources Wilber cites [IC]. On the fifth tenet, that each emergent holon transcends and includes its predecessors, he argues that lower wholes such as atoms and cells often exist outside higher ones, and that human cases often show loss: tribal and village life declines as larger societies form [IC]. He also argues that the holarchy's terms carry value judgements, so its ranking is not neutral [IC]. Chapter 1 is published on *Integral World*, with a foreword to the book by Frank Visser [IC]. The wiki takes it as the main dissent from Wilber's version of C1.
