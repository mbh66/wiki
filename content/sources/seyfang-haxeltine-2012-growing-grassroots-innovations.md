Seyfang, G. and Haxeltine, A. (2012). Growing grassroots innovations: exploring the role of community-based initiatives in governing sustainable energy transitions. *Environment and Planning C: Government and Policy* 30(3), 381-400.

The authors read the Transition Towns movement as a grassroots niche. They find many local projects working together as a niche, and find weaknesses in its niche processes, including how hard it is to pass learning from one project to another. They conclude that strategic niche management needs adapting for civil-society niches. The wiki cites it as evidence that the core model fits community work only in part.
