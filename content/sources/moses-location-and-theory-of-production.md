Moses, L. N. (1958). "Location and the Theory of Production." *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 72(2), 259–272.

Moses showed that when a firm can vary the proportions of its inputs, the optimal location and the optimal input mix must be chosen together, which Weber's model, with fixed proportions, could not capture. The paper completed the merger of location theory with the theory of production. The wiki takes from it the point that automation changes location twice: it lowers the labor share of cost and it changes which sites are least-cost for a given output.
