Bauer, C. J. (2015). "Water Conflicts and Entrenched Governance Problems in Chile's Market Model." *Water Alternatives* 8(2): 147-172.

Bauer finds that the market model gave legal security to private investment and moved water to higher-value uses in some places, at the cost of poor coordination among uses and weak conflict resolution. In the Copiapó valley the state granted paper rights to several times the annual groundwater recharge; mining firms outbid farmers for irrigation rights; and the 2005 reform reached only future grants. The wiki uses it as the clearest record of what follows when a claim on water can be sold away from the land and the place.
