Raven, R., Kern, F., Verhees, B. and Smith, A. (2016). Niche construction and empowerment through socio-political work: a meta-analysis of six low-carbon technology cases. *Environmental Innovation and Societal Transitions* 18, 164-180.

The authors compare solar PV, offshore wind and carbon capture in the United Kingdom and the Netherlands. They note that few studies ask how, and by whom, protective space is made. They treat empowerment as outward socio-political work by advocates, and find that it depended on tying the innovation to the agendas of powerful actors. The six cases are syntheses of earlier retrospective studies by the same team. The wiki takes from it that the terms of protection are political, which bears on who sets them and in public.
