Starosta, G. (2016). "Revisiting the New International Division of Labour Thesis." In G. Charnock & G. Starosta (eds.), *The New International Division of Labour: Global Transformation and Uneven Development*. London: Palgrave Macmillan.

A reassessment of Fröbel, Heinrichs and Kreye forty years on. Starosta reviews the critiques, chief among them the industrial upgrading of East Asian economies into complex, capital-intensive sectors, which the original thesis did not anticipate, and argues for keeping its core insight about the global fragmentation of production. The wiki uses the chapter as its guide to the critical literature on the NIDL.
