MacLeod, M.A. and Emejulu, A. (2014). "Neoliberalism With a Community Face? A Critical Analysis of Asset-Based Community Development in Scotland." *Journal of Community Practice* 22(4): 430-450.

The authors examine how asset-based community development was taken up in Scottish policy and argue that its focus on a community's own strengths fits a politics of state retreat, leaving questions of ownership, inequality and power unasked. The wiki takes from it the main critique of conventional ABCD, and tests a narrower version of it: in the case register, ABCD practice rarely reached the question of who holds a mountain catchment.
