Te Urewera Act 2014 (New Zealand), Public Act 2014 No 51.

The Act declares Te Urewera a legal entity with the rights, powers, duties and liabilities of a legal person, ends its status as a national park, and vests governance in the Te Urewera Board, on which Tūhoe hold the majority. It forms part of the Crown's settlement with Ngāi Tūhoe. The wiki cites it as the first of three Aotearoa New Zealand statutes that made a forest, a river and a mountain legal persons, and as a case where decision rights changed and water was not measured.
