Ronfeldt, D. (2025 to 2026). *Rethinking What "Tribes" and "Networks" Are Good For*, Parts I to IV and continuations. Onward With TIMN (Substack). Part I, "Networks For Hugging Inward vs. Networks For Reaching Outward", is dated 20 October 2025 by a source close to the network [TBV]; Part II, 30 October 2025; Part IV, "Toward TIMN 2.0: Preamble: Bottom Lines Up Front", 9 January 2026 [IC]. The post URLs are on the [[frameworks/timn-framework|TIMN Framework]] page.

David Ronfeldt revisits the two "sideways" forms of his TIMN framework [IC]. He now treats tribes and networks as network-centred forms: tribes reach inward to kin and identity, networks reach outward to purpose and expertise [IC]. He holds that the network form will make its own home realm, probably a commons realm centred on care, and says he never meant civil society to be that home [IC]. He floats renaming the network form exonets or equinets, which would make the acronym TIME, the name of the network's own framework [IC]. Later parts add valences, axes and pulsations without dropping the four-form sequence [TBV]. The series assigns named movements and organisations to forms; the wiki records that it does so and repeats none of the placements.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a canon work in political science, with evidence type E4 and claim codes B3, C1, C2, C3, C4. It speaks of forms of organisation where the claim chain speaks of structures of awareness, so every grid square it holds qualifies.
