May, M. (2014). "Kenyan communities succeed in managing scarce water, where aid projects once foundered." *The World*, PRI, 3 July 2014.

The report describes 18 boreholes and 9 wells serving more than 70,000 people, a road and 162 latrines, and records failed rains, contamination risk at dams, salty borehole water fit only for livestock, and boreholes costing four to five times as much as dams. Most claims are attributed to the association's founder, and the Aga Khan Foundation funded both the series and the program. The wiki cites it as the only account found outside Coady, and records that its counts of roads and water points differ from Coady's.
