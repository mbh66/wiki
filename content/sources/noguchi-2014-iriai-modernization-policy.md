野口俊邦 (2014). 「入会林野近代化政策の総括と展望」 [A review and outlook of iriai forest modernization policy]. *入会林野研究* 34: 5-8.

The article reviews the policy that followed the 1966 law and characterizes it as "入会権の否定と入会林野解体の歴史", a history of denying iriai rights and dismantling iriai forests. The wiki takes from it the scholarly reading of the law's purpose, set beside the Forestry Agency's figures on its results.
