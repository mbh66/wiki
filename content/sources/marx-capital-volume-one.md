Marx, K. (1867). *Das Kapital: Kritik der politischen Ökonomie*, Band I. Hamburg: Otto Meissner. English edition (1887), trans. S. Moore & E. Aveling.

Marx's account of capitalist production includes a sequence of labor processes, from handicraft through manufacture, based on the detailed division of labor in the workshop, to machinofacture, the factory system built on machinery. The wiki reports the sequence as a framework without adopting it, as one candidate for an evolution of production, and notes that it treats later forms as displacing earlier ones, where TIME holds that all forms operate together.
