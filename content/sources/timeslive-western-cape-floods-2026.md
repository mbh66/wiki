TimesLIVE (2026, 11 June). *Western Cape faces R9.1bn flood repair bill as farms, roads and communities count the cost*.

The report gives the provincial cabinet's figures for the May 2026 storms: an estimated R9.1 billion in damage, 231,029 people affected, 22,890 houses damaged and agricultural losses above R5.2 billion. The wiki uses it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as a worked example of repair that GDP will count as growth.
