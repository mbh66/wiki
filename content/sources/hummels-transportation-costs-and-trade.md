Hummels, D. (2007). "Transportation Costs and International Trade in the Second Era of Globalization." *Journal of Economic Perspectives*, 21(3), 131–154.

Hummels measured how transport costs changed after 1950 and found that ocean freight costs, as a share of the value of goods, fell little, while air freight costs fell by an order of magnitude and the speed of delivery became worth more. The wiki uses the paper to correct a common account of hyperglobalization: cheaper shipping did less of the work than tariff cuts, reliability, air freight and falling coordination costs.
