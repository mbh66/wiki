Modelski, G. (1987). *Long Cycles in World Politics*. Seattle: University of Washington Press.

Modelski describes cycles of about 100 to 120 years, each passing through global war, world power, delegitimation and deconcentration, with leadership held in turn by Portugal, the Netherlands, Britain twice and the United States. The wiki cites it alongside Arrighi as a second account of hegemonic succession within the modern world-system.
