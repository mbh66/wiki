Rodrik, D. (2016). "Premature Deindustrialization." *Journal of Economic Growth*, 21(1), 1–33.

Rodrik shows that countries now reach their peak shares of manufacturing employment and output earlier and at lower incomes than early industrializers did, and attributes this to globalization and labor-saving technology. The wiki cites it in the developing-country objection to its own framing: the export-led path that lifted East Asia was already narrowing, and a levelling that closes it further harms countries that have not yet industrialized.
