World Bank (2026). *World Bank Prices 14-Year Spekboom Restoration Outcome Bond in South Africa.* Press release, 23 April 2026.

The release for an outcome bond issued by the World Bank's lending arm, with principal "fully protected by the triple-A credit strength of the World Bank". Priced four weeks after the Cape Water bond, it shows the other model of nature-linked bond: the development bank as issuer, protecting principal, where in the Cape Water bond a development bank is an investor taking a commercial bank's credit risk. The wiki uses it as the comparison case.
