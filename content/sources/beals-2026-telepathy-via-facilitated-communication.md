Katharine Beals (2026). "Telepathy via Facilitated Communication in Profound Autism: A 21st Century Delusion". *Skeptical Inquirer* 50(5), September/October 2026.

The article is part of the issue's feature on the podcast filed as [[sources/dickens-2024-telepathy-tapes|Dickens (2024)]] [IC]. Its title states its claim: that telepathy reported through facilitated communication with profoundly autistic people is a delusion of the present century [IC]. Beals has written on facilitated communication for years at facilitatedcommunication.org, including posts on the podcast and on claims of telepathy, and appeared as an expert on the Science Vs episode on telepathy [IC]. The magazine's site blocks automated reading, so the text was not read; the coding rests on the title and the author's published record [TBV]. No child or participant is named here.

Supporters of the podcast read the same material differently. Marina Weiler and Marjorie Woollacott (2025), writing in *Explore* (DOI 10.1016/j.explore.2025.103271), argue that the nonspeakers' communication warrants consideration in light of earlier parapsychological research [IC].

The wiki takes from it a critic's case, made from the facilitated communication record, against reading the podcast as evidence of telepathy [TBV].
