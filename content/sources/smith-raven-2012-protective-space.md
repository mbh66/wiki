Smith, A. and Raven, R. (2012). What is protective space? Reconsidering niches in transitions to sustainability. *Research Policy* 41(6), 1025-1036.

The central text on protective space. Smith and Raven say effective protection does three things: shielding holds back selection pressure, nurturing builds learning, networks and expectations, and empowerment prepares the innovation to stand outside. Empowerment can make an innovation fit the existing rules or stretch those rules to fit it. Shielding can use spaces that already exist or spaces built on purpose. They call empowerment the least developed property and warn that protection can be captured, or can come to shelter weak work. The wiki builds its brief on this model and on its warnings.
