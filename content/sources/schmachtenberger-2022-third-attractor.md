Daniel Schmachtenberger (2022). *In Search of the Third Attractor (parts 1 and 2)*. Rebel Wisdom, hosted by David Fuller. Part 1, August 2022; part 2, 23 August 2022.

In two conversations with David Fuller, published by Rebel Wisdom in August 2022 (part 1 dated between 15 and 18 August, depending on the platform, and part 2 on 23 August) [IC], Daniel Schmachtenberger describes two attractors toward which current dynamics pull: catastrophe from unchecked rivalry with powerful technology, and dystopia from the control needed to prevent it [IC]. He argues for a [[glossary/t-z/third-attractor|third attractor]], which requires collective sensemaking and choice-making at the scale of present risks [IC]. It is a talk and has not been peer-reviewed; it is treated as an introduction in metacrisis circles [IC]. The wiki takes it as a source for steps C2, C3 and C4, and reads its claims as theory.

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a practice work in metacrisis, with evidence type E4 and claim codes C2, C3, C4.

Glossary: [[glossary/t-z/third-attractor|Third Attractor]].



The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T6; it qualifies T8.
