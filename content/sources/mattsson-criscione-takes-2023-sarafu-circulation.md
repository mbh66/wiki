Mattsson, C. E. S., Criscione, T. and Takes, F. W. (2023). "Circulation of a Digital Community Currency." *Scientific Reports* 13: 5864. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-33184-1

From Sarafu's transaction records, the authors build a flow network of 40,657 accounts, 421,329 transactions and 293.7 million Sarafu, and find circulation highly modular and geographically local, with community institutions acting as hubs and early adopters and women's participation central. The wiki uses it for the circulation evidence on the Sarafu case.
