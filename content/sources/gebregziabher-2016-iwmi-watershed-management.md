Gebregziabher, G., Abera, D.A., Gebresamuel, G., Giordano, M. and Langan, S. (2016). *An assessment of integrated watershed management in Ethiopia*. IWMI Working Paper 170. Colombo, Sri Lanka: International Water Management Institute. 28 pp.

The paper assesses six integrated watershed management programs in Ethiopia. Haile and Gebregziabher (2020) cite it for groundwater at Abreha we Atsbeha rising from a depth of about 50 meters to about 5 meters below the surface. The wiki's earlier reading of the same paper as a 5-meter rise has been revised to match; the paper itself could not be opened in the 9 November 2026 check, so the figure stays [TBV] until it is read directly.
