Kostakis, V., Latoufis, K., Liarokapis, M. and Bauwens, M. (2018). *The convergence of digital commons with local manufacturing from a degrowth perspective: Two illustrative cases*. Journal of Cleaner Production 197(2): 1684-1693. [Publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652616314184).

The authors argue that open designs shared as digital commons, made with local, small-scale manufacturing, suit a degrowth path [TBV]. They point to durable design, making on demand, and shared tools and workshops, and set the argument out through two illustrative cases [TBV].

This research has read the article only through its co-authors' summary in Kostakis, Niaros and Giotitsas (2023), which is no independent account [TBV]. Michel Bauwens, a co-author, is close to the BioConomy network.
