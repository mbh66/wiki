Samuelson, P. A. (2004). "Where Ricardo and Mill Rebut and Confirm Arguments of Mainstream Economists Supporting Globalization." *Journal of Economic Perspectives*, 18(3), 135–146.

Samuelson showed that productivity gains in a trading partner, in a sector where the home country had held its comparative advantage, can permanently lower the home country's welfare, and that free trade's net gain to each country is not guaranteed. The wiki cites the paper as a concession from inside mainstream trade theory, set against the stronger claim that comparative advantage makes every relocation of production a loss.
