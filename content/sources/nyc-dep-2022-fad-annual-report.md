New York City Department of Environmental Protection (2023). *2022 Filtration Avoidance Determination Annual Report*. New York: NYC DEP.

The annual report covers the city's watershed programs for 2022. It states that "During 2022, the WAP did not approve any new WFPs on WOH farms" and that no new conservation easements were conveyed to the state that year. The wiki takes from it the official record that new enrollment of West-of-Hudson farms had stalled three decades into the program.
