陈姝楠 (2025). 「元阳县：一条山路何以撬动全域经济」 [Yuanyang County: how a mountain road lifts the whole county economy]. 上观新闻, 29 December 2025.

The report states that Azheke's village collective tourism company, operating since February 2019, had by 11 October 2025 received 277,000 visitors, earned CNY 7.1356 million and paid 12 rounds of dividends totaling CNY 3.4525 million, an average of CNY 53,116 per household. The wiki cites it, with the Honghe Prefecture government's account of the company's rules, for the Azheke sub-entry of the Hani terraces case.
