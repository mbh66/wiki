Bauwens, M., Kostakis, V. and Pazaitis, A. (2019). *Peer to Peer: The Commons Manifesto*. University of Westminster Press, London. ISBN 978-1-911534-77-8. DOI: [10.16997/book33](https://doi.org/10.16997/book33). [Open access on OAPEN](https://library.oapen.org/bitstream/id/2e3f561d-b1f5-4e7c-8da3-ccab0f00501d/UWP-033-REVISED.pdf).

The book sets out commons-based peer production as a mode of production beyond state and market [TBV]. It describes a cycle in which open contribution, participatory governance and shared outputs build up commons, and it proposes a transition in which the commons comes to lead [TBV].

Michel Bauwens, a co-author, is close to the BioConomy network. Run 5 read the book through Liodaki et al. (2025), and run 6a read one passage; neither found it taking up place-based and globally shared knowledge working together [TBV].
