Roger P. Briggs (2021). *Emerging World: The Evolution of Consciousness and the Future of Humanity*. Atlantic Publishing Group, Ocala, FL. ISBN: 9781620238820.

Roger Briggs, a physicist turned deep-time historian, picks up where his *Journey to Civilization* (2013) ended, at the start of civilisation about 5,000 years ago [MS]. Drawing on Gebser, Merlin Donald and Arthur Young, he names four macro-stages of culture and consciousness: Mimetic (about three million years ago), Magical (one million), Mythic (60,000) and Material (5,000, with the first civilisations). A fifth, Planetary consciousness, is now emerging (ch. 5, p. 166) [TBV]. Nowhere in the book does he place a logistic curve on civilisation. Its one growth graph shows the pace of evolution accelerating up to the present (ch. 6, p. 223) [TBV]. Where he describes living peoples in terms of his stages (ch. 5, p. 168), the wiki repeats none of it [TBV]. It is a trade book; no academic reviews were found [TBV]. The wiki's [[concepts/major-evolutionary-transitions|Major Evolutionary Transitions]] adopts and extends his synthesis, so the grid codes the book as one source among many.

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].
