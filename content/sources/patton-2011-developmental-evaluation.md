Patton, M.Q. (2011). *Developmental Evaluation: Applying Complexity Concepts to Enhance Innovation and Use*. New York: Guilford Press.

Patton sets developmental evaluation apart from formative evaluation, which improves a settled model, and summative evaluation, which judges a finished one. Here the evaluator works with an innovation whose goals may still be changing, and helps it adapt. The wiki cites it with reflexive monitoring as a method suited to young initiatives, and records that its advantage over standard impact metrics rests on design reasoning.
