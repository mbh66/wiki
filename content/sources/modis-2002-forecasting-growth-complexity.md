Theodore Modis (2002). "Forecasting the Growth of Complexity and Change". *Technological Forecasting and Social Change* 69(4): 377-404. DOI: [10.1016/S0040-1625(01)00172-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0040-1625(01)00172-X).

Modis, a physicist and founder of the consultancy Growth Dynamics, gathers lists of major milestones from the Big Bang to the present compiled by several authors [IC]. He treats the shrinking intervals between milestones as a measure of rising complexity, and fits a logistic curve to the series [IC]. On that fit, the world stands near the middle of the curve, and the rate of change is about to stop accelerating [IC].

Modis revisited the forecast in 2020, in *The 21st Century Singularity and Global Futures*, and reported that the slowdown he expected had begun [IC]. In the same volume Andrey Korotayev fits hyperbolic equations to similar milestone series and reads them as accelerating toward a singularity in the twenty-first century [IC]. Vaclav Smil (2019) warns against placing a system on its curve from how well past data fit [TBV].

The wiki takes from it the closest published fit of a logistic curve to the long history of complexity, which the paper does not tie to any change in consciousness [IC].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it qualifies T7; it qualifies T8.
