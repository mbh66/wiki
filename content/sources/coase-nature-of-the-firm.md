Coase, R. H. (1937). "The Nature of the Firm." *Economica*, 4(16), 386–405.

Coase asked why firms exist if markets coordinate production through prices, and answered that using the market has costs, so activity is organized inside a firm wherever internal direction costs less. The boundary between firm and market moves as those costs change. The wiki sets Coase's firm and market against TIME's Institutional and Market forms, and reads Benkler's peer production as a third mode that Coase's framework makes visible.
