Scherr, S.J., Granados, B., Reed, J., Ros-Tonen, M., & Sunderland, T. (2026). A home for integrated landscape research and practice: introducing CABI Multifunctional Landscapes. *CABI Multifunctional Landscapes*, 1(1). https://doi.org/10.1079/cabimfl.2026.0001

The editorial introduces a gold open-access journal that CABI launched in April 2026 with 1000 Landscapes for 1 Billion People as its primary partner. CABI's announcement describes the journal as a platform for critical dialogue on how integrated approaches can reconcile community well-being and economic resilience with environmental sustainability, and invites research articles, case studies, policy insights and reflections on initiatives that did not succeed. The wiki treats the journal as the main peer-reviewed venue for [[glossary/f-l/integrated-landscape-management|integrated landscape management]] and follows new work through its [table-of-contents feed](https://www.cabidigitallibrary.org/action/showFeed?type=etoc&feed=rss&jc=cabimfl). This note draws on CABI's launch announcement; the editorial's full text has not yet been reviewed for the wiki.
