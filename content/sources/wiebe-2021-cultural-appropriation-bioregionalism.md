Wiebe, J. (2021). *Cultural Appropriation in Bioregionalism and the Need for a Decolonial Ethics of Place*. Journal of Religious Ethics 49(1): 138-158. DOI: [10.1111/jore.12342](https://doi.org/10.1111/jore.12342).

Joseph Wiebe argues that bioregionalism's ideal of becoming native to a place appropriates Indigenous attachment to land, and so erases Indigenous presence [IC]. He calls for a decolonial ethics of place [IC].

Hubbard et al. (2023) in Geography Compass and Wearne et al. (2023) in People and Nature discuss the article [IC]. The research brief said Wiebe cites Kirkpatrick Sale; the passages read do not confirm it [TBV]. On step T9, run 7b codes the article as an independent critic of bioregionalism on parts (b) and (c). The [[glossary/a-e/bioregionalism|bioregionalism]] entry gives the wider tradition.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
