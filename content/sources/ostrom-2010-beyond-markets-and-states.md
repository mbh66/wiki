Ostrom, E. (2010). *Beyond Markets and States: Polycentric Governance of Complex Economic Systems*. American Economic Review 100(3): 641-672. DOI: [10.1257/aer.100.3.641](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.100.3.641). An earlier version was her Nobel Prize lecture of 8 December 2009 [IC].

Elinor Ostrom reviews decades of field and laboratory work on common-pool resources [IC]. She argues that complex resource systems are often governed well by polycentric arrangements: many centres of decision at several scales, with rules fitted to local conditions [TBV]. She restates the design principles drawn from long-lived commons [IC].

The wiki's page on her earlier book is [[sources/ostrom-governing-commons|Governing the Commons]].
