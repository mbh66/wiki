Johannesburg Stock Exchange (2026). *JSE Welcomes Africa's First Nature-Linked Performance-Based Bond.* 17 April 2026.

The exchange's listing announcement for the R2.5 billion FirstRand Bank note. It names IFC, FSD Africa Investments and Aluwani Capital Partners as co-anchor investors and the FirstRand Foundation as anchor outcomes-based funder, and quotes FirstRand's Group Treasurer that "The IFC's investment was essential to achieve the target bond size". The wiki treats it as the official record of the listing and of the investor roles.
