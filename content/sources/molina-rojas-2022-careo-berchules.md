Molina Rojas, A.M., González Ramón, A., Barberá, J.A., Peregrina del Río, M., Villagómez Antequera, A.B., Díaz Puga, M.Á. and Martos Rosillo, S. (2022). "Caracterización físico-química e isotópica del agua subterránea relacionada con acequias de careo. Cuenca del río Bérchules (Sierra Nevada, Sur de España)." *Geogaceta* 71: 39-42.

The authors sampled springs across the Bérchules basin in 2019 and found a homogenizing effect in groundwater composition: samples from high elevations closely resemble those from middle elevations on the right bank, which they attribute to mixing with water infiltrated by the careo channels. The wiki takes from it independent evidence that the careo commons shapes the basin's groundwater.
