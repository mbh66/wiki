Papadimitropoulos, V. (2020). *The Commons: Economic Alternatives in the Digital Age*. University of Westminster Press, London, Critical Digital and Social Media Studies series. 272 pp. [Open access on OAPEN](https://library.oapen.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.12657/42987/the-commons.pdf).

Vangelis Papadimitropoulos surveys the politics of the commons and sorts it into liberal, reformist and anti-capitalist variants [TBV]. He treats cosmolocalism and glocal commons as ways of linking digital commons to material production, one variant among several [TBV].

Run 7b chose this book as the fullest open statement of his work on the question. The passages read did not confirm, as stated, a gap between designs that travel and the materials and skills that do not [TBV]. He co-wrote Liodaki et al. (2025) and draws on the P2P Lab and the work of Michel Bauwens, who is close to the BioConomy network.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T9.
