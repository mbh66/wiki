Ramos-Mejía, M., Franco-Garcia, M.L. and Jauregui-Becker, J.M. (2018). Sustainability transitions in the developing world: challenges of socio-technical transformations unfolding in contexts of poverty. *Environmental Science and Policy* 84, 217-223.

The authors ask what changes when a socio-technical transformation takes place among people living in poverty, where basic services are missing and informal arrangements carry much of daily life. They argue that transitions theory has to account for these conditions. The wiki cites it on protection where the formal regime does not reach.
