Ky Dickens (2024). *The Telepathy Tapes*. The Telepathy Tapes (audio podcast). [Link](https://thetelepathytapes.com).

Ky Dickens, a documentary filmmaker, presents in this podcast claims that some nonspeaking autistic people communicate by telepathy [IC]. The evidence offered is tests that her crew filmed in participants' homes, with interviews with families and with researchers who support the claims [IC]. The first season ran from 9 September to 23 December 2024, and later episodes have followed [IC]. Most participants spell by pointing to letters on a board or keyboard with a communication partner, in methods related to facilitated communication, such as Spelling to Communicate and the Rapid Prompting Method [IC].

The podcast reached a very wide audience [IC]. Professional bodies, among them the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association, hold that facilitated communication is discredited and do not recommend the Rapid Prompting Method, and critics including Stuart Vyse argue that the messages come from the facilitators [IC]. The SPR *Psi Encyclopedia* records researchers calling for a more careful view of motor-supported typing [IC]. This page names no participant. It is an audio podcast, so its type is `website`.

The wiki records the podcast as testimony on step T2, beside the published responses to it. Anomalous experience research is recorded on the Anabranch layer, never adopted.
