Forestry Agency of Japan (2025). *入会林野整備の現状と生産森林組合の組織変更等の状況* [Status of iriai forest reorganization and changes in production forest associations]. 14 March 2025.

The agency reports that about 1.85 million hectares of iriai forest existed when the 1966 modernization law was enacted. By FY2023, 0.58 million hectares had been reorganized under the law and 0.89 million including voluntary reorganization, with the annual pace peaking in FY1974; a survey of owners confirmed only 0.45 million hectares as remaining iriai. It attributes the slowdown to aging iriai groups and a depressed forestry sector. The wiki takes from it the official measure of how far Japan's mountain commons were converted into ownership.
