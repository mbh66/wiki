IOL Business Report (2022, 24 February). *Sasria gets R22bn shot in the arm after civil unrest brought the insurer to its knees*.

The report covers the 2022 Budget allocation of R22 billion to Sasria and National Treasury's estimate of about R32 billion in claims from the looting of July 2021. The wiki cites it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as evidence that much of the unrest's cost fell on the state, which recapitalized its own insurer. Journalism, cited for a recent event only.
