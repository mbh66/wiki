World Trade Organization. (2026, March). *Global Trade Outlook and Statistics*. Geneva: WTO.

The WTO's outlook reports that world trade in goods and services grew about 4.7 percent in 2025 against world GDP growth of 2.9 percent, that services trade reached US\$9.56 trillion, and that trade in AI-enabling goods rose 21.9 percent to US\$4.18 trillion; it forecasts trade and GDP growth of about 2.7 and 2.8 percent for 2026. The wiki cites it as the strongest official evidence that trade has not retrenched, which the glocalization framing has to answer.
