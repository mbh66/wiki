Te Awa Tupua (Whanganui River Claims Settlement) Act 2017 (New Zealand), Public Act 2017 No 7.

The Act recognizes Te Awa Tupua, the Whanganui River from the mountains to the sea, as a legal person, appoints Te Pou Tupua (one iwi nominee and one Crown nominee) as its human face, and vests the Crown-owned riverbed in it. As read in the brief, it gives Te Awa Tupua no rights to use water, and resource consents to take water do not need Te Pou Tupua's consent. The wiki cites it as the clearest case where legal personhood changed standing and governance and left water rights where they were.
