Hernández Asensio, R. (2013). *El Parque de la Papa de Cusco*. Santiago: RIMISP.

The study, written for RIMISP's program on rural territorial development, examines how the Potato Park's communities and the Asociación ANDES organize governance, enterprise and conservation, and discusses the difficulty of reproducing the model elsewhere. As read through the second enrichment run, it includes critical observations alongside its description. The wiki uses it as an account independent of the park's partners.
