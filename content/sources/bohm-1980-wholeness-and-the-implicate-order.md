David Bohm (1980). *Wholeness and the Implicate Order*. Routledge & Kegan Paul.

In brief: An enfolded order underlies matter and mind [TBV].

How the work is received in its own field is still to be checked [TBV].

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in physics, with evidence type E4 and claim codes H, B3.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it is silent on T5; it qualifies T6; it is silent on T7; it is silent on T8.
