Fröbel, F., Heinrichs, J., & Kreye, O. (1980). *The New International Division of Labour: Structural Unemployment in Industrialised Countries and Industrialisation in Developing Countries*. Trans. P. Burgess. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. (Original work published 1977.)

The study that named the new international division of labor: the relocation of simplified manufacturing from industrial countries to world-market factories and export processing zones in developing ones. It names three preconditions, a worldwide reserve of low-paid labor, the subdivision of production into simple tasks, and transport and communication technology that made dispersed production manageable. The wiki tests each precondition against the present, and finds the first weakened in China while the other two hold.
