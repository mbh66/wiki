Heckscher, E. F. (1919). "Utrikeshandelns verkan på inkomstfördelningen" [The Effect of Foreign Trade on the Distribution of Income]. *Ekonomisk Tidskrift*, 21, 497–512.

The paper in which Heckscher argued that countries trade because they differ in their endowments of factors of production, and that trade changes the incomes of those factors. With Ohlin's later work it became the Heckscher-Ohlin model. The wiki uses it to correct a common blend: a cheap-labor country exporting labor-intensive goods is a factor-endowment story, which is a different theory from Ricardo's comparative advantage.
