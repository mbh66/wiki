Te Ture Whakatupua mō Te Kāhui Tupua 2025/Taranaki Maunga Collective Redress Act 2025 (New Zealand), Public Act 2025 No 1.

The Act declares Te Kāhui Tupua, comprising Taranaki Maunga and the surrounding peaks, a legal person (section 18), vests the former Egmont National Park in it, and creates Te Tōpuni Kōkōrangi, with four iwi and four Crown-appointed members, to act for it. Public access continues. The wiki cites it as the first mountain to become a legal person under these settlements; its outcomes are too recent to assess.
