IOL (2024, 14 February). *How South Africa's load-shedding crisis escalated over 17 years*.

The article compiles yearly load-shedding figures from CSIR statistics, from 176 GWh shed in 2007 to the record of 2023. The wiki uses its figures for the plateau years in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]]. The data are the CSIR's; the article is journalism, cited for the compilation.
