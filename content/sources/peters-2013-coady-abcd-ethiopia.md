Peters, B. (comp.) (2013). *Applying an Asset-Based Community-Driven Development Approach in Ethiopia, 2003-2011: Final Internal Evaluation Report*. Antigonish: Coady International Institute.

The evaluation covers the program run with Oxfam Canada, Hundee, KMG Ethiopia and Agri-Service Ethiopia, which grew from five groups in 2003 to 24 in 2011. The groups drew physical asset maps that included natural resources, and a comparison of the 2008 and 2011 maps "did not reveal many changes in the natural resource base or physical infrastructure" (p. 19). It is an internal evaluation by the program's own institute. The wiki takes from it the best-documented case of ABCD practice that mapped land and water as assets, and the evidence that mapping them did not by itself change who governed them.
