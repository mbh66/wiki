Von Thaden, J.J., Binnqüist-Cervantes, G., Perevochtchikova, M. and Clark-Tapia, R. (2025). "Deforestation dynamics post-payment for ecosystem services in Sierra Juárez, Oaxaca, Mexico." *Regional Environmental Change* 25: 22.

The study grouped parcels enrolled in Mexico's payments for hydrological services by the time since payments ended (up to 5, 5 to 10, and 10 or more years) and compared each group with controls. Parcels up to ten years out showed no significant difference; parcels ten or more years out had significantly higher deforestation. Nearly 70% of enrolled parcels lay in areas of low to medium deforestation risk. The wiki takes from it the clearest evidence in the register that a payment's effect fades after the money stops, set beside community forest institutions that persisted.
