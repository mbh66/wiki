Dudley, N., Baker, C., Chatterton, P., Ferwerda, W.H., Gutierrez, V., & Madgwick, J. (2021). *The 4 Returns Framework for Landscape Restoration.* UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration report. Commonland, Wetlands International, Landscape Finance Lab and IUCN Commission on Ecosystem Management.

The report presents the framework [[ferwerda-willem|Willem Ferwerda]] developed from 2012: a restored landscape should deliver a return of inspiration, a social return, a natural return and a financial return within one generation, planned across natural, combined and economic zones over twenty years. According to the 1000 Landscapes guide, it also incorporates the five elements of [[glossary/f-l/integrated-landscape-management|integrated landscape management]]. The wiki reads the four returns alongside its own [[regenerative|regenerative]] value vocabulary. This note draws on the 1000L guide and Scherr et al. (2026); the report's full text has not yet been reviewed for the wiki.
