Christopher M. Bache (2019). *LSD and the Mind of the Universe: Diamonds from Heaven*. Park Street Press, Rochester, Vermont; foreword by Ervin Laszlo.

Christopher Bache, a philosopher of religion, records the insights of 73 high-dose LSD sessions over twenty years, conducted on protocols established by Stanislav Grof [IC]. He reports a deepening communion with what he calls the mind of the universe, and a coming transformation of the human species as a whole [TBV]. The book is testimony: one person's systematic first-person record, with no controlled study behind it. Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* draws on it for several steps. The wiki takes it as testimony (E5) for the claims that a new collective structure is emerging and that consciousness is fundamental.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a context work in philosophy of religion, with evidence type E5 and claim codes B3, C2, C3, H.
