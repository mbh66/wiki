Ralf W. Schlosser, Susan Balandin, Bronwyn Hemsley, Teresa Iacono, Paul Probst and Stephen von Tetzchner (2014). "Facilitated Communication and Authorship: A Systematic Review". *Augmentative and Alternative Communication* 30(4): 359-368. DOI: [10.3109/07434618.2014.971490](https://doi.org/10.3109/07434618.2014.971490).

The International Society for Augmentative and Alternative Communication formed an ad hoc committee to bring together the evidence on whether messages produced through facilitated communication (FC) come from the person with a disability [IC]. The review reports that controlled studies give "unequivocal evidence for facilitator control" over the messages [IC]. The Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists and the Association for Science in Autism Treatment cite it in their guidance [IC]. Advocates of FC dispute the reach of the controlled studies; their published responses were not read for this record [TBV].

The review says nothing about telepathy [IC]. It bears on T2 because the telepathy claims in *The Telepathy Tapes* rest on messages produced through assisted typing, and the review names an ordinary author for such messages (inference) [TBV]. The record names no participant.

The wiki takes from it the authorship evidence beneath the nonspeakers material, coded on the same basis as the ASHA statement [TBV].
