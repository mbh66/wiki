Allen, J. J. and Smith, S. M. (2023). "Market-Oriented Solutions for Groundwater Commons through Collective-Action." *Environmental Research Letters* 18: 045006. https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/acc8ec

From interviews with stakeholders and local hydrological and farm data, the authors explain why San Luis Valley irrigators chose a pumping fee and Kansas irrigators a uniform quota, and argue that local flexibility in collective action matters more than the instrument chosen. They record that the San Luis Valley fee initially cut water use by a third, but that most gains in aquifer storage were lost in the 2018 drought. The wiki takes from it that pricing one's own use can cut pumping without restoring the resource.
