Smith, A. and Stirling, A. (2010). The politics of social-ecological resilience and sustainable socio-technical transitions. *Ecology and Society* 15(1): 11.

Smith and Stirling compare resilience and transitions research and find both inclined to narrow governance to expert framings of what the system is and what change it needs. They argue for opening up that governance to plural views. The wiki takes from it the case for setting and recording the terms of protection in public, where more than one framing can be heard.
