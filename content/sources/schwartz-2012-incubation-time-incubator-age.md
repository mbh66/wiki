Schwartz, M. (2012). Incubation time, incubator age, and firm survival after graduation. *International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Innovation Management* 15(1/2), 108-130. Discussion-paper version: IWH Discussion Paper 14/2008.

Using survival models on 149 graduate firms, Schwartz finds that both the time a firm spent in an incubator and the incubator's own age lower its survival after leaving. He reads the result as support for strict limits on incubation time. It is the nearest evidence found on whether a time-bound rule for exposure helps, and it concerns firms. The wiki cites it on the exit question in [[research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives|Protective Space for Young Initiatives]].
