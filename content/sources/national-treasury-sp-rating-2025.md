National Treasury (2025, 14 November). *Treasury responds to rating action by S&P Global Ratings*. Media statement.

The statement reports the upgrade of South Africa's long-term foreign currency rating from BB- to BB, the first upgrade by a major agency in over 16 years, and quotes the agency's reference to "a reduction in contingent liabilities tied to performance improvements" at Eskom. The wiki cites it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] as a recovery marker of the Government of National Unity period.
