Ophuls, W. (2012). *Immoderate Greatness: Why Civilizations Fail*. North Charleston, SC: CreateSpace.

Ophuls names six factors in civilizational failure: ecological exhaustion, exponential growth, expedited entropy, excessive complexity, moral decay and practical failure. His moral-decay chapter is largely a synthesis of Glubb's ages. The book is self-published and was not peer reviewed. The wiki lists it in the Cycle of Civilizations lineage and has not yet quoted it, pending a check of page numbers against the printed text.
