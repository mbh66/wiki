Verwaltungsgerichtshof (2011). *2010/07/0091*, 30 June 2011.

The Administrative Court held that the substance value of Gemeindegut belongs to the municipality as its share in the agrarian community, and in individual cases reached different results on which communities count as atypical Gemeindegut. The wiki cites it with the Constitutional Court's rulings as the official record of the Tyrolean dispute.
