Teschke, B. (2003). *The Myth of 1648: Class, Geopolitics and the Making of Modern International Relations*. London: Verso.

Teschke reads the Westphalian settlement as a confirmation of dynastic, absolutist rule and places the origin of a system of modern sovereign states later, in the spread of capitalist property relations from Britain. With Osiander, the book is the main scholarly caveat on the 1648 start of the Cycle of Globalization. The wiki keeps 1648 as a political anchor and notes that economic globalization gathers pace later, in the model's third phase.
