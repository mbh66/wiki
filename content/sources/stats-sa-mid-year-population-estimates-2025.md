Statistics South Africa (2025, 28 July). *Mid-year population estimates 2025*. Statistical release P0302. Pretoria: Stats SA.

The release puts the mid-2025 population at 63.1 million, life expectancy at birth at 66.9 years and the number of people living with HIV at 8.15 million. The wiki uses it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] for the latest health readings and to note the gap between this estimate and the United Nations figure carried in the World Bank series.
