National Treasury (2025, 24 October). *South Africa exiting the FATF greylist*. Media statement.

The statement announces the exit on 24 October 2025, after South Africa completed the action plan agreed when it was grey-listed in February 2023. It describes the work as recovery from "the weakening of key law enforcement and other institutions during the state capture era." The wiki cites it in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] to date one of the recovery markers and to show that the work began before June 2024. It is the government's own statement.
