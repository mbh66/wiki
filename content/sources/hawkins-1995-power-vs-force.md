David R. Hawkins (1995; edition cited 2014). *Power vs. Force*. [TBV]; 2014 Hay House [TBV].

David R. Hawkins, a psychiatrist, presents a logarithmic scale of levels of consciousness from 1 to 1,000, with shame near the bottom and enlightenment at the top, and claims that [[glossary/a-e/applied-kinesiology|applied kinesiology]] (muscle testing) can read the level of any statement, work or person [IC]. Double-blind tests of applied kinesiology (Kenney and colleagues, 1988; Schwartz and colleagues, 2014) found its results no better than chance [IC]. The book calibrates nations and groups; the wiki records that it does so and repeats none of the placements. The grid gives it no position.

The work places nations, groups or cultures on its scale. The library records that it does so and repeats none of the placements.

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a practice work in popular psychology, with evidence type E4 and claim codes B1, C2, H.

Glossary: [[glossary/a-e/applied-kinesiology|Applied Kinesiology]].
