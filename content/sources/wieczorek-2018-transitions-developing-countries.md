Wieczorek, A.J. (2018). Sustainability transitions in developing countries: major insights and their implications for research and policy. *Environmental Science and Policy* 84, 204-216.

Wieczorek draws together transitions research on developing countries under themes of niche formation, experiments, regime stability, transnational links and power. Her review suggests regimes there are often less uniform than in Europe, and that niches may need a degree of regime stability to develop. The wiki uses it as the main review behind the finding that frameworks built on European cases need adapting for Southern settings.
