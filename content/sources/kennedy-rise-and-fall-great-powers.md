Kennedy, P. (1987). *The Rise and Fall of the Great Powers: Economic Change and Military Conflict from 1500 to 2000*. New York: Random House.

Great powers decline when their military commitments outgrow the economic base that pays for them. The theory is not cyclical, and it supplies a mechanism for the late phases of cyclical models. The wiki cites it on the Cycle of Civilizations page.
