Luigi Fantappiè (1942). *Principi di una teoria unitaria del mondo fisico e biologico*. Rome; presented to the Accademia d'Italia, 30 October 1942; later edition Di Renzo Editore [TBV].

The mathematician Luigi Fantappiè presented his theory to the Accademia d'Italia on 30 October 1942 [IC]. He argued that the physical world is governed by forward-in-time solutions and the law of entropy, while life is governed by backward-in-time solutions and a symmetrical law he named syntropy, a tendency toward convergence [IC]. The idea sits outside mainstream physics; it has been carried forward mainly by Ulisse Di Corpo and Antonella Vannini [IC]. Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* cites syntropy for its claim that complexity has a direction. The wiki takes it as an early statement of that claim, drawn from mathematics.
