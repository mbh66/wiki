Swyngedouw, E. (2004). "Globalisation or 'Glocalisation'? Networks, Territories and Rescaling." *Cambridge Review of International Affairs*, 17(1), 25–48.

A geographer's reading of glocalisation as a rescaling. Swyngedouw treats scales as made by history and contest, and describes regulation and economic organization moving at once toward supranational institutions and toward cities and regions, with the national scale losing ground in both directions. The wiki takes from him the point that scale is decided by who governs it, which is the test this site applies to its own use of the word: a glocal economy is one in which the place holds governance over what it produces and keeps.
