Danckwerts, B. (2025). Another Six Months of the Campaign to Bring back the Rains. *Biotic Regulation and Biotic Pump*, guest post, 25 October 2025.

Danckwerts reports that a village whose headman banned tree cutting built no stoves after a demonstration, that one school in Choma District built a school-lunch stove, and that he will no longer seek political endorsement. He argues the science can follow the restoration work. Makarieva adds a note on Elinor Ostrom's design principles for the commons. The wiki cites it for the gap between rules and provision, and for the Ostrom mapping.
