Reshoring Initiative. (2025, 9 June). *Reshoring Initiative 2024 Annual Report*.

The advocacy group's count of announced US manufacturing jobs: 244,000 in 2024 from reshoring and foreign direct investment combined, 88 percent of them in high or medium-high technology sectors. The figures count announcements. The wiki sets them against Kearney's measure of realized imports to show that announcements outrun relocations, and labels the source as an advocate for reshoring.
