Schwartz, M. (2009). Beyond incubation: an analysis of firm survival and exit dynamics in the post-graduation period. *Journal of Technology Transfer* 34(4), 403-421.

Schwartz studies what happens to firms after they leave five German business incubators. Graduation brings an immediate fall in survival that lasts up to three years, exit patterns differ between incubators, and performance during incubation signals the risk of closure afterwards. The wiki reads this as evidence that the moment of exposure is itself a hazard to plan for, while noting that the firms are businesses and the finding carries to community initiatives only by analogy.
