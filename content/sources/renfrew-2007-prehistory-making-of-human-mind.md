Renfrew, C. (2007). *Prehistory: The Making of the Human Mind*. Weidenfeld and Nicolson, London.

Colin Renfrew poses the sapient paradox: modern humans appeared long before the great changes in their ways of life [IC]. He ties a change in modes of thought to sedentism and the first settled communities, and reads it as a widening of material engagement [IC]. In his account the earliest symbols keep material referents, such as measures of weight and value [TBV].

Reviews in International Socialism and elsewhere confirm this reading [IC]. On step T7, run 7b codes the book as the only work read that places a change in thought in the Neolithic. It qualifies part (a), since it grounds the new symbols in material things. No critical reply to it was found. His 1998 chapter on external symbolic storage was not read.

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it is silent on T2; it qualifies T7.
