Weick, K. E., Sutcliffe, K. M. and Obstfeld, D. (2005). Organizing and the process of sensemaking. *Organization Science* 16(4): 409-421. https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.1050.0133

The authors take stock of a decade of sensemaking research and argue that organising and sensemaking are the same process seen from two sides. They call for the concept to become more future-oriented, more tied to action and more concerned with how people give sense to others as well as make it. The wiki uses the paper's definition in its glossary entry, because it is short enough to apply to a community meeting as readily as to a firm.
