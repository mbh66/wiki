Julian Jaynes (1976). *The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind*. Houghton Mifflin.

In brief: Introspective consciousness arose about 3000 years ago [IC].

How its field receives it: Block (1981) objected; Jaynes Society replies; specialist rejection of the dating [TBV].

The work is on Michael Haupt's reading for the evolution of consciousness [MS].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] codes it as a canon work in psychology, with evidence type E4 and claim codes A2, A3, C1.
