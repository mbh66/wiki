International Federation of Robotics. (2026, 8 April). "Robot Density Surges in Europe, Asia and Americas." Press release. Frankfurt: IFR.

The IFR's 2026 release on robot density in manufacturing gives 1,220 robots per 10,000 employees in the Republic of Korea, 449 in Germany and 307 in the United States, and 166 for China, based on updated employment data from China's National Bureau of Statistics. Its *World Robotics 2025* report counted about 542,000 robots installed worldwide in 2024, 54 percent in China. The IFR is the industry's federation. The wiki uses the figures for the substitution of capital for labor, and notes that China's figure is not comparable with the previous edition.
