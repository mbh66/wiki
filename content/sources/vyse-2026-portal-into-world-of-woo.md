Stuart Vyse (2026). "The Telepathy Tapes: A Portal into a World of Woo". *Skeptical Inquirer* 50(5), September/October 2026. Cover story. [Author's note](https://stuartvyse.com/2026/08/18/my-first-cover-story-my-csicon-talk/).

Vyse reports on the second season of the podcast filed as [[sources/dickens-2024-telepathy-tapes|Dickens (2024)]] [IC]. On his own site he describes the season as one where the guardrails come off and the podcast endorses a wide range of paranormal ideas, and he notes that it is his first cover story for the magazine [IC]. The season's own episode listings show it widening from telepathy among nonspeakers to near-death experiences and other claims about consciousness [IC]. The magazine's site blocks automated reading, so the article itself was not read [TBV].

The piece follows his column on the first season, filed as [[sources/vyse-2025-telepathy-tapes|Vyse (2025)]] [IC]. Supporters of the podcast, among them Marina Weiler and Marjorie Woollacott (2025) in *Explore*, read the same material as evidence that deserves consideration [IC].

The wiki takes from it a critic's account of the second season, which carries the podcast's claims beyond telepathy [TBV].

The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] codes its positions: it dissents on T2.
