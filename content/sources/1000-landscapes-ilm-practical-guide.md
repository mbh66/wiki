1000 Landscapes for 1 Billion People. (2022). *A Practical Guide to Integrated Landscape Management* (Version 1.0). Washington, DC: EcoAgriculture Partners, on behalf of 1000 Landscapes for 1 Billion People. Licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

The guide sets out a generic, locally adaptable process for [[glossary/f-l/integrated-landscape-management|integrated landscape management]] in five elements (Landscape Partnership, Shared Understanding, Vision and Planning, Taking Action, Impact and Learning), each with two to four concrete outputs. It synthesizes earlier work including *The Little Sustainable Landscapes Book*, the Sayer et al. ten principles, Commonland's 4 Returns framework and the LandScale assessment method, and it expects the full cycle to take at least twenty years. The wiki takes from it the process sequence a [[biohub|BioHub]] cohort can use for convening, and reads its landscape investment portfolio against the BioConomy's [[tenderable-services-portfolio|tenderable services portfolio]].
