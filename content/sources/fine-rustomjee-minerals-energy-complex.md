Fine, B., and Rustomjee, Z. (1996). *The Political Economy of South Africa: From Minerals-Energy Complex to Industrialisation*. London: Hurst.

Fine and Rustomjee describe a minerals-energy complex: mining and cheap coal-fired electricity, with the heavy industry and finance organized around them, which shaped state policy and investment through the twentieth century. They argue that this core persisted through apartheid's import-substitution phase and held back diversification into other manufacturing. The wiki reads the complex as South Africa's form of [[throughput-economics|throughput economics]] in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]]. The book was not read directly for that page; its argument enters through the research report and secondary accounts.
