FSD Africa (2026). *Behind the Investment: Africa's First Nature-Linked Outcomes Bond.* 26 May 2026.

FSD Africa's write-up of its investment arm's R234 million ticket. It describes the bond's payment logic (noteholders receive "a base return plus a success premium"; outcome funders "only pay when results are achieved"), names hectares of invasive plants cleared as the payment metric, and gives three purposes for anchoring: proving the structure, drawing in local asset managers and pension funds, and creating a template for later issues. The wiki uses it as the clearest public description of how the two layers of capital interact.
