O'Rourke, K. H., & Williamson, J. G. (2002). "When did globalisation begin?" *European Review of Economic History* 6(1): 23 to 50.

Taking commodity-price convergence as the test of economic globalization, the authors find no convergence before the nineteenth century and a clear start in the 1820s. The wiki uses the finding to anchor Phase 3 of the Cycle of Globalization at 1815 and to separate the political anchor of 1648 from the economic start.
