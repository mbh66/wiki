Vernon, R. (1966). "International Investment and International Trade in the Product Cycle." *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 80(2), 190–207.

Vernon argued that new products are made near the innovating market, where producers need close contact with customers and suppliers, and that production moves to low-cost locations abroad as products mature and standardize. The wiki reads the product life cycle against the strategic greenfield investment of 2025, in which new products such as advanced semiconductors were placed near the markets that design them.
