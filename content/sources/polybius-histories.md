Polybius. *The Histories*. Trans. W. R. Paton, rev. F. W. Walbank and C. Habicht. Loeb Classical Library. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2010 to 2012. Written in the second century BCE.

Book VI describes a cycle running from primitive monarchy through kingship, tyranny, aristocracy, oligarchy, democracy and mob rule back to monarchy, and applies the biological pattern of growth, prime and decay to constitutions. The wiki cites it as the earliest systematic cycle theory in the Cycle of Civilizations lineage.
