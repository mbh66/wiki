FONAG (2013). *Memoria Anual 2013*. Quito: Fondo para la Protección del Agua.

The annual report describes the Junta del Fideicomiso as "integrada por los representantes de las entidades constituyentes" and as the fund's top decision-making body, and dates the joining of each constituent: EPMAPS and The Nature Conservancy at the founding in 2000, Empresa Eléctrica Quito in 2001, Cervecería Nacional in 2003, and Tesalia in 2007, with Consorcio CAMAREN. The wiki cites it as FONAG's own record of who governs the fund. The report was read through the second enrichment run of 9 November 2026; the wiki has not opened it directly.
