South African Reserve Bank (2019). Observations on the evolution of South Africa's fiscal position. *Quarterly Bulletin*, box.

The box traces national government debt from a peak of 48.2 percent of GDP in 1995/96 to a low of 26.0 percent in 2008/09, and its rise after the 2008 crisis. The wiki uses these endpoints in [[research/south-africa-on-the-curve|South Africa on the Curve]] for the debt arc.
