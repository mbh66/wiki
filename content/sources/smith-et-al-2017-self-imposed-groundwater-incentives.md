Smith, S. M., Andersson, K., Cody, K. C., Cox, M. and Ficklin, D. (2017). "Responding to a Groundwater Crisis: The Effects of Self-Imposed Economic Incentives." *Journal of the Association of Environmental and Resource Economists* 4(4): 985-1023.

Using five years of data from farmers inside and outside Subdistrict 1 of the Rio Grande Water Conservation District, before and after its fee on net pumping began in 2011, the authors find that farmers in the fee district reduced groundwater extraction by about 32%. The wiki uses the study as its reference case for resource users who priced their own use and kept the proceeds under their own rules, the self-imposed incentives test in the currency register.
