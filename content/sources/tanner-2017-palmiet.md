Tanner, J., Smith, C., Ellery, F. and Hughes, D. (2017). "Determining the hydrological functioning of the endemic Palmiet wetlands in the Eastern Cape of South Africa." IAHS Scientific Assembly, abstract IAHS2017-322. Related research record: [Rhodes University repository](https://researchrepository.ru.ac.za/handle/123456789/8001).

Preliminary results from the Rhodes University Institute for Water Research project on palmiet (*Prionium serratum*) wetlands. The wetlands sit on alluvial fans in Table Mountain Group terrain and are fed by subsurface flow, which sustains a larger and longer baseflow. Steep discharge gradients make them prone to collapse. The wiki cites it as the most transferable South African evidence that wetland retention supports dry-season flow in sandstone catchments.
