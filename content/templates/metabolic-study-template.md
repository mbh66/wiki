A companion template to the [[templates/index|founding suite]]. A [[biohub|BioHub]] cohort sets its [[bioregion|BioRegion]]'s [[carrying-budget|carrying budget]], maps how materials and money move through the BioRegion in a [[metabolic-study|metabolic study]], and agrees its [[circulation-goals|Circulation Goals]], including the first loop to close. Produces three outputs: the Carrying Budget, the Metabolic Map and the Circulation Goals.

The full prompt text is on [[templates/metabolic-study-template-prompts|Metabolic Study Template: Prompts]].

## Overview

[[life-benjamin|Benjamin Life]]'s [[bioregional-development-plan|Bioregional Development Plan]] rests on a biophysical and metabolic map. Its two instruments measure different things. Carrying-capacity analysis asks how much the watershed can bear. Metabolic analysis asks how value and materials move through it. Carrying capacity sets the boundary condition, and the metabolic study sets the goals for circulation within it.

The founding suite already carries much of the biophysical side. The BioRegion Atlas holds the ecological, hydrological and climate profiles, the [[water-retention-landscapes|Water Retention Landscape]] baseline, the soil carbon baseline and the biodiversity inventory. The Value Proposition Evidence Pack holds the [[retention-economics|retention economics]] scaffolding. No template turns the baselines into limits, and no template traces the flows. This template does both, and its third output gives the Place Mandate and the Twenty-Year Value Ledger measured targets for value retained.

The template follows the suite's pattern: work the cohort does without AI, three prompts with cohort review between them, and three documents in the Statement, Evidence Pack and Compact shape. It adds one rule of its own. Flow data comes from the place's households and enterprises, so it is gathered with consent and published only in aggregate.

## When to run this template

Run it after the [[bioregion-establishment-template|BioRegion Establishment Template]] and the [[value-proposition-template|BioConomy Value Proposition Template]], when the BioRegion Atlas and the Value Proposition Evidence Pack are in hand, and before the [[templates/place-mandate-template|Place Mandate Template]]. Its outputs then feed the Value Ledger from the start.

A BioHub that has already produced its Place Mandate can run the template afterwards and bring the Circulation Goals into the Ledger at its next revision. A BioHub preparing a Bioregional Development Plan runs it as the stage that maps the economy; see [[templates/bioregional-development-plan|Assembling a Bioregional Development Plan]].

## Prerequisite documents

- The BioRegion Definition, BioRegion Atlas and BioRegion Charter.
- The Value Proposition Evidence Pack, with its readiness diagnostic and [[gap-register|gap register]].
- The [[founding-compact|Founding Compact]]'s consent and custodial principles.
- The Flow Gathering record from Step 0.
- Public data for the BioRegion: census and household survey tables, municipal and provincial budgets and procurement records, water and energy utility data, agricultural statistics, business registers.
- Any existing ecological threshold work for the territory (catchment reserve determinations, groundwater allocations, biodiversity targets).

## Step 0: Flow Gathering

Like Step 0 of the Place Mandate Template, this is cohort work without AI. Public statistics rarely describe a bioregion at the scale the template needs, and they say little about where a household's money goes. The cohort fills that gap from the place itself.

The cohort gathers four kinds of record. A spending sample asks a spread of households, across settlements and income levels, where they buy food, energy, building materials, transport and care, and from whom. An enterprise inventory lists the businesses operating in the BioRegion with what each buys and sells, where it buys, and whether its owners live in the BioRegion. A procurement record lists what public bodies and large institutions buy for the BioRegion and from whom. Producer conversations ask farmers and makers what they sell locally, what leaves the BioRegion, what they import as inputs, and where waste goes.

Every household and enterprise takes part by consent, and the record is kept in aggregate. No named household's spending, and no enterprise's confidential figures, enters any output. Custodial knowledge about land and water follows the Founding Compact's custodial principles: the record notes that it exists and who holds it, and never its content.

## The three prompts

**Prompt 1: Set the carrying budget.** From the Atlas, public data and existing threshold work, states the ecological ceilings (water, soil, biodiversity, land, carbon) and the social floor (food, water, shelter, energy, health, learning, work and income, voice) for the BioRegion. For each, the threshold, its source, the current state against it, and the allocations among uses and sub-areas. Data gaps are listed for the cohort.

**Prompt 2: Map the metabolism.** From the Flow Gathering record and public data, maps the material flows of each provisioning system (food, water, energy, building materials, care, waste) and the money flows that pay for them: what is met from within, what is imported, where money leaves with each purchase, where ownership sits outside the BioRegion, where flows overshoot the carrying budget, and where loops could close. Ranks the loop opportunities and names the chokepoints in each.

**Prompt 3: The three final documents.** Produces the Carrying Budget, the Metabolic Map and the Circulation Goals.

## Cohort work between prompts

Between Prompts 1 and 2, the cohort tests each threshold and allocation against local knowledge, and decides how to treat thresholds the evidence leaves uncertain. Allocations among uses are decisions about the place's future. The cohort marks which ones need the ratifying authorities and takes them there.

Between Prompts 2 and 3, the cohort corrects the map from what it knows, then sets the Circulation Goals: a target internal share for each flow in each period, and the pilot loop. Life's guidance is to close one loop end to end, usually food, and to fund chokepoints in order of severity. The cohort also states the conditions under which later financing facilities would open, starting with a bioregional trust. Prompt 3 waits until the ratifying authorities have ruled on what they hold.

Both steps operate under the Founding Compact's decision-making forms. See [[running-a-template|Running a Template]] for the shared conventions.

## The three outputs

**Carrying Budget.** Five to ten pages. Formal register. The ecological ceilings and social floor of the BioRegion, each with its threshold, source and current state, and the allocations the cohort and ratifying authorities have agreed. Read by public bodies, scientific partners and any party proposing work that draws on land or water.

**Metabolic Map.** Twenty-five to fifty pages. Reference register. The flows of each provisioning system, the money that pays for them, the leakage and ownership findings, the points of overshoot, the ranked loop opportunities and their chokepoints, the method and the data gaps. Read by the cohort planning work, by enterprises looking for local buyers and suppliers, and by investors conducting due diligence on a pilot loop.

**Circulation Goals.** Five to ten pages. Co-signed by the cohort and the ratifying authorities. Today's internal share of each flow, targets for each five-year period, the carrying-budget limit each target respects, the pilot loop with its chokepoints in order, the conditions for opening later financing facilities, and the revision cycle. Read by every party that engages, alongside the [[terms-of-engagement|Terms of Engagement]].

## Handoff to the Place Mandate Template

Attach the three outputs to Prompts 1 and 2 of the Place Mandate Template. The Metabolic Map informs the strands and the overlaps. The Circulation Goals give the Value Ledger's section on value held and retained its measures, and give the money sequence its pilot loop. The Carrying Budget becomes part of the Terms of Engagement's fixed ground: no engaging party may fund work that breaches an agreed allocation.

The Place Mandate Template's rule still holds. Its list of things that need doing comes from the [[place-knowledge-brief|Place Knowledge Brief]], and this template's findings may evidence that list, never add to it. Where the Metabolic Map shows something the Brief does not name, it goes to the cohort as a question for the next round of listening.

## Revision

Carrying capacity changes as a BioRegion regenerates, and circulation changes as loops close. Rerun Prompts 1 and 2 at the end of each five-year Ledger period, or sooner if the ratifying authorities call for it, and revise the Circulation Goals through the place's own process.

## Limits of the method

Life attributes the metabolic study to [[power-samantha|Samantha Power]] and [[wakefield-tyler|Tyler Wakefield]] and reports that the BioFi Project is developing the capacity at bioregional scale. Their method is not yet published, and this template is the wiki's own construction on Life's account of it. Expect the money-flow side to rest on samples and estimates in a first run, with many claims tagged [TBV]. The template is marked `working-hypothesis` until a BioHub has run it.
