A place produces the [[bioregional-development-plan|Bioregional Development Plan]] that [[life-benjamin|Benjamin Life]] places at the center of a [[bioregional-development-organization-bdo|Bioregional Development Organization]] by commissioning it through its own convening body, running the [[templates/index|founding suite]] with the [[templates/metabolic-study-template|Metabolic Study Template]], and binding the outputs into one ratified pack.

## Overview

Life's plan braids three maps. A biophysical and metabolic map sets what the land can carry and how materials and money move. A social map, drawn with the communities who feel overshoot first, sets the gap between current conditions and an adequate social foundation. A governance map seats stakeholders and their decision rights before significant money arrives. An investment thesis rests on the three, and the whole is redrawn as the economy develops.

The founding suite already produces most of this by its own route, as the [[templates/place-mandate-template|Place Mandate Template]] notes. This page makes the correspondence exact. It sets out the process a place follows, maps each element of Life's plan to the document that carries it, shows how the documents bind into one plan, and lists the additions the suite still needs.

The process starts before any template runs. Life's rule is that "the organizing precedes the money, always." A plan drafted by a coordinator and brought to the place for endorsement fails that rule, and it fails the suite's own test of standing. So the first stage is a decision by the place's convening body to make a plan at all.

## The process

### Stage 1: Commission the plan

The place's convening body decides to make a plan before anything is drafted. The body may be a development forum, a council, a traditional authority, a catchment management forum, or several of these acting together. It records six decisions: the area the plan covers and how the boundary question will be settled; who holds the pen (the BioHub cohort, or a cohort the body appoints); who ratifies each part; how the place's people will be heard; the timeline; and how dissent is recorded.

No draft plan enters the room before the commission. The cohort may bring the questions above, a description of the process, and evidence the place already holds.

The convening body's area is often administrative: a ward, a municipality, a district. Life requires bioregional boundaries. Where the two differ, the commission chooses between two arrangements. The plan may cover the convening body's area as one chapter of a plan for the whole BioRegion, reconciled with neighboring chapters under the BioRegion Charter. Or the plan may cover the BioRegion from the start, with the convening body as host and seats for the bodies that govern the rest of it.

### Stage 2: Seat the governance

Before money is sought, the cohort sets its decision rights. The [[biohub-identity-template|BioHub Identity Template]] produces the [[founding-compact|Founding Compact]], and the BioRegion Charter recognizes the custodial and coordination bodies already present. Test both against Life's four membership classes (sensing, productive, capital, public) and his safeguards against capture, listed under *Additions the suite needs* below. Life's warning is direct: "governance negotiated in the presence of capital is governance negotiated at gunpoint."

### Stage 3: Define the BioRegion

The [[bioregion-establishment-template|BioRegion Establishment Template]] settles the boundary and compiles the BioRegion Atlas. Its definition is the area Life calls the [[regenerative-development-district|Regenerative Development District]].

### Stage 4: Listen

Step 0 of the Place Mandate Template gathers the [[place-knowledge-brief|Place Knowledge Brief]] from every settlement and land-use group, without AI, under the protocols of any Indigenous authority. Listening takes longest, so it may begin as soon as the commission is made and run alongside Stages 3 to 5. The ratifying authorities accept the Brief, and its list of things that need doing becomes fixed input.

### Stage 5: Map the economy

The [[value-proposition-template|BioConomy Value Proposition Template]] sets out what the BioHub can tender. The Metabolic Study Template then sets the [[carrying-budget|carrying budget]], maps the flows in a [[metabolic-study|metabolic study]], and records the [[circulation-goals|Circulation Goals]], including the first loop to close.

### Stage 6: State the work and the terms

The Place Mandate Template produces the [[place-mandate|Place Mandate]], the [[twenty-year-value-ledger|Twenty-Year Value Ledger]] and the [[terms-of-engagement|Terms of Engagement]], with the Metabolic Study outputs attached.

### Stage 7: Ratify and publish the plan

The cohort binds the documents into one plan (see *Binding the plan* below). The ratifying authorities confirm the parts they hold. The convening body that commissioned the plan receives it and records its decision.

### Stage 8: Close the first loop, then redraw

The plan's first act is the pilot loop the Circulation Goals name, funded through a bioregional trust as Life's sequence requires. The carrying budget, the metabolic map and the Circulation Goals are redrawn at the end of each five-year Ledger period, and the Terms are revised only through the place's own process.

## Where each part of the plan lives

| Element of Life's plan | Document that carries it | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Organizing before money | The commission (Stage 1) | Process only; no template output |
| Bioregional boundary | BioRegion Definition | Held |
| Carrying-capacity analysis | Carrying Budget, drawing on the BioRegion Atlas baselines | Held with the companion template |
| Metabolic study | Metabolic Map | Held with the companion template |
| Social map: the place's own account | Place Knowledge Brief | Held |
| Social map: gap to an adequate social foundation | Carrying Budget, social floor | Held with the companion template |
| Indigenous nations as co-authors | Brief protocols; Founding Compact and Charter custodial principles | Held |
| Governance map: seats and decision rights | Founding Compact; BioRegion Charter | Partial: no membership classes or sensing-layer majority |
| Governance map: terms for outside capital | Terms of Engagement | Held |
| Safeguards against capture | Founding Compact entity form; Terms fixed ground and reporting | Partial: no unsellability or exit clause |
| Investment thesis | Place Mandate strands; Twenty-Year Value Ledger | Held |
| Pilot vertical and chokepoints | Circulation Goals | Held with the companion template |
| Activation of later financing facilities | Circulation Goals; Ledger money sequence | Held with the companion template |
| Living map, redrawn as the economy develops | Terms revision rule; Metabolic Study revision cycle | Held |
| Regenerative Development District | BioRegion Definition | Area held; legal standing open |
| Bioregional learning center | None | Open |

The investment thesis needs a note. Life organizes it around food, health, housing and ecology, resting on energy, water and infrastructure. The founding suite takes its strands from the Place Knowledge Brief. Use Life's sectors as a check on the strands after the Brief is accepted, and send any sector the Brief does not name back to the cohort as a question for the next round of listening.

## Binding the plan

The plan is a pack of existing documents with a short cover. The cover runs to two or three pages and states the commission and its date, the area and its relation to the BioRegion, the ratifications each part has received, the pilot loop, the revision dates, and where each document is held. It quotes the Place Mandate's goal. It asks no one for money.

The pack has three parts, matching Life's three maps.

1. **Land and flows.** BioRegion Definition, Carrying Budget, Metabolic Map, with the BioRegion Atlas as reference.
2. **People and work.** Place Knowledge Brief (as accepted, with custodial content withheld), Place Mandate, Twenty-Year Value Ledger, Circulation Goals.
3. **Governance.** Founding Compact, BioRegion Charter, Terms of Engagement.

The Value Proposition Statement and Evidence Pack travel with the plan as supporting documents.

## Additions the suite needs

These are proposals. None is yet in the template source documents.

**Founding Compact: membership classes.** Add to the BioHub Identity Template's Prompt 3 a section mapping the Compact's members to Life's four classes, with the sensing layer holding a structural majority on plan decisions, and a statement of how the public layer represents the [[commons|commons]] and the more-than-human.

**Founding Compact: safeguards.** Add steward-ownership provisions that make the BioHub unsellable, an exit clause that lets a member facility leave with its assets, and open books across every body the BioHub federates.

**Place Knowledge Brief: a ninth question.** Add "Who here goes without, and of what?" so that the social floor in the Carrying Budget is checked against the place's own account.

**Place Mandate Template: the metabolic study.** Treat the Metabolic Study outputs as a standard input where a plan is being made. Today the template lists a metabolic study as optional.

**Bioregional learning center.** Life pairs the BDO with a [[bioregional-learning-centre|bioregional learning center]] and warns that capital deployed without one reaches "a culture that cannot yet receive it." The plan should name where that capacity is grown. The suite has no template for it yet.

## Multi-BioHub regions

Where several BioHubs share a BioRegion, they set one carrying budget for the whole BioRegion under the Charter. Each may map its own part, state its own Mandate and set its own Circulation Goals, provided allocations and outcomes are reconciled. The Charter records whether the BDO's capital functions sit with a federation of BioHubs or with one capital-holding BioHub, and the plan cover states which.
