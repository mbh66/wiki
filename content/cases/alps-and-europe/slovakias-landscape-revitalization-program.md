## What the sources show

The wiki's [[research/water-retention-evidence|water retention evidence brief]] graded this program in detail. Its supreme audit office found duplicate invoices worth €32,181 across 46 municipalities, reporters found half the checked structures damaged in 2020, and a dam built under the program at Rudno nad Hronom breached in 2021, killing one person [IC]. No hydrological evaluation of the program was published. The program paid municipalities for labor and left no community body to hold or maintain the works.
