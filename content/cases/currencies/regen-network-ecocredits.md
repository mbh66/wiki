## What the sources show

Regen Network runs a public ledger, the Regen Registry for ecological claims, and Regen Market, where project developers list ecocredits issued under credit classes and methodologies; it also hosted NCT (Nature Carbon Ton), a Toucan pool of tokenized Verra credits from nature-based methodologies [MS]. In a response written in early 2023 to the *Guardian*'s January 2023 reporting on REDD+, Regen stated that six of the 13 tokenized forestry and land-use projects in NCT were issued under Verra's REDD+ program [MS]. A single-case study by Jirásek (Masaryk University, working paper) credits its design with improving traceability and methodological standards, and also records that scientists (Simmons et al. 2021) questioned the soil-carbon credits Regen Network sold to Microsoft; that in the Grgich Hills grazing project Regen-related entities filled the roles of methodology developer, administrator, project developer and monitor; that an August 2023 goal of issuing over 2 million ecocredits did not materialize; that one representative reported selling 30 biodiversity credits in five months; and that of 6,238 credits issued in an Ecuadorian jaguar habitat pilot, 2,707 (43%) had been retired by 10 October 2025 [TBV: one independent working paper].
