## What the sources show

South Africa's Electronic Silo Certificates System, run through SAFEX on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange since 2004, is by far the largest warehouse receipt system in a comparative table of African systems, at 3.0 million tonnes in 2019/20; the next largest listed is Ghana's, at 47,000 tonnes in 2016 (Thunde and Baulch 2020) [TBV: one secondary source]. The certificates settle trades and secure finance among commercial farmers, traders and banks. The run's statement that large farms are favored was not found in the source.
