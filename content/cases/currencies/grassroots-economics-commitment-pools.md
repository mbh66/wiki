## What the sources show

Grassroots Economics' [[glossary/a-e/commitment-pool|commitment pools]] on the Celo blockchain let people exchange Community Asset Vouchers, which represent commitments to provide goods and services. The issuer reports 188 active commitment pools and 745 active vouchers on Celo, from activity between 5 July 2023 and 20 July 2025 [MS]; an independent preprint dates the Celo-based version of the Sarafu Network to 2023 (Evite 2025). In Kiriba village, Kilifi, the Mnyumbuni community pool became operational in June 2025 and five neighborhood pools followed in January 2026; they record *Mweria*, a local practice of rotating shared labor, and work on syntropic beds and tree planting. A dataset generated on 6 August 2026 for the six pools shows 138 vouchers, 15,204 transfer events and 526 linked reports, 192 of them dated after a pool became operational [MS]. No measured resource outcome was found.
