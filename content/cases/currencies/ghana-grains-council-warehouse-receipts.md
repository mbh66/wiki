## What the sources show

The Ghana Grains Council runs a certified warehouse receipt system for maize, set up in 2008. A comparative table of African systems gives its volume as 47,000 tonnes in 2016 (Thunde and Baulch 2020); the research run had read this figure as a count of receipts. Aboagye (2023) finds that GGC-linked volumes were about 1.2% of national maize production in 2015, that commercial warehouses required minimum deposits of 5 tonnes, and that loans against receipts mostly went to corporate bodies or large traders. Farmers using community warehouses in northern Ghana reported post-harvest losses of about 2% and fees of about US\$0.35 per 100 kg bag [IC]. The receipts serve as collateral and settlement instruments among traders and banks; none circulates as household money.
