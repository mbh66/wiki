## What the sources show

The Bristol Pound circulated from 2012, backed by sterling held at the Great Western Credit Union. At its peak, "more than £1million in Bristol Pounds was circulated in the city every year through some 650 business members" (Bristol247). The community interest company said in January 2020 that it needed major funding to survive and announced the closure in April 2020; the digital scheme closed in August 2020, with accounts reverting to ordinary credit union accounts, and in February 2021 the company said the project could not continue without new funding. Notes left circulation at the end of September 2021 (Bristol247; ITV). ITV counted 1,500 individual users and 650 businesses and cited the shift to contactless payments and falling use of cash in the pandemic [IC]. *The Bristol Cable* (November 2022) reported, in its own narration, that "only about 0.02% of Bristol's population used the currency" and that about 80% of users had a degree; the managing director, Diana Finch, named the scheme's tone of voice and marketing as the main problem and said the model was exclusive and burdensome for small businesses [IC, media].
