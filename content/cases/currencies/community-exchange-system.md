## What the sources show

Tim Jenkin and Don Northcott founded the Cape Town Talent Exchange in February 2003; it became the Community Exchange System, an online mutual credit network trading in "talents." Margaret Legum reported in IOL in October 2006 that about 2.5 million talents, roughly R500,000, had been traded and that about 2,000 members traded in Cape Town; later counts put the global server at 485 exchange groups in 53 countries in 2013 (Wikipedia) [TBV]. No South African Reserve Bank statement on community currencies was found.
