## What the sources show

A law of 18 March 1796 created the mandats territoriaux, exchanged at one mandat for 30 assignats, with issue capped at 2.4 billion livres and tied to the value of the national lands (*biens nationaux*). The mandats traded at about 20% of face value by April and were fully devalued about three months after issue. They lost legal tender status in February 1797 and were worth almost nothing by May, as France returned to metal money on 4 February 1797 [IC].
