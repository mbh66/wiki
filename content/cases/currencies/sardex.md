## What the sources show

Sardex is a business mutual credit system founded in Sardinia in 2009 and run by the company Sardex S.p.A. One Sardex credit equals one euro as a unit of account; balances carry no interest, credit lines are capped by company, and the company funds itself through yearly membership fees of €200 for social and nonprofit enterprises up to €2,000 for large companies. In 2015 it had about 3,000 members, mostly small and medium enterprises, and mediated €51 million of trade, almost 0.2% of Sardinian GDP (Motta, Dini and Sartori 2017, citing Littera 2015) [IC]. The study draws on semi-structured interviews with members across the island; members report that the zero-interest credit buffer has helped some companies retain employees during the crisis [MS].
