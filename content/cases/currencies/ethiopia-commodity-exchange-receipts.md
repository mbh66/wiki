## What the sources show

Ethiopia enacted a special law governing warehouse receipts in 2003, among the first in Africa (Wehling and Garthwaite 2015). The Ethiopian Commodity Exchange issues electronic warehouse receipts, which a bank creditor can sell through foreclosure 15 days after the payment date in its notice; the bank must inform the exchange so that its central depository releases the goods [IC]. A comparative table lists a Warehouse Receipt Finance Initiative from 2009, run with the exchange and backed by the IFC, with minimal volumes (Thunde and Baulch 2020) [IC]. In June 2026 the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration created a formal national warehouse receipt system letting farmers borrow against produce in certified warehouses; Birr Metrics reports that earlier initiatives had been limited in scale and lacked a nationwide legal framework [IC, media].
