## What the sources show

KlimaDAO describes KLIMA as "our carbon-backed, algorithmic cryptocurrency" and says the treasury "must have at least 1 BCT held in reserves in order to mint 1 KLIMA" [MS]. Its "sweeping the floor" campaign encouraged buying the cheapest available credits, which critics said were often low quality (*Time*, 26 May 2022). KLIMA traded above US\$1,000 at first, briefly reached the mid-US\$3,000s, and had fallen to near US\$20 by 7 April 2022 (CarbonPlan) [IC]. KlimaDAO called Verra's May 2022 ban "a broadly positive move forward for the Web3 carbon space" while criticizing Verra's closed-door approach (S&P Global) [IC]. It is the only implemented carbon-backed currency the research run found.
