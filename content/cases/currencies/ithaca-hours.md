## What the sources show

Paul Glover started Ithaca HOURS in Ithaca, New York; the first notes were printed in October 1991 and the system launched that November. One HOUR was valued at US\$10, roughly the average hourly wage in Tompkins County. More than 1,000 individuals and 500 businesses enrolled in the following years. Use declined after Glover moved away and as payments shifted to cards; the currency was effectively discontinued in the 2010s, and revival attempts around 2019 failed (Wikipedia, citing *The Progressive*, *Forbes*, the *New York Times* and the *Ithaca Times*) [TBV].
