## What the sources show

Sarafu is Grassroots Economics' digital [[glossary/a-e/community-inclusion-currency|Community Inclusion Currency]] in Kenya; users understood one Sarafu to be worth about a Kenyan shilling, though actual exchange took place only in limited instances. Over the observation period from 25 January 2020 to 15 June 2021, registered accounts grew from 8,354 to about 55,000, and transactions totaled about 300 million Sarafu in value, about US\$2.8 million. On 19 October 2020 penalties were applied to mostly long-inactive accounts, and many balances were zeroed (Mattsson, Criscione and Ruddick 2022) [IC]. The flow network built from standard transactions covers 40,657 accounts, 421,329 transactions and 293.7 million Sarafu. Circulation was highly modular and geographically localized among users with diverse livelihoods, community-based institutions often acted as local hubs, and centrality measures confirm the importance of early adopters and of women's participation (Mattsson, Criscione and Takes 2023) [IC]. A randomized trial exists only as an unpublished bachelor's thesis [TBV].
