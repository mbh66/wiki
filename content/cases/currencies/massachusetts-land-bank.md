## What the sources show

The Land Bank of 1740 in Massachusetts issued "Land-Bank bills" against mortgages on its subscribers' lands in Barnstable, Essex, Bristol and Hampshire counties. Governor Belcher recommended its suppression, and the General Court passed acts "for the more speedy finishing" of the bank. Its partners then faced assessments, lawsuits and warrants of distress from the commissioners winding it up (Colonial Society of Massachusetts, calendar of the Land Bank records) [IC]. The calendar refers to a bill to extend the Bubble Act of 1720 in connection with bills issued in America; whether Parliament's action ended the bank, as the research run recorded, was not confirmed.
