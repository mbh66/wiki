## What the sources show

From October 2021, holders could retire Verra carbon credits and mint Base Carbon Tonne (BCT) tokens through Toucan. CarbonPlan (Badgley and Cullenward, 7 April 2022) counted over 21.6 million Verra credits moved on chain, and S&P Global put the total at about 22 million, around 4% of all Verra issuance. After HFC-23 credits were bridged early on, Toucan restricted their eligibility. CarbonPlan found that about 28% of Toucan-bridged credits, about 6.0 million tons of CO₂-equivalent, came from "zombie" projects, defined as projects with no public retirement in the two years before a Toucan retirement or with more than 95% of their retirements made through Toucan. Verra's board decided in April 2022 to ban tokens based on retired credits, and Verra announced the ban on 25 May 2022; Toucan welcomed it [IC]. The pool filled with the cheapest eligible credits, and the claim behind each token left the place it came from.
