Terrace and water-temple systems in Bali, the Cordillera and Yunnan have coordinated mountain water for centuries and are now losing land and water to tourism and labor to out-migration.

## The cases

**[[cases/southeast-and-east-asia/subak-and-water-temples-of-bali|Subak, Bali]] (Indonesia).** Farmer associations coordinate irrigation and cropping through a hierarchy of water temples. Lansing modeled how this coordination manages pests and water across a watershed (Lansing and Kremer 1993; Lansing 2007). UNESCO inscribed the Cultural Landscape of Bali Province in 2012, covering 20 subaks over about 2,400 hectares (SEI 2015). A Transnational Institute brief (2018) reports Bali lost nearly 25% of its agricultural land in 25 years and that tourism receives preferential water [IC]. Farmers cite ritual costs, land taxes and water shortage [IC].

**[[cases/southeast-and-east-asia/ifugao-rice-terraces|The Ifugao terraces]]**. UNESCO removed them from the List of World Heritage in Danger in 2012, after listing abandonment tied to a neglected irrigation system and out-migration among the threats, and noted that sustained funding remains needed [IC].

**[[cases/southeast-and-east-asia/hani-rice-terraces|The Hani terraces]]**. In the main scenic area, the county and the Yunnan Shibo Tourism Holding Group said in 2014 that 10% of pre-tax ticket revenue went to the county for villagers, while villagers and a village committee said they had received nothing; this stays [TBV]. At Azheke, a village company set up in 2019 with a Sun Yat-sen University team pays 70% of revenue to villagers, with shares tied to keeping traditional houses and farming the terraces, and had paid CNY 3.4525 million in 12 dividend rounds (CNY 53,116 per household) by October 2025 [IC]. Cultivated land in Yuanyang County fell by 84.16 km² between 2010 and 2017, by one unreviewed thesis.

**[[cases/southeast-and-east-asia/iriai-commons-of-japan|Japan's iriai]]**. About 1.85 million hectares of iriai forest existed when the 1966 modernization law was enacted; 0.89 million hectares have since been reorganized into individual or association ownership, and an owners' survey confirmed only 0.45 million hectares as remaining iriai (Forestry Agency 2025) [IC]. No source documented a water role for the surviving iriai.
