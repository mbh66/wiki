## What the sources show

**Second enrichment run.** The second enrichment run found no independent measured evaluation. TNC's 2015 business case projected a 50% cut in sediment concentration, up to 15% more water yield and US\$250,000 a year in savings for Nairobi's water company. Reported results are smaller: an 11% reduction in turbidity in the fund's own report, and suspended sediment 21.3% below average with 8% less filtration chemical use as of December 2023 in the Resilient Watersheds Toolbox [MS]. The metrics and dates differ, so the gap is indicative. A mid-term evaluation of August 2019 and a final evaluation planned for March 2022 were not found.
