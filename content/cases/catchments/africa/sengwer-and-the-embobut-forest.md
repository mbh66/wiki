## What the sources show

Amnesty International's 2018 report on Embobut Forest (about 22,000 hectares) found that the government's 2013 decision to resettle all residents was taken without genuinely consulting the Sengwer, that forest guards and police burned up to 1,500 homes in January 2014 despite a court injunction, and that more than 300 houses were burned after 29 December 2017. On 16 January 2018 a Sengwer man was shot dead during an eviction, and the next day the EU suspended its Kenya Water Towers programme [IC]. A government task force found the Kenya Forest Service had colluded in illegal logging in the forest.
