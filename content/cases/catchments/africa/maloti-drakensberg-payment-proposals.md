## What the sources show

Blignaut and colleagues (2010) note that the Maloti-Drakensberg covers under 5% of South Africa's surface and yields about 25% of its runoff. Their linked hydrology, ecology and economic model found that restoring and managing degraded grassland returns more than it costs in low to medium degraded areas, and that baseflow gained this way compares well with built water supply; they propose a payment scheme [IC]. No record of the scheme being implemented with communities was found, so the case stays coded as a proposal that did not launch.
