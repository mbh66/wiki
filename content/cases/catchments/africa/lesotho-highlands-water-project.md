## What the sources show

International Rivers' 2001 evaluation against the World Commission on Dams guidelines reports that the first phases affected more than 30,000 rural farmers, that people received no compensation before displacement, and that living standards for most affected people were declining. Water royalties and customs dues made up 27.8% of Lesotho's government revenue, and the Lesotho Highlands Water Revenue Fund was restructured by the World Bank after it was used to reward ruling-party supporters [IC]. Households in the highlands hold no part of the catchment's water value; the state receives it.
