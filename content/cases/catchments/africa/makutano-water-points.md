## Note on sources

This case and [[coady-abcd-program-in-ethiopia|Coady ABCD Program in Ethiopia]] both rest on evaluations compiled by Brianne Peters at the Coady International Institute, which ran or framed both programs. The second enrichment run of 9 November 2026 searched for independent evaluations of each; it found none for Ethiopia, and for Makutano one 2014 radio report (May, *The World*) whose counts of roads and water points differ from Coady's. The two cases are kept separate, and their shared evaluator should be weighed when they are read together.
