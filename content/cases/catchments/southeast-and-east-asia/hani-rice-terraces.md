## What the sources show

A 2023 master's thesis on Yuanyang County reports that cultivated land in the county fell by 84.16 km² between 2010 and 2017, and that some terraces near Azheke village were abandoned after labor shortages; it describes tourism as helping some farmers stay [TBV]. The run's coding that tourism revenue goes to outside firms was not confirmed in this pass. The Cultural Landscape of Honghe Hani Rice Terraces was inscribed as a World Heritage site in 2013.

## Azheke village company

Azheke is a named sub-entry of this case, graded [IC] on two independent sources, while the case as a whole stays [TBV].

**Main scenic area.** The county government and the Yunnan Shibo Tourism Holding Group formed a joint company to run the terraces as a scenic area. A Shibo office director said in 2014 that Shibo pays 10% of pre-tax ticket revenue to the county government to be passed back to villagers, and that how it reaches them is the government's responsibility. Villagers in Qingkou and staff of the Tuguozhai village committee said they had not received or heard of the payment (China Youth Daily, reproduced by China Daily, 18 February 2014). Yuanyang's ticket revenue was CNY 8.4187 million in 2013. No later source on this flow was found, so it stays [TBV].

**Azheke.** A village company has run tourism at Azheke since February 2019, under a plan led by Bao Jigang's team at Sun Yat-sen University. The county holds 30% of its CNY 10 million registered capital as infrastructure, and the village's houses, terraces and way of life are valued at 70% (People's Forum, 4 February 2024). The village assembly adopted rules against leasing, sale and outside capital. Revenue goes 30% to the company and 70% to villagers, and the villagers' share is divided 40% for keeping traditional mushroom houses, 30% for farming the terraces, 20% for living in the village and 10% for keeping household registration (Honghe Prefecture government). By 11 October 2025 the company had received 277,000 visitors and CNY 7.1356 million in revenue and paid 12 dividend rounds totaling CNY 3.4525 million, or CNY 53,116 per household across 65 households (Shangguan News, 29 December 2025) [IC]. The rule that pays villagers for farming the terraces is a direct response to abandonment. A few households in concrete houses rejected the dividend rule (People's Forum 2024).
