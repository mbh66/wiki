## What the sources show

UNESCO's 2012 state of conservation report lists abandonment of the terraces, tied to a neglected irrigation system and people leaving the area, alongside unregulated development, tourism and the lack of an effective management system, as the threats for which the property was on the List of World Heritage in Danger. That year the World Heritage Committee found the threats addressed and removed it from the list, while noting that sustained financial resources remain a long-term need [IC].
