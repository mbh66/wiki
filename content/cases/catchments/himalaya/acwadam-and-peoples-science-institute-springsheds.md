## What the sources show

No independent evaluation of ACWADAM or People's Science Institute springshed work was found in this pass. A 2025 interview with the head of the Himmotthan Society reports a baseline survey of 10,063 springs in 2,413 Uttarakhand villages by more than 100 trained para-hydrogeologists: 76% of springs showed falling discharge over the last decade, and springs on abandoned land were drying about twice as fast as those on cultivated land [MS]. The survey shows the scale of the problem and does not test whether revival works.

**Second enrichment run.** The second enrichment run found only the implementers' own assessments. A PSI and ACWADAM impact study of a Nagaland program (about 2022) reports discharge gains at named springs, such as 40 to 60% at Alohsa in the 2021 lean season, and records one community that saw no significant increase after two years. PSI's 2021-22 annual report claims a 40% rise in treated springs in Uttarakhand. CHIRAG's Spring Atlas of Uttarakhand, prepared with ACWADAM, reports one spring rising from 1.76 to 2.6 liters per minute and attributes a 16% rise at another to higher rainfall [MS]. All are before-after comparisons without controls or systematic rainfall correction.
