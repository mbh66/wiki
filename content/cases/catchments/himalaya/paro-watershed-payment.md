## What the sources show

Bhutan's third payment-for-ecosystem-services agreement was signed on 29 October 2015 for 2016 to 2020, between the Namey Nichu Watershed Management Group, 29 households of Namey Nichu village in Tsento gewog, and the area's drinking water users: five hotels and the Satsham Water Association. SNV facilitated it with the Watershed Management Division and the Paro Dzongkhag Forestry Office (World Bank 2019) [IC]. The providers clear debris from two streams, graze village cattle by day only, keep a 50 m buffer around springs, clean the sources twice a year and patrol them. SNV's account states that they "shall be paid Nu.142,000 only upon compliance to the four agreed activities and after verification by the PES Verification Team", and IUCN reports that the community forest group manages the money, which it gives as about US\$2,000 a year from four hotels and water associations (IUCN 2019); the two accounts differ on the number of hotels. Consultations on a second phase from December 2018 raised concerns about controlling water allocation to the hotels in an area opposite Taktsang that is seeing rapid hotel development (IUCN 2019). No water measurement was found.
