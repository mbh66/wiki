## What the sources show

**Second enrichment run.** The second enrichment run found an official record. On 14 October 2022 Sunass, the national sanitation regulator, recorded the inauguration of two microdams in San Juan de Tarucani under Sedapar's MERESE, financed by drinking-water users through the tariff Sunass approved, with S/135,000 disbursed after the first year [IC]. Descosur reports one qocha of 133,500 m³ and that communities may use the water for their herds, and a 2024 report gives 25 qochas storing more than 600,000 m³ with more than S/8 million invested [MS]. Fundación Mohme (2019) reported that Sedapar had not yet begun collecting MERESE funds, which places the start of collection after 2019. No hydrological monitoring result was found.
