## What the sources show

Nüsser and colleagues (2019) inventoried 14 ice reservoirs built in central Ladakh over about 30 years. At Nang they measured about 1,010 to 3,220 m³ of stored ice in an unfavorable winter, far below the published capacity estimates of 17,000 to 23,500 m³, and found that only five catchments could irrigate all their fields even in optimal years. Funding came from government, NGOs, the army and crowdfunding; maintenance suffered from labor shortages, and the ice stupa caused disputes over water sharing. They judge the structures useful site-specific water conservation and unlikely to be effective climate adaptation [IC].
