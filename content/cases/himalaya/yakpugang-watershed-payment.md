## What the sources show

The Yakpugang Community Forest Management Group signed a contract with the Mongar municipal corporation in December 2010 to protect the springs that supply the town (SAARC Forestry Centre 2014); WWF-Bhutan dates its implementation to 2011 and counts 113 members in the provider group. The first three-year contract paid Nu 52,000 a year (UNDP 2012), and the contract was renewed [IC]. A 2021 study gives the protected zone as 373.129 acres (about 151 hectares), though other passages in it give 538, 638 and 1,176.22 acres; it reports current payments of Nu 178,200 a year from town users and Nu 50,000 from Mongar Regional Referral Hospital, and finds Yakpugang residents more aware of the payment than town dwellers, about 20% of whom knew of it (Norten 2021); a national synthesis records late renewal and late payment in the first period. Community forest members fence the sources, keep livestock out, plant native trees and sign yearly agreements, and report year-round water and better maize harvests (WOCAT) [MS]. WOCAT gives 2007 and a 255-hectare recharge zone, which may refer to the community forest's founding and the wider catchment; this is unconfirmed. No water measurement was found.
