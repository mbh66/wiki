## What the sources show

Oldekop and colleagues (2019), using national data across Nepal, found that community forest management, practiced by more than 18,000 user groups covering over a third of the country's forests, was associated with a 37% relative reduction in deforestation and a 4.3% relative reduction in poverty, and that areas under it were 51% more likely to see both fall together [IC]. The study measures forest and poverty; it does not measure water.
