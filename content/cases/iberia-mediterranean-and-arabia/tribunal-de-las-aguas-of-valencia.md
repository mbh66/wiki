## What the sources show

The Tribunal is formed by nine irrigation communities drawing on the Turia (Quart, Benàger and Faitanar, Tormos, Mislata, Chirivella, Mestalla, Favara, Rascanya and Rovella), meets every Thursday at noon at the door of Valencia Cathedral, and was listed by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage in 2009. Ostrom (1990) used the Valencian huerta among her long-enduring irrigation commons [IC]. The Tribunal governs river water downstream; the mountain catchment of the Turia is outside its rules.
