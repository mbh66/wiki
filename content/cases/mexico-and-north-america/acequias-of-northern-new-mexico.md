## What the sources show

Field studies in an acequia-irrigated valley of northern New Mexico, summarized by Rango and colleagues (2013), found that about 60% of the water diverted from a main acequia returns to the river as surface flow, about 33% returns as shallow groundwater, and only about 7% is lost to evapotranspiration. Seepage and deep percolation recharge the shallow aquifer at about 1,169 mm a year, and the aquifer releases water slowly back to the river, extending the runoff season for downstream users [IC]. Cox (2014) documents the acequias as a long-enduring commons under pressure from water-right sales.
