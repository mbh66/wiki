The wiki keeps its cases in registers, one page per case, so that research runs and enrichment passes add to a single filterable record and every brief draws on the same entries.

## The registers

**[[bioconomy/cases/catchments/index|Community Catchment Case Register]].** Cases in which a community holds, governs or is paid for a living catchment, organized in eight mountain regions and coded for assets, coordination, tenure, value flow, beyond-market markers, water and community outcomes, capture and evidence level. It began with the [[research/mountain-water-commons|Mountain Water Commons]] brief of 9 November 2026 and also holds the water rights, fees and markets from the resource-backed currencies research run of 10 November 2026.

**[[bioconomy/cases/currencies/index|Currency Case Register]].** Currencies, vouchers, warehouse receipts and tokens whose value was tied to grain, land, carbon or ecological work, with community currencies that have no resource backing kept beside them for comparison. Cases are coded for backing, measurement, issuer, holding cost, design principles, economy and resource outcomes, failure and evidence level. It began with the resource-backed currencies research run of 10 November 2026.

## How the registers relate

A case lives in one register. Where a case could belong to both, as with water shares in the Omani aflaj or the self-imposed groundwater fee in the San Luis Valley, it lives in the catchment register and the currency register links to it. Both registers grade claims the same way: [IC] for two or more independent sources or an official record, [MS] for the account of the party the claim is about, and [TBV] for a plausible claim not yet confirmed. Both describe communities, organizations and projects only through their published records and statements, and both keep failures on equal terms with the cases that lasted.

Catchment case pages carry the `case` tag and currency case pages the `currency-case` tag, so each register's bases list only their own cases.
