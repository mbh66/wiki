[[sources/briggs-2021-emerging-world|Roger Briggs, Emerging World (2021)]] places the Mimetic macro-stage at this date: starting about 3 million years ago, with the first manufactured stone tools. Its basis: following Merlin Donald, he takes the first manufactured stone tools as the sign of mimetic consciousness, and notes finished tools 2.6 to 3.4 million years old (ch. 4, pp. 121, 123-124) [TBV]. His Grand Synthesis table gives the start as about 3 million years ago (p. 152) [TBV].

The nearest dated evidence: Oldest known stone tools, 3.3 million years ago at Lomekwi 3, Kenya (Harmand et al. 2015, Nature 521); earliest Homo fossils about 2.8 million years ago [IC].

The date is consistent with the nearest dated record.
