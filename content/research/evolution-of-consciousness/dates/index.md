Each entry here records where one source places one threshold of the evolution of consciousness, and how that date compares with the dated record.

## Overview

The sources name their stages in their own terms and date them in their own ways. Each entry gives the date as the source states it, what the source rests it on, the nearest dated evidence and a fit: consistent, earlier, later, undated or not comparable. Each entry is also matched to the nearest of the network's five structures; that match is the grid's reading, and the source keeps its own terms. A field called `years_ago` converts each date into an approximate count of years before 2026, so the table can sort in time.
