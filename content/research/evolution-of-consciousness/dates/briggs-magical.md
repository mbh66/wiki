[[sources/briggs-2021-emerging-world|Roger Briggs, Emerging World (2021)]] places the Magical macro-stage at this date: starting about 1 million years ago. Its basis: he argues for Gebser's magical structure as a stage of its own between the mimetic and the mythic, and finds no sign of it in the tool industries. He ties it to *Homo heidelbergensis*, which appeared about one million years ago (ch. 4, pp. 127-128, 154) [TBV]. He also links Gebser's magical structure to anthropologists' studies of living peoples (p. 127); the wiki repeats none of these placements.

The nearest dated evidence: Controlled fire about 1 million years ago at Wonderwerk Cave (Berna et al. 2012, PNAS); burnt bone dated 1.07 to 1.79 million years ago at Wonderwerk (Marin-Monfort et al. 2026, PLOS One) [IC].

The date is consistent with the nearest dated record.
