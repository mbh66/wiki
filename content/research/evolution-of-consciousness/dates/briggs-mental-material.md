[[sources/briggs-2021-emerging-world|Roger Briggs, Emerging World (2021)]] places the Material stage at this date: about 5,000 years ago, with the first civilisations. Its basis: chapter 5 dates the onset of the Material macro-stage to the first civilisations in Persia and Egypt, and lists the Mental (2,500 years ago), the Mental Rational (400 years ago) and the Postmodern (50 years ago) as sub-stages within it (ch. 5, pp. 163, 166) [TBV]. He also treats the megaliths of Göbekli Tepe, from about 11,500 years ago, as the first signs of the built world (p. 161) [TBV].

The nearest dated evidence: Earliest writing in Mesopotamia about 3200 BCE [TBV].

The date is consistent with the nearest dated record.
