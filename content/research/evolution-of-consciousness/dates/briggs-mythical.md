[[sources/briggs-2021-emerging-world|Roger Briggs, Emerging World (2021)]] places the Mythical macro-stage at this date: starting about 60,000 years ago, with the Great Leap. Its basis: he dates the Mythical stage to the Great Leap about 60,000 years ago, marked by many new kinds of tools, the dispersal out of Africa and complex spoken language, the basis of oral storytelling (ch. 4, p. 124) [TBV]. Chapter 5 calls the same stage Mythic (p. 166) [TBV].

The nearest dated evidence: Pigment use about 164,000 years ago at Pinnacle Point (Marean et al. 2007, Nature 449); ochre drawing about 73,000 years ago at Blombos (Henshilwood et al. 2018, Nature 562) [IC].

The source's date is later than the dated record shows.
