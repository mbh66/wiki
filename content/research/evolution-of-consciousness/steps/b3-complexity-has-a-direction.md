Measured complexity has risen across cosmic, biological and cultural systems. Some sources go further and hold that consciousness rides a direction in that rise.

This is step B3 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: E1 to E3 for the rise in complexity (Chaisson); E4 to E5 for the direction (Teilhard, Aurobindo, Wilber).

The step is read differently depending on whether consciousness is fundamental (an inference; see P9 on the grid page).

## How the wording changed

Run 2 split the step into its two halves, which rest on different kinds of evidence, and marked it contested.

Of the 94 grid sources, 10 affirm this step, 14 qualify it and 6 dissent. The other 64 are silent on it.
