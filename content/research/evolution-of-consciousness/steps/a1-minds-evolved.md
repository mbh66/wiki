Awareness occurs in graded forms across many animal lineages and arose by evolution. Support is strongest for mammals and birds; for other vertebrates and many invertebrates it is a realistic possibility.

This is step A1 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: E1 measured study.

The step holds whether or not consciousness is fundamental (an inference; see P9 on the grid page).

## How the wording changed

Run 2 added the split between strong support and realistic possibility, following the New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness (2024).
