Gebser, Jaynes, Barfield and others argue that space, time and self were experienced differently after each step. Donald, Ong, Mithen and Deacon describe the same steps as changes in cognitive capacity and memory.

This is step A3 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: E3 theory on evidence to E4 synthesis.

The step is read differently depending on whether consciousness is fundamental (an inference; see P9 on the grid page).

## How the wording changed

Run 2 reworded the step as a claim some sources make, and set the cognitive-capacity reading beside it, because the sources split here.

Of the 93 grid sources, 9 affirm this step, 6 qualify it and 2 dissent. The other 76 are silent on it.
