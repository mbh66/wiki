The hominin and archaeological record shows capacities arriving at different times: stone tools, controlled fire, pigment and symbolic marking, and writing. Language is inferred from these traces and carries no direct date.

This is step A2 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: E2 dated record, with E3 theory for language.

The step holds whether or not consciousness is fundamental (an inference; see P9 on the grid page).

## How the wording changed

Run 2 separated out language, which leaves no direct trace and so carries no date of its own.
