This is step C1 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: E3 theory on evidence to E4 synthesis.

The step holds whether or not consciousness is fundamental (an inference; see P9 on the grid page).
