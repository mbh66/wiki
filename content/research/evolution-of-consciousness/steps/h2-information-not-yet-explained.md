Some information reaches minds by routes that current science has not explained.

This is step H2 of the claim chain on the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]]. It sits in the hinge beside [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/steps/h-consciousness-is-fundamental|H]], and it is the grid step that step T2 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/t2-information-arrives|Anabranch thesis]] draws on.

## Evidence

Evidence type: Measured studies (E1), contested by failed replications and critiques, with testimony (E5).

The step covers telepathy, precognition and other psi effects in experiments; reports of accurate perception during cardiac arrest or near-death experiences; terminal and paradoxical lucidity where they are offered as evidence of information beyond the brain's state; cases suggestive of reincarnation; and accounts from traditions and testimony of knowledge received by such routes. It does not cover the claim that consciousness is fundamental (step H) or that it survives the body, unless a work also claims that information arrives by an unexplained route.

The claim is empirical, so it holds or fails under either answer to H. Anomalous experience research is recorded here, never adopted, and its critics are coded beside it by design.

## How the wording changed

Added on 21 November 2026, at Michael Haupt's decision, as the grid wording of Anabranch step T2. The wording is new; it reads T2 ("Information arrives in ways that Material Consciousness has not explained") in the grid's own terms.

Of the 86 grid sources, 12 affirm this step, 9 qualify it and 17 dissent. The other 48 are silent on it.
