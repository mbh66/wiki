Consciousness exists beyond the body.

This is step T1 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Consciousness reaches beyond the body".

## Evidence

Evidence type: Argument, with testimony

Coded: a grid step covers the claim closely.

Drawn from grid step H, consciousness is fundamental. A source can hold that consciousness is fundamental without holding that it exists outside a body, so each position is checked against this wording. Thirty-one sources are now coded directly. James's Human Immortality (1898) argues only that survival is compatible with the brain's role, and Myers's Human Personality (1903) argues that personality survives bodily death. Wilber holds consciousness to be nondual Spirit with no argument for individual survival, and qualifies the step. Chalmers, Deacon, Hoffman, Barfield, Gebser, Varela and colleagues and Medawar say nothing on survival. Seth and Dennett tie experience to the living body. Feinberg and Mallatt, Ginsburg and Jablonka, Jaynes and Lewis-Williams give production accounts that leave nothing beyond it, and Churchland (2013), Lamont (1935) and Paul Edwards's Immortality (1992) argue against survival from the dependence of mind on brain. One position, Laszlo's, is still copied from H.
