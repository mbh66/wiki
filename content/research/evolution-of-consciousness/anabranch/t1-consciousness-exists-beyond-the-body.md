Consciousness exists beyond the body.

This is step T1 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Part 1: Consciousness is non-material".

## Evidence

Evidence type: Argument, with testimony

Coded: a grid step covers the claim closely.

Drawn from grid step H, consciousness is fundamental. On 25 November 2026 a further 156 sources were coded silent from their source pages alone, graded [TBV], for works that never take up the step; 189 sources now carry a code here, and 14 works await a research run. A source can hold that consciousness is fundamental without holding that it exists outside a body, so each position is checked against this wording. Thirty-two sources are now coded directly, and none is still copied from H. James's Human Immortality (1898) argues only that survival is compatible with the brain's role, and Myers's Human Personality (1903) argues that personality survives bodily death. Wilber holds consciousness to be nondual Spirit with no argument for individual survival, and qualifies the step. Chalmers, Deacon, Hoffman, Barfield, Gebser, Varela and colleagues and Medawar say nothing on survival. Seth and Dennett tie experience to the living body. Feinberg and Mallatt, Ginsburg and Jablonka, Jaynes and Lewis-Williams give production accounts that leave nothing beyond it, and Churchland (2013), Lamont (1935) and Paul Edwards's Immortality (1992) argue against survival from the dependence of mind on brain. Laszlo's Science and the Akashic Field (2004) qualifies: it holds that experience is conserved in the A-field and that individuals themselves are not immortal.
