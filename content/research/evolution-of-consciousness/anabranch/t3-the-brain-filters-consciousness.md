The human brain filters consciousness from the field and does not produce it. A possible synthesis shows environment, education and enthusiasm (personal interests, passions and desires) shaping what comes through.

This is step T3 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Consciousness reaches beyond the body". The connecting word from the step before is *so*.

## Evidence

Evidence type: Theory. The three Es are Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS].

Partly coded: a grid step covers part of the claim.

Grid step H covers the claim that brains do not produce consciousness. James's Human Immortality (1898), Bergson's Matter and Memory (1896) and Myers's Human Personality (1903) are the primary filter texts, Huxley's reducing valve (1954) is its best-known modern statement, and Kelly and colleagues (2007) defend the filter model. Kastrup (2019), REBUS, McGilchrist (2021), James's Varieties, Aurobindo, Chalmers and Webb (2023) qualify it; Webb questions how Huxley built the valve from Bergson and Broad. Augustine and Martin (2015), Churchland (2013), Lamont (1935), Paul Edwards's Immortality (1992), Lewis-Williams, Seth, Dennett, Deacon, Feinberg and Mallatt, and Ginsburg and Jablonka hold that brains give rise to consciousness. No critic found so far presses the point that the founders of filter theory gave analogies and no mechanism. Six positions are still copied from H.
