The human brain filters consciousness from the field and does not produce it. Environment, education and enthusiasm (personal interests, passions and desires) shape what comes through.

This is step T3 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Consciousness reaches beyond the body". The connecting word from the step before is *so*.

## Evidence

Evidence type: Theory. The three Es are Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS].

Partly coded: a grid step covers part of the claim.

Grid step H covers the claim that brains do not produce consciousness. Filter theory itself has few direct codes: REBUS, McGilchrist (2021) and James's Varieties qualify it, read from summaries, and Augustine and Martin (2015) dissent. James's Human Immortality (1898), Bergson, Myers, Huxley and Kastrup are not yet coded.
