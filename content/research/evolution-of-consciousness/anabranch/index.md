## What is an anabranch?

- In hydrology, an [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|anabranch]] is a stretch of river that leaves the main channel and rejoins it downstream. The Anabranch of Consciousness thesis interprets the last 12,000 years of human consciousness in the same way: as a river with two channels. Human consciousness exists beyond the human brain, and every individual has access to all earlier forms throughout three million years of development.
- The main channel in the Anabranch of Consciousness is [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]]: grounded, place-based thinking, knowledge held in relationship with a particular place, and tested against the physical constraints of the place.
- At the [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]], over the last 12,000 years, from the Neolithic through writing, the early state, and the scientific revolution, [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] branched away from Municipal Consciousness, making knowledge general and measurable so that it could extend globally. It rose slowly at first. After the scientific revolution, it drove the steep climb of the [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|growth curve]], and in that climb it became globalized thinking that pursued growth at any cost. Both channels have served humanity well.
- As growth meets its physical limits, the thesis interprets the present disruptive period as the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]]: the two channels learning to work side by side, rooted in place and connected across the planet, and braiding into [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]]. Together, the thesis claims, they can create the conditions for thriving.
- [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] belong to the peoples who hold them. They stay in the evidence, coded in their own terms, and the thesis gives them none of its own names.

The thesis consists of nine sub-claims, each with an extensive library of evidence, for and against. Eight steps draw on the grid, where every source is coded, critics included; T2 draws on step H2, added to the grid for it, and every source is also coded on T2 directly. T9, which no grid step tests, is coded directly against its own wording, with its critics. Since Anabranch runs 7a and 7b (22 November 2026), every position on every step is coded directly against the step's own wording, and none is still copied from the grid.

## Where the thesis comes from

The river reading, the nine steps and their wording are Michael Haupt's [MS]. They build on work the network credits elsewhere:

- The form of a claim chain tested against named sources follows Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* (*The Anomaly Explained*, The Leading Edge). Morgan calls the meeting point of his argument "the Anomaly". The Anabranch uses its own names.
- The sequence of structures (Mimetic, Magical, Mythic, Material, Mycelial) builds on Roger Briggs's synthesis, as the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/index|evolution of consciousness research]] sets out.
- Reading the Garden of Eden as the S-curve is Michael's telling, one among others.
- The logistic curve was first described by Pierre-François Verhulst (1838). The [[frameworks/s-curve-thesis|S-Curve Thesis]] sets out how the network applies it.

The structures are present in every person now. The thesis places no people, culture or person at any point on the river or the curve.

## Provenance

Created on 19 November 2026 from Michael Haupt's statement of the Anabranch thesis [MS]. The names Anabranch, Bifurcation, Confluence and two streams were chosen the same day, in place of Tom Morgan's "Anomaly". The evidence types, statuses and grid crosswalks on the step pages are working codes; a third research run will check every position against the thesis wording and add the evidence the grid lacks. On 20 November 2026 the introduction's last sentence changed to match the new wording of T9; it read "the two streams flow back together in place". Later the same day the introduction gained the date of the Bifurcation and the slow start of the climb. On 21 November 2026 the site title changed from "Anabranch" to "The Anabranch of Consciousness", and the site subtitle, which read "Two streams of consciousness, and the evidence for each step of the argument", was rewritten to date the split and to name the Momentous Leap. On 22 November 2026 the introduction and the site subtitle were rewritten from Michael Haupt's new statement of the thesis, after he renamed the two streams: the main channel is now Municipal Consciousness, grounded, place-based thinking, in place of Indigenous Knowledge Systems as the Golden Thread, which the thesis retired. The dating of the Bifurcation follows the new wording of T7. The subtitle read "Over three million years, human consciousness has progressed through a sequence of transitions. The Anabranch interprets the most recent 12,000 years as two separate streams. If the evolutionary impulse strives toward wholeness, the evidence tested here (critics included) offers a basis for navigating the current transition, from Material Consciousness to Mycelial Consciousness." On 22 November 2026 a sentence was added after Anabranch runs 7a and 7b: no position on any step is still copied from the grid. The same day the section "The sources in numbers" was added, generated from the source pages, and the introduction dropped its fixed count, which read "86 sources are coded".
