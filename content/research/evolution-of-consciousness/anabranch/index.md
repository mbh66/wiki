## What is an anabranch?

- In hydrology, an [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|anabranch]] is a stretch of river that leaves the main channel and rejoins it downstream. The Anabranch of Consciousness thesis reads the last 12,000 years of human consciousness the same way, as a river with two channels. Over three million years, human consciousness has passed through a sequence of transitions, and its earlier forms stay present in everyone.
- The main channel is [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]]: grounded, place-based thinking, knowledge held in relationship with a particular place and tested against what that place can bear.
- At the [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]], over the last 12,000 years, from the Neolithic through writing, the early state and the scientific revolution, [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] branched away from it, making knowledge general and measurable so that it could travel. It rose slowly at first. After the scientific revolution it drove the steep climb of the [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|growth curve]], and in that climb it became globalized thinking that pursued growth at any cost. Both channels have served us well.
- As growth meets its limits, the thesis reads the present as the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]]: the two channels learning to work side by side, rooted in place and connected across the planet, and braiding into [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]]. Together, the thesis holds, they can create the conditions for thriving.
- [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] belong to the peoples who hold them. They stay in the evidence, coded in their own terms, and the thesis gives them none of its own names.

The thesis consists of nine steps. Each step names the kind of evidence it rests on. Eight steps draw on the grid, where 86 sources are coded, critics included; T2 draws on step H2, added to the grid for it, and every source is also coded on T2 directly. T9, which no grid step tests, is coded directly against its own wording, with its critics.

## Where the thesis comes from

The river reading, the nine steps and their wording are Michael Haupt's [MS]. They build on work the network credits elsewhere:

- The form of a claim chain tested against named sources follows Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* (*The Anomaly Explained*, The Leading Edge). Morgan calls the meeting point of his argument "the Anomaly". The Anabranch uses its own names.
- The sequence of structures (Mimetic, Magical, Mythic, Material, Mycelial) builds on Roger Briggs's synthesis, as the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/index|evolution of consciousness research]] sets out.
- Reading the Garden of Eden as the S-curve is Michael's telling, one among others.
- The logistic curve was first described by Pierre-François Verhulst (1838). The [[frameworks/s-curve-thesis|S-Curve Thesis]] sets out how the network applies it.

The structures are present in every person now. The thesis places no people, culture or person at any point on the river or the curve.

## Provenance

Created on 19 November 2026 from Michael Haupt's statement of the Anabranch thesis [MS]. The names Anabranch, Bifurcation, Confluence and two streams were chosen the same day, in place of Tom Morgan's "Anomaly". The evidence types, statuses and grid crosswalks on the step pages are working codes; a third research run will check every position against the thesis wording and add the evidence the grid lacks. On 20 November 2026 the introduction's last sentence changed to match the new wording of T9; it read "the two streams flow back together in place". Later the same day the introduction gained the date of the Bifurcation and the slow start of the climb. On 21 November 2026 the site title changed from "Anabranch" to "The Anabranch of Consciousness", and the site subtitle, which read "Two streams of consciousness, and the evidence for each step of the argument", was rewritten to date the split and to name the Momentous Leap. On 22 November 2026 the introduction and the site subtitle were rewritten from Michael Haupt's new statement of the thesis, after he renamed the two streams: the main channel is now Municipal Consciousness, grounded, place-based thinking, in place of Indigenous Knowledge Systems as the Golden Thread, which the thesis retired. The dating of the Bifurcation follows the new wording of T7. The subtitle read "Over three million years, human consciousness has progressed through a sequence of transitions. The Anabranch interprets the most recent 12,000 years as two separate streams. If the evolutionary impulse strives toward wholeness, the evidence tested here (critics included) offers a basis for navigating the current transition, from Material Consciousness to Mycelial Consciousness."
