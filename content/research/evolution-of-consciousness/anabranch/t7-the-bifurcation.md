At the Mythic to Material transition, consciousness split into two streams, as the Garden of Eden story tells it. [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] held Gaia wisdom as the [[glossary/f-l/golden-thread|Golden Thread]]. [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] drove the species up the steep part of the S-shaped [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|Logistic Growth Principle]].

This is step T7 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "The Anabranch". The connecting word from the step before is *together*. The [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] is the first of the two moments of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: Dated record and argument. The S-curve reading is Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS].

Partly coded: a grid step covers part of the claim.

Drawn from grid step A3, the structure of awareness changed, with C1, earlier structures stay present, for the Golden Thread. Modis (2002) and Smil (2019) fit logistic curves to complexity and to empires. No source read in its text, Briggs's Emerging World (2021) included, ties the steep part of a civilisational S-curve to Material Consciousness; that link is Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS].
