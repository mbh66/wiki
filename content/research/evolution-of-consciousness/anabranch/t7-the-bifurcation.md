At the Mythic to Material transition, consciousness split into two streams, as one possible interpretation of the Garden of Eden story tells it. [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] held Gaia wisdom as the [[glossary/f-l/golden-thread|Golden Thread]]. [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] drove the species up the steep part of the S-shaped [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|Logistic Growth Principle]].

This is step T7 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "The Anabranch". The connecting word from the step before is *together*. The [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] is the first of the two moments of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: Dated record and argument. The S-curve reading is Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS].

Partly coded: a grid step covers part of the claim.

Drawn from grid step A3, the structure of awareness changed, with C1, earlier structures stay present, for the Golden Thread. Thirty sources are coded directly, three of them silent, and four positions are still copied from A3. No source affirms all three parts. On the split, part (a), Jaynes, Ong, Jaspers, Gebser, Barfield, McGilchrist, Wilber and Briggs describe a transition in mind or culture and speak to no other part, so each qualifies; Block (1981), Street (1984) and Graeber and Wengrow (2021) dispute such a transition. Eisler (1987), Taylor (2005) and Quinn's The Story of B (1996) argue for a break some thousands of years ago, and Trible (1978) reads Eden as a love story gone awry. Eller (2000) disputes the lost partnership age behind such readings, and Barr (1992), Fretheim (1994) and Meyers (2013) read Genesis 2 and 3 without a split in consciousness. On part (b), Wilson (2008), Odora Hoppers (2002) and Deloria (1995) speak of the standing of Indigenous knowledge in their own terms, beside South Africa's Indigenous Knowledge Systems policy (2004). On part (c), Modis (2002) and Smil (2019) fit logistic curves to complexity and to empires. No source read in its text, Briggs's Emerging World (2021) included, ties the steep part of a civilisational S-curve to Material Consciousness; that link is Michael Haupt's synthesis [MS]. Material Consciousness branches at the Bifurcation, with the Neolithic Revolution about 12,000 years ago, and rises slowly while Mythic structures still lead coordination; it drives the steep part of the curve once it comes to lead, which the Major Evolutionary Transitions page dates from the scientific revolution [MS].
