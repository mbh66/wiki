Over the last 12,000 years, from the Neolithic through writing, the early state and the scientific revolution, abstract thinking branched away from grounded, place-based thinking; the thesis offers one reading of the Garden of Eden story as an image of this. Place-based thinking carried on as [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]]. The thesis reads [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] as the force that drove the species up the steep part of the S-shaped [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|Logistic Growth Principle]].

This is step T7 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "The Anabranch". The connecting word from the step before is *together*. The [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] is the first of the two moments of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]].
