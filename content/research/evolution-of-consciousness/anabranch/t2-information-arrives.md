Information arrives in ways that [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] has not explained.

This is step T2 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Consciousness reaches beyond the body". The connecting word from the step before is *and*.

## Evidence

Evidence type: Measured studies (E1), contested by failed replications and critiques, with testimony (E5)

Coded: grid step [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/steps/h2-information-not-yet-explained|H2]] covers the claim, and every source is also coded on it directly, each against the wording above.

Drawn from grid step H2, added on 21 November 2026 as T2's grid wording, and coded directly on every source as well. Of 141 sources, fourteen affirm, twelve qualify and seventeen dissent; 98 are silent, 48 of them grid sources with no position on H2. The supporters are the experimental record (Bem; Storm and colleagues; Mossbridge and colleagues; Cardeña), Myers, Kelly and colleagues, William James's Human Immortality, Laszlo, Aurobindo, Gebser, Briggs, Bache, the Ra Material and The Telepathy Tapes. Hyman (2010) and Rouder and colleagues (2013) answer the ganzfeld meta-analysis, and Ritchie, Wiseman and French report failed replications. Lamont, Paul Edwards and Augustine and Martin argue from the dependence of mind on brain. Weiler and Woollacott (2025) call for controlled tests of the claims in The Telepathy Tapes, and Delgado-Ron (2026) replies. Beals, Boynton, Hupp and Vyse answer the podcast in Skeptical Inquirer 50(5), and the Association for Science in Autism Treatment rejects its claims. Danny Whitty, a nonspeaking writer, trusts peers who report telepathy and objects that the podcast harms access to spelling. Critics outnumber supporters, seventeen to fourteen.

## Where the sources stand

The positions, source by source, are on the [Anabranch site](https://anabranch.bioconomy.earth). Anomalous experience research is recorded there, never adopted, and its critics are coded beside it by design.
