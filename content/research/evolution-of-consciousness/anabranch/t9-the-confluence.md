Now the two streams are learning to work side by side, towards place-based wholeness. Bioregionalism is how that looks on the ground.

This is step T9 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "The Anabranch". The connecting word from the step before is *pointing towards*. The [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]] is the second of the two moments of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]].

## Evidence

Evidence type: Theory on evidence (E3), with national policy (E2)

Partly coded: no grid step tests this claim, so sources are coded on it directly, each against the wording above.

No grid step tests this. Sources are coded on it directly: work on Two-Eyed Seeing, on bioregionalism and on place-based and global knowledge, with their critics. It is also the position the BioConomy network writes from. The count is 5 affirms, 14 qualifies and 5 dissents. The affirms all come from the first run on this step; Odora Hoppers (2002) argues for joining indigenous knowledge systems with the sciences through what her volume calls articulation. The run of 21 November 2026 added ten works, and none affirms the step as worded. Quilligan (2026), Wynants (2026), Life (2026), Ronfeldt (2026, paper and post), Massey (1991), Escobar (2001), Tsing (2005) and Harvey (1996) each join place with wider scales, but name another form for it or leave Indigenous knowledge out, and qualify. Born and Purcell (2006) dissent: no scale is good in itself. The same run tested two readings of the streams. On the claim that the knowledge behind science, the state and the market works by abstraction from place, five works affirm (Scott, Porter, Escobar, Life, the Cosmo-Local Reader), four qualify (Latour, Tsing, Harvey, Quilligan) and none dissents. On the claim that place-based and globally shared knowledge are now being joined, seven affirm (Kostakis and colleagues in 2015 and 2023, Bauwens, Kostakis and Pazaitis, the Cosmo-Local Reader, Life, Escobar, Quilligan) and nine qualify; most of the affirming works come from authors close to the network. A new wording built on these readings is before Michael Haupt.
