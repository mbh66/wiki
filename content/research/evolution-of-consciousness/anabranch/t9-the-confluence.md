Now the two streams are learning to work side by side, rooted in place and connected across the planet, and together they can create the conditions for thriving. [[glossary/a-e/bioregionalism|Bioregional]] and [[glossary/a-e/cosmo-local-production|cosmo-local]] commons are two of the forms this takes on the ground.

This is step T9 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], Michael Haupt's position [MS], in the part headed "Part 3: Two streams of thinking, rejoining". The connecting word from the step before is *pointing towards*. The [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]] is the second of the two moments of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]].

## Evidence
