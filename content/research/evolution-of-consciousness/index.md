This folder holds the wiki's research on whether human consciousness has a history, and on the evidence for the sequence the network writes from.

## Overview

The network reads human history through the [[concepts/major-evolutionary-transitions|Major Evolutionary Transitions]]: five structures of consciousness (Mimetic, Magical, Mythic, Material and Mycelial) joined by four transitions, with the fourth under way. That sequence is Michael Haupt's synthesis, built on Roger Briggs [MS]. The structures are present in every person now. The sequence never ranks one people or person above another.

What does the sequence rest on? Which parts are measured, which are dated, which are argued, and which come from tradition? Where do the sources part ways? The pages here set out to answer those questions with sources a reader can check.

## In this folder

- [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|The Evolution of Consciousness: A Graded Library]]. 95 works coded by tier, evidence type and the claims each speaks to, with coverage tests and the gaps that remain.

A claim grid, setting the sources against each step of the argument and including the critics, and a dating table, setting each source's dates beside the archaeological record, will follow from a second research run.
