The Major Evolutionary Transitions (MET) is the BioConomy corpus's developmental sequence for human consciousness and coordination: five structures (Mimetic, Magical, Mythic, Material, and Mycelial) joined by four transitions. The fourth transition, from Material to Mycelial, is currently underway.

## Overview

The MET sequence maps the evolution of human consciousness across civilizational scale, drawing on and integrating the developmental frameworks of [[sources/gebser-ever-present-origin|Jean Gebser]], [[momentous-leap|Clare Graves]], [[sources/kegan-in-over-our-heads|Robert Kegan]], and [[sources/sahtouris-earthdance|Elisabet Sahtouris]]. Each transition changes what humans know and how they organize. It also changes the structure of consciousness itself, producing different relationships to space, time, causality, production, and exchange.

The structures are co-present. Following Gebser, whose title *The Ever-Present Origin* names the point, every earlier structure remains active in every person living now. The sequence describes which structure dominates coordination at civilizational scale. It does not place any living people or culture at an earlier point on the curve.

The sequence is mapped onto the logistic growth curve ([[s-curve|S-curve]]) of human civilization since the Neolithic Revolution, approximately 12,000 years ago. The first three transitions, from Mimetic through to Material, carry human coordination through the lag phase and up the steep ascent of the curve. The fourth, from Material to [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial]], corresponds to the inflection point where the curve bends into its deceleration phase.

The name follows John Maynard Smith and Eörs Szathmáry's *The Major Transitions in Evolution* (1995), which describes eight transitions in biological organization, from replicating molecules to human societies with language. In each, units that could once live apart join into a larger whole. [[briggs-roger|Roger Briggs]] extended the idea to human consciousness as Cognitive METs, and the sequence here adopts and extends his synthesis.

The MET framework is original to this corpus, synthesizing convergent evidence from multiple independent developmental traditions into a single civilizational sequence. The component frameworks are well-established in their respective disciplines; the integration across them is the original contribution.

## The Five Structures

### Mimetic

The earliest form of human coordination, operating through imitation, embodied knowledge, and direct physical transmission. Consciousness is undifferentiated from the group and from nature. Coordination is achieved through shared gesture, ritual movement, and the bodily enactment of meaning. In Gebser's terms, this corresponds to the archaic and early magical structures. In [[frameworks/time-framework|Ronfeldt's TIME framework]], the Mimetic phase precedes even the Tribal form.

### Magical

Consciousness begins to differentiate from nature but remains embedded in collective participation. The world is experienced as animate, responsive, and interpenetrated by human intention. Coordination operates through kinship, totemic identification, and ritual practice. Gebser's magical structure. The TIME Tribal form (+T) emerges here, with sacred sites as the characteristic nodes.

### Mythic

Consciousness achieves narrative structure. The capacity to organize experience through story, symbol, and cosmological framework produces the great civilizations of antiquity. Coordination operates through shared myth, priestly hierarchy, and the identification of political authority with cosmic order. Gebser's mythical structure. The TIME Institutional form (+I) emerges, with cathedrals and temples as characteristic nodes. Culture-dominant empires (Egypt, Sumer, Babylon) organize the steep ascent of the civilizational S-curve under Mythic consciousness.

### Material

Consciousness achieves perspectival, analytical, and abstracting capacity. The mental-rational structure in Gebser's terms. The capacity to stand outside nature, outside tradition, and outside the group produces the scientific revolution, individual rights, market economics, and the progressive commodification of land, labor, and money. The TIME Market form (+M) emerges, with cities and exchanges as characteristic nodes. Economy-dominant empires (Portuguese, Dutch, British, American) organize the peak and overshoot of the S-curve.

[[material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] privileges individual intelligence applied to commodified labor. Under this form, Steiner's V2 (the application of Spirit to Labor) operates as concentrated, individual intelligence producing productivity gains captured as private capital. This is the form of consciousness that has governed the last several centuries of civilization and is now reaching the structural limits described by the [[frameworks/three-futures|Three Futures]] analysis.

### Mycelial

The fifth structure, emerging through the fourth transition. Consciousness achieves aperspectival, relational, and distributed capacity. Gebser's integral structure. Graves's [[second-tier|Second Tier]]. Kegan's self-transforming mind. The capacity to hold multiple perspectives simultaneously without collapsing into any single one, and to participate in distributed intelligence without needing to control it.

[[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] draws its name from the biological template of mycorrhizal networks, which coordinate resource distribution, chemical signaling, and adaptive learning across forest ecosystems without central command. The TIME Emergent form (+E) takes shape under this consciousness, with bioregions as the characteristic nodes.

Under Mycelial Consciousness, V2 transforms structurally: distributed intelligence applied to ecological participation, producing regenerative capacity that circulates as relational wealth. The [[frameworks/farmer-forger-founder-framer|Farmer-Forger-Founder-Framer]] sequence maps the progressive abstraction of production across the MET transitions.

## The Convergence Table

The corpus demonstrates that multiple independent frameworks, developed from different disciplinary starting points, converge on the same developmental sequence:

- **Gebser's structures**: Archaic, Magical, Mythical, Mental-Rational, Integral
- **Graves's levels**: Beige/Purple (survival/kinship), Red/Blue (power/order), Orange/Green (achievement/community), Yellow/Turquoise (systemic/holistic)
- **Kegan's orders**: Impulsive, Imperial, Interpersonal, Institutional, Inter-individual
- **Steiner's epochs**: mapped through soul faculty and dominant sphere
- **Ronfeldt's TIME framework**: Tribal, Institutional, Market, Emergent (formerly TIMN)
- **Holling's adaptive cycle**: growth (r), conservation (K), release (omega), reorganization (alpha)
- **Sahtouris's maturation**: competition-dominant (young ecosystem) to cooperation-dominant (mature ecosystem)

Each framework arrives at the same structural observation from its own evidence base: human civilization is at a developmental threshold, and the consciousness adequate to the emerging conditions is qualitatively different from the consciousness that produced the current system.

## The S-Curve and the Current Threshold

[[briggs-roger|Roger P. Briggs]]' application of the logistic growth principle to civilizational scale provides the quantitative frame. Logistic growth is a model fitted to a system's record: it describes the shape of growth within finite conditions, and it does not predict collapse. Briggs reads the slowdown in energy returns, demographic transitions and debt ratios as evidence that the 12,000-year growth curve since the Neolithic Revolution has entered its deceleration phase. If that reading holds, the growth assumption embedded in every pension fund, business plan, national budget, and retirement strategy rests on a phase that is ending.

The fourth transition, from the fourth structure to the fifth (Material to Mycelial), corresponds to the inflection point on this curve. Past it, the competitive, extractive logic that drove the steep ascent works against the conditions of deceleration. Sahtouris's biological precedent is that young ecosystems are dominated by competition and mature ecosystems by mutual aid. The corpus reads the shift from Material to Mycelial Consciousness as what the deceleration phase requires of human coordination.

## Epistemic Status

The MET sequence is a structural inference: each component framework is independently documented, and the convergence across them is the corpus's original synthesis. The mapping of these frameworks onto a single developmental sequence, and the claim that the fourth transition is currently underway, bridges between literatures that do not themselves make the connection explicit.
