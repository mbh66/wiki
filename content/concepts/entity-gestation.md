[[glossary/a-e/entity-gestation|Entity gestation]] is a fixed period at the founding of a [[biohub|BioHub]] in which the cohort works under a signed compact and registers no legal entity, choosing its entity form at the end of the period, once the place has said what work the entity is for.

## Overview

A founding cohort faces the entity question early. The [[templates/biohub-identity-template|BioHub Identity Template]] asks for it in Prompt 1, alongside the name and the patronage architecture. The usual answer is to register something straight away: a non-profit company, a cooperative, a trust. Registration feels like progress, and many funders ask for a registered recipient.

Entity form decides a great deal. It sets who governs, what the body can own, what capital it can accept, what it must report and to whom. A form chosen before the place has spoken is chosen on assumptions: the cohort's own, a funder's requirements, or a template from elsewhere. That is the pattern [[concepts/direction-of-fit|Direction of Fit]] warns about, applied to the body itself. The place's work ends up fitting the entity's objects clause.

Gestation reverses the order. For a set period, counted from the cohort's first meeting, the cohort works as a group bound by its [[founding-compact|Founding Compact]] and holds nothing that needs a legal person to hold it. During that time the cohort listens, runs the templates and gathers the [[place-knowledge-brief|Place Knowledge Brief]]. At the end it chooses an entity to carry the work the place has stated. Nine months is a workable length. It is long enough for listening sessions across several settlements and short enough that the question does not drift.

This page sets out the choice from a clean slate: register at once or gestate, the forms a gestation can take, how the period ends, and a worked example of the legal detail in one jurisdiction.

## Register now, or gestate

Registering at once suits a cohort that already has a job only an entity can do:

- Land or buildings to hold.
- People to employ.
- A contract or a patron's capital that needs a registered counterparty, and that has passed the relationship check in Direction of Fit.

Gestation suits a cohort that does not yet have such a job:

- The place has not yet stated its work.
- The cohort is newly formed and its members' roles are still settling.
- No money needs holding in the BioHub's name.

A cohort may also choose gestation because registration pressure is coming from a funder. Gestation removes the deadline, and the relationship can be tested when an entity exists to hold it.

## Forms a gestation can take

| | 1. Compact only | 2. Constituted association | 3. Registered host | 4. Working group of a steering body |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | The Founding Compact as a signed agreement among the founders | An unregistered association with a written constitution giving it a life separate from its members | An existing registered body holds any funds for the cohort under a written agreement | The cohort works as a group inside the body that steers its work |
| Can hold money or property | No | Yes, where the constitution provides for it | Through the host | Through the steering body, if it can |
| Can contract in its own name | No | Yes, where the constitution provides for it | No | No |
| Who carries liability | Whoever signs | The association, within its constitution | The host, for what it holds | The steering body, or whoever signs |
| Main risk | Personal liability for anything signed | Becomes a de facto entity before the place has spoken | The host's scope, reporting and governance come with the money | Merges steering and coordination |
| Ends by | Registering an entity, or dissolving under the Compact | Converting, registering, or dissolving under its constitution | Ending the hosting agreement | The steering body's decision |

### 1. Compact only

The founders sign the Founding Compact as an agreement among themselves. The BioHub holds no money and signs nothing in its own name. What the cohort commits in kind runs through [[commitment-pool|commitment pools]], which need no entity. This is the lightest form and the one most faithful to the purpose of gestation. Its limit is liability: anyone who signs for the cohort signs personally, so the cohort keeps its obligations close to nil.

### 2. Constituted association

The cohort adopts a written constitution without registering a company. In several legal systems, an association whose constitution gives it perpetual succession and the power to own property apart from its members is a legal person at common law. It can open a bank account and contract in its own name, and in some jurisdictions it can register as a nonprofit or apply for tax exemption. This gives the most capacity short of registration. The cost is that the cohort writes a constitution before the place has spoken, which is the step gestation exists to postpone. It is best kept in reserve, for when a real need to hold money or sign a contract arises before the period ends.

### 3. Registered host

An existing registered organization receives and accounts for funds on the cohort's behalf under a written hosting agreement, and the cohort keeps its own decisions. The practice is common and is often called fiscal hosting or fiscal sponsorship. The host's governance, reporting and scope travel with the money, so the agreement goes through the relationship check in Direction of Fit before it is signed. A host is named only after it has been asked.

### 4. Working group of a steering body

Where a local forum or council already steers the work, the cohort can gestate as its working group. The arrangement is simple, and it places the BioHub inside the body meant to steer it at the moment the Founding Compact is meant to separate the two. It also depends on the steering body's own legal standing.

## What gestation rules out, and what it makes possible

During gestation the BioHub cannot take grants that require a registered recipient, issue tax receipts to donors, or hold capital in a commons trust as [[concepts/mycelial-patronage|Mycelial Patronage]] requires. Any of these that the cohort wants waits for the end of the period, or goes through a host under form 3.

Gestation makes three things possible that early registration forecloses. The entity's objects, governance and land-holding layer can be written from what the place has said. Questions the cohort does not own, such as a pending land transfer or a matter before a custodial authority, stay out of the entity's design until the parties who own them have decided. And the first months pass without an entity's compliance load, which leaves the cohort's time for listening.

## Ending the gestation

The Founding Compact records, at the start, the gestation form, the date of the first meeting, the length of the period, and the criteria the cohort will use to choose an entity at the end. Useful criteria:

- Has the Place Knowledge Brief been accepted by the place's ratifying authorities?
- Which functions must the entity carry: land, money, employment, contracts?
- What capital does the place intend to hold in the commons, and in what form?
- What do the BioRegion's other BioHubs and its Charter require?
- What does a legal opinion on the candidate forms say?

At the end the cohort chooses one of four courses and records the choice:

- Register the entity the criteria point to.
- Continue as a constituted association, where that proves sufficient.
- Extend the period once, by cohort decision, with the reason recorded.
- Conclude the BioHub under the Compact's dissolution provision.

A cohort that expects to register under a particular name protects it during the period, where its registry allows reservations.

## Worked example: South Africa

The detail varies by jurisdiction. In South Africa it runs as follows; every point needs confirming by a legal opinion before a cohort relies on it.

- **Companies.** A non-profit company registers under the Companies Act 71 of 2008 with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC). A company name reservation lasts six months from approval and cannot be extended; an expired name has to be reserved again [TBV against CIPC's own rules]. A nine-month gestation therefore needs a second reservation, or registration under the name if it is still free at the end.
- **Constituted associations.** South African common law treats a voluntary association as a legal person (a *universitas*) when its constitution provides for perpetual succession and the capacity to hold property apart from its members. The courts have applied this test in cases such as *Ex parte Doornfontein-Judiths Paarl Ratepayers Association* and *Mitchell's Plain Town Centre Merchants Association v McLeod* [TBV for the citations and holdings].
- **Nonprofit registration.** An association, trust or non-profit company can register with the Department of Social Development under the Nonprofit Organisations Act 71 of 1997. Registration was voluntary for most organizations; amendments since 2022 made it compulsory for some [TBV for the current scope].
- **Tax exemption.** SARS lists a signed constitution for an association of persons among the founding documents it accepts for public benefit organization applications, alongside trust deeds and company memoranda [IC, SARS Income Tax Exemption Leaflet 004]. Approval to issue receipts that donors can deduct under section 18A of the Income Tax Act is a further step [TBV].
- **Liability.** Members of an association without legal personality are liable for obligations they incur on its behalf. This is why form 1 keeps obligations close to nil.

## In the founding suite

- **BioHub Identity Template, Prompt 1.** Gestation is a candidate under entity form, assessed alongside registered forms, with its gestation form named.
- **Founding Compact, section 3.** Records the gestation form, the start date, the length and the end criteria. Section 11, on dissolution, is simpler while nothing is held.
- **Place Mandate Template, Step 0.** The listening sessions that produce the Place Knowledge Brief fit inside a nine-month period, so the entity decision can follow the Brief.
