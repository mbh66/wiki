This wiki documents a coordination architecture that spans ecological science, monetary theory, institutional design, commons governance, and bioregional economics. The body of work is large, internally cross-referenced, and draws on source material from disciplines that rarely speak to one another. Producing it without generative AI would have taken years longer and cost orders of magnitude more.

## How This Content Was Made

Generative AI (primarily Anthropic's Claude) produced much of the wiki's text, working from the author's frameworks, source annotations, and editorial direction. The author reviewed and approved every published page. Factual claims trace to cited sources. Structural relationships between concepts reflect the author's intellectual architecture, not the model's training distribution.

The glossary, which defines the controlled vocabulary used across both the wiki and the BioHubs sites, was written collaboratively: the author supplied the definitions and distinctions; AI drafted the entries to a consistent format; the author verified each one.

Research briefs followed a similar pattern. The author identified the question, supplied or directed the model to specific sources, and checked the resulting analysis against those sources. Where the model lacked access to a source, the author provided the relevant passages directly.

## Why the Prose Is Functional, Not Polished

The wiki is designed to be machine-readable. Its primary use case is as a source corpus: practitioners feed wiki pages into AI platforms alongside their own local documents to generate outputs tailored to their bioregion. The [[essays/ai-quick-engage|AI Quick-Engage]] essay and the [[templates/running-a-template|founding-suite templates]] are built around this workflow.

Because the wiki's readers are frequently AI systems generating refined output for human readers downstream, editorial effort has gone into structural consistency, vocabulary precision, and cross-referencing rather than literary polish. Pages are accurate, clearly structured, and internally consistent. They are not optimized for the experience of reading them straight through.

## What This Means for You

If you are reading the wiki directly, you will find the prose serviceable but sometimes mechanical. That is a deliberate allocation of effort, not an oversight. The controlled vocabulary in the [[glossary/|glossary]] and the cross-linking between pages carry more of the wiki's value than any individual page's sentence-level style.

If you are feeding wiki pages into an AI platform, the structural consistency and precise vocabulary will produce better downstream output than more literary source material would. The wiki was built for this.

## Verification and Limits

Every page has been reviewed by the author. That review covers factual accuracy, structural coherence, and consistency with the wiki's frameworks and controlled vocabulary. It does not guarantee that every sentence reads the way the author would have written it from scratch.

The wiki's source annotations (in [[sources/|Sources]]) state what each cited work argues and why it matters to this corpus. Those annotations are the author's interpretive positions, checked against the source material, not neutral summaries.

Where the wiki states a framework or thesis as its own contribution (the [[frameworks/emancipation-architecture|Emancipation Architecture]], the [[concepts/substrate-hypothesis|Substrate Hypothesis]], the [[frameworks/time-framework|TIME Framework]] extensions), the intellectual content is the author's. AI served as a drafting and structuring tool, not as a co-author.
