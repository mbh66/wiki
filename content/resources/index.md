Collections of books, papers, videos, websites and interviews, one page per subject, for practitioners who want to see a practice at work before reading the evidence for it.

## Overview

The [[sources/index|Sources]] folder holds one annotated page per work the wiki cites, most of them peer-reviewed or published books. Resource pages are looser. They gather practitioner films, talks, project websites, interviews and short posts alongside a few papers, and they grow as Michael finds new material.

Each video on a resource page carries a short summary and a note on what it adds to the wiki: which concept it illustrates, which case it shows on the ground, or which gap in the wiki's coverage it fills. Figures quoted from filmmakers and websites are the maker's own account and carry [TBV] until checked. The argument and the peer-reviewed evidence for each subject stay on its concept or research page, which every resource page links to.
