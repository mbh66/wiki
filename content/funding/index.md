Organizations, programs, and funds whose grant-making or investment mandates overlap with the BioConomy's coordination architecture: bioregional economics, commons governance, regenerative land management, community currency, and related fields.

## Overview

Each funding source page carries a short description of the funder's mandate, geographic and thematic scope, grant mechanics (size, eligibility, cycle), and a note on where the funder's priorities intersect the wiki's subject matter. Pages are listed alphabetically by organization name.

The folder is not a grant database. It documents the structural alignment between a funder's theory of change and the coordination architecture the wiki describes, so that a bioregional practitioner can judge relevance before investing time in application research.

## Where to start
