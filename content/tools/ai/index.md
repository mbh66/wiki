AI-powered tools, skills, and automations used in the construction and maintenance of the BioConomy wiki and its underlying research corpus.

## Overview

The wiki is built by a human author working with AI agents. The tools in this folder document the specific skills, prompts, and workflows that make that collaboration reproducible. Each entry names what the tool does, how it is invoked, what it expects as input, and what it produces.

These are not general-purpose AI tools. They are purpose-built capabilities configured for the wiki's structure, voice, and editorial standards.

## Tools

- [[tools/ai/wiki-extraction|Wiki Extraction]]. Skill for extracting publishable wiki content from a Claude project knowledge base into structured markdown pages.
