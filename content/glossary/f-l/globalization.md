The steep, juvenile phase of the human species' development as one living system: the period of rapid growth in which humanity joined into a single planetary metabolism and built the capacities it needs to mature.

## Extended definition

The standard definition describes globalization as the integration of national economies through trade and capital flow. This wiki reads the same events as a stage in a life cycle. Humanity is treated as one living system, and its history traces the [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|S-curve]] that every growing living system traces within finite conditions. Globalization is the steep part of that curve, the phase in which growth compounds and the system expands into every niche available to it.

The frame comes from [[people/ancestors/sahtouris-elisabet|Elisabet Sahtouris]]'s account of evolution. Young ecosystems and juvenile species grow fast by consuming what is around them and compete hard for it. Mature ecosystems, crowded and constrained, sustain themselves through cooperation and negotiation, and steward the substrate they share. Juvenile growth is a phase every living system passes through on the way to maturity. On this reading globalization is the human species' juvenile growth spurt: competitive and extractive, as juvenile growth is.

In the [[frameworks/cycle-of-globalization|Cycle of Globalization]], this growth runs through the expansion phases from 1815 to the financial crisis of 2008, inside a longer cycle that began with the European settlement of 1648.

The phase was necessary because it built what maturity requires. Before globalization, humanity had no means of acting as one system. The growth phase gave the species a planetary nervous system in its communications networks, a shared memory in open science, common languages in its standards and protocols, and planetary sight through Earth observation and climate science. These capacities let the species perceive itself as one body inside one biosphere, and perceive the limits it has reached. A system that had not grown to its limits could not have learned where they are.

The juvenile pattern becomes harmful when it continues past its phase. Growth strategies that fit the steep part of the curve consume the substrate once the curve flattens. The [[glossary/a-e/deceleration-phase|deceleration phase]] is the point at which the species must change its metabolism from consumption to stewardship, the transition the wiki calls [[glossary/f-l/glocalization|glocalization]]: the mature form that keeps the capacities globalization built and places them under the governance of places.

Calling the phase necessary places it in the developmental sequence. It passes no verdict on the costs that places and peoples bore during it, which the concept page sets out.

## Contrast with adjacent terms

- **Globalization in standard usage**: the integration of trade and capital across borders, judged by output and trade volume. The wiki's reading keeps those facts and adds the developmental frame: what the phase was for, and why it ends.
- **[[glossary/f-l/global-economy|Global economy]]**: the institutional layer globalization built (trade agreements, capital markets, development banks and the reserve currency). Globalization is the phase; the global economy is the structure the phase left standing.
- **[[glossary/f-l/glocalization|Glocalization]]**: the mature phase that follows. Globalization grows the capacities; glocalization stewards them from the places that make up the whole.
- **[[glossary/t-z/throughput-economics|Throughput economics]]**: the economic logic that fits the juvenile phase, measuring value by what flows through a system.
