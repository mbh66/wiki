The Golden Thread is a retired name. From 19 to 22 November 2026 the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] used it for its main channel, which it then read as [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] carrying Gaia wisdom through human history [MS].

On 21 November 2026 Michael Haupt renamed the main channel [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]]: grounded, place-based thinking, knowledge held in relationship with a particular place [MS]. The thesis retired the name Golden Thread the next day. Indigenous Knowledge Systems left the thesis's wording. They stay in its evidence, coded in their own terms, through their own scholars and institutions.

Written on 19 November 2026, when Michael Haupt named the arc of his consciousness thesis the Anabranch, with the Bifurcation and the Confluence as its two moments [MS]. Rewritten on 22 November 2026, when Michael Haupt retired the name after renaming the main channel Municipal Consciousness. The entry is kept so that older links resolve.
