In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], the Golden Thread is [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] seen as the continuous line that has carried Gaia wisdom through all of human history, holding it until the limits of [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] are reached [MS]. It is the main channel of the [[glossary/a-e/anabranch|Anabranch]]: it runs on through the [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] and receives the other stream at the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]].

The term names a continuity in the thesis. The knowledge itself belongs to the peoples who hold it, and the wiki presents it only through their own scholars and institutions, in their own terms.
