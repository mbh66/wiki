Elisabet Sahtouris's keyboard metaphor for science, from her essay A Keyboard Metaphor.

## Extended definition

The metaphor lays the sciences out along a single keyboard. On the reading this entry starts from, Western science plays a narrow range of keys, those of the measurable material world, while indigenous and contemplative traditions play other ranges, and a complete science would use the whole keyboard [TBV]. That reading needs checking against Sahtouris's essay before the entry is expanded.

The wiki's page on [[people/ancestors/sahtouris-elisabet|Elisabet Sahtouris]] credits her with popularizing the concept. It sits beside her co-founding of the Worldwide Indigenous Science Network, and it bears on how the wiki treats [[indigenous-knowledge|indigenous knowledge]] as a science in its own right.
