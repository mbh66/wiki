The economic form of the deceleration phase of the industrial [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|S-curve]]: place-based economies that govern themselves while drawing on the knowledge, standards and instruments globalization made shareable.

## Extended definition

[[concepts/globalization|Globalization]] was the economic form of the acceleration phase, organized around mobile capital and long supply chains across a single planetary market. Glocalization keeps the parts of globalization that every place can share, including open knowledge, communications infrastructure, common standards, planetary observation and cross-border finance directed to place outcomes. It places them under the governance of the [[glossary/a-e/bioregion|bioregion]]. In globalization, places serve the global system. In glocalization, the global layer serves places.

The full treatment, including what glocalization keeps, what it leaves with the acceleration phase and what it means for a BioHub, is on the concept page [[concepts/glocalization|Glocalization]].

## Contrast with adjacent terms

- **Globalization and the [[glossary/f-l/global-economy|global economy]]**: the acceleration-phase form, measured by [[glossary/t-z/throughput|throughput]]. Glocalization belongs to the deceleration phase and is measured by retention.
- **Glocalization in business usage**: a central firm adapting a global product to local markets. Ownership and design stay at the center. This wiki reframes the word for the reverse arrangement, with governance in the place.
- **[[glossary/a-e/cosmo-local-production|Cosmo-local production]]**: the production rule within glocalization (light things global, heavy things local). Glocalization is the wider form, covering governance, finance and measurement as well.
- **Autarky**: a glocal economy trades with the world, and decides what to trade from the standpoint of the place.
