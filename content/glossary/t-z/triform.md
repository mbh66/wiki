A society organized around three coordination forms: kinship (+T), institutional hierarchy (+I), and market exchange (+M). In [[ronfeldt-david|David Ronfeldt]]'s [[time|TIME]] sequence, triform describes the dominant coordination architecture of the modern world. The combination is +T+I+M.

## Extended definition

The triform society is the one most readers of this wiki live inside. Its three coordination layers operate simultaneously: families, clans, and community networks (T); governments, courts, churches, universities, and bureaucracies (I); and firms, banks, stock exchanges, commodity markets, and the global trade system (M). Each layer coordinates a different class of problems using a different logic (kinship, hierarchy, price), and the health of the whole depends on all three remaining vigorous. Ronfeldt argues that when any one form captures or suppresses the others, the society degrades: crony capitalism is M capturing I; authoritarianism is I capturing T and M; tribalism in the pejorative sense is T resisting the legitimate functions of I and M.

The triform society matured across the seventeenth through twentieth centuries as market exchange, the joint-stock corporation, central banking, and international trade law layered onto existing tribal-institutional orders. The [[m-form-market|M form]]'s contribution is allocative efficiency at scale: coordination among strangers through price signals, without requiring kinship or hierarchical authority. Its limitation, as the BioConomy corpus documents extensively, is that price signals cannot see ecological function, unpaid care, cultural inheritance, or the health of the substrate on which production depends. These register only after they have been destroyed enough to generate a cost or enclosed enough to generate a revenue.

## The (+T+I+M)^+N test

The triform concept is the anchor for the corpus's central diagnostic. The (+T+I+M)^+N test asks: does a proposed fourth-form effort change the monetary substrate, or does it leave the substrate intact and add a coordination layer on top? If the monetary substrate remains, the architecture defaults to triform coordination with an overlay, however sophisticated the overlay. The test is operational: can the proposed fourth-form coordination persist when the +M substrate is removed? If it cannot, the system is still triform.

This diagnostic applies to most regenerative, commons-based, and social-enterprise initiatives operating today. [[impact-investing|Impact investing]], ESG frameworks, social enterprise, B-Corp certification, payment for ecosystem services, and carbon markets all operate within the triform substrate. They pursue +E outcomes (regeneration, social equity, ecological restoration) using +M instruments (equity, debt, blended finance, tradable credits). The +E intention is real. The structural position is triform with an overlay.

## The triform ceiling

The BioConomy corpus treats the triform configuration as carrying a structural ceiling on what coordination can achieve. The ceiling is set by the [[two-machines|Two Machines]] that Benjamin Life identifies as the M form's operating architecture at maturity: debt-based money (which requires growth to service interest) and the shareholder corporation (which requires extraction to service fiduciary duty). These two mechanisms convert every coordination outcome back into throughput. A regenerative initiative funded by debt must generate the revenue to service that debt. A cooperative structured as a shareholder vehicle must extract enough to satisfy fiduciary obligation. The triform ceiling means that within +T+I+M, the default behavioral incentive is extractive, and changing that default requires changing the substrate, not the participant.

The transition from triform to [[quadriform]] is the addition of the [[e-form-emergent|E form]]: a coordination layer that operates through pooled commitment at landscape scale, denominated in a currency whose logic is retention (through [[demurrage|demurrage]]) rather than throughput (through interest). The BioConomy's claim is that this transition is the one currently underway, and that the [[alpha-window|Alpha Window]] names the interval within which it must achieve structural viability.
