In hydrology, an anabranch is a section of a river that leaves the main channel and rejoins it further downstream [IC]. Michael Haupt uses the word for the whole arc of his [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] [MS]. The main channel is [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]], the [[glossary/f-l/golden-thread|Golden Thread]]. [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] is the stream that leaves it at the [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] and returns at the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]]. Below the Confluence both streams flow in one channel.

The image places no people or culture on either stream. Every structure of consciousness is present in every person now.
