In hydrology, an anabranch is a section of a river that leaves the main channel and rejoins it further downstream [IC]. Michael Haupt uses the word for the whole arc of his [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]] [MS]. The main channel is [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]], grounded, place-based thinking. [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]], abstract thinking that makes knowledge general and measurable so that it can travel, is the stream that leaves it at the [[glossary/a-e/bifurcation|Bifurcation]] and returns at the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]]. Below the Confluence the two learn to work side by side and braid into [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] [MS].

The image places no people or culture on either stream. Every structure of consciousness is present in every person now.

## Provenance

Written on 19 November 2026, when Michael Haupt named the arc of his consciousness thesis the Anabranch, with the Bifurcation and the Confluence as its two moments [MS]. On 22 November 2026 the main channel was renamed Municipal Consciousness, at Michael Haupt's decision; it read "Indigenous Knowledge Systems, the Golden Thread".
