A light registration through which a customary territory joins a restoration campaign or financing instrument: a boundary, one page of terms, and published offers, seeks and a baseline commitment.

## Extended definition

The full [[glossary/a-e/biohub|BioHub]] founding suite runs through several templates before a place can align to a financial instrument. A chief's council taking part in a regional campaign needs a shorter entry. The Chiefdom Coordination Surface gives it three outputs: the territory's boundary with the smaller units where work is done (headman areas or villages) marked inside it; Chiefdom Terms, a one-page form of the [[glossary/t-z/terms-of-engagement|Terms of Engagement]] covering consent, data, the division of payments and graduated sanctions; and a published list of offers and seeks with the territory's agreement to a baseline survey.

Registration is an act of coordination and makes no claim on land. Payments under it go to village [[glossary/a-e/commitment-pool|commitment pools]] with committees that include women and youth, and the customary authority receives a fixed, published stewardship fee for enforcing the Terms. Where many territories work toward one instrument, the campaign body runs the alignment work once on behalf of all of them.

## Contrast with adjacent terms

A [[glossary/a-e/coordination-surface|coordination surface]] is the general interface any BioHub publishes. The chiefdom form is a reduced version for a body whose authority is customary and whose capacity for template work is limited. It can grow into a full BioHub surface later.
