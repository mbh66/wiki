A unit of account issued at the bioregional scale, denominated in the ecological and coordination capacity of a specific place, and designed to circulate within and between bioregional coordination surfaces. The term names a class of instrument, not a single design. Multiple lineages converge on the idea; none has yet produced a bioregional currency operating at the scale the term implies.

The [[frameworks/emancipation-architecture|Emancipation Architecture]] specifies the most complete design: a [[demurrage|demurrage]]-based currency earned through verified regenerative activities (the [[regenerative-participation-income-rpi|Regenerative Participation Income]]), ecologically backed, bioregionally issued, and interconnected through a mycelial clearing layer that enables trade between bioregions without requiring a single global unit.

The [[bioregional-financing-facility-bff|Bioregional Financing Facility]] (BFF) architecture identifies the Bioregional Bank as the institutional entity that could issue a bioregional currency, and Power and Seefeld (2024) name the issuance of "nature-based currencies or other complementary currencies" as one of its functions. The BFF report, however, stops short of specifying a currency design. It proposes the institutional vessel (the bank) without prescribing the monetary architecture the vessel would carry. The currency question is left open for each bioregion to answer according to its conditions.

The [[concepts/obligation-and-invitation|Obligation and Invitation]] concept supplies the demand-side theory: a bioregional currency acquires circulation not through a tax obligation (the mechanism that drives demand for national fiat) but through access to goods and services that exist only within the bioregional coordination surface and cannot be purchased with the national unit.

For the full treatment of this instrument class, see [[frameworks/bioregional-currency|Bioregional Currency (framework)]].
