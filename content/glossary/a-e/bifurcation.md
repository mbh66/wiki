In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], the Bifurcation is the turn in which abstract thinking branched away from grounded, place-based thinking [MS]. The thesis places it over the last 12,000 years, from the first farms to the scientific revolution [MS]. Place-based thinking carried on as [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]], and [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] branched away, making knowledge general and measurable so that it could travel. It rose slowly at first. The thesis reads it as the force that drove the species up the steep part of the [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|S-curve]] after the scientific revolution [MS]. Michael Haupt offers one reading of the Garden of Eden story as an image of this turn [MS]; the reading is the thesis's image and sits outside the claim of step T7. The sources coded on [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/t7-the-bifurcation|step T7]] that accept such a turn date it to writing, the Axial Age, the scientific revolution or the modern state.

The word comes from the study of systems far from equilibrium, where Ilya Prigogine used it for a point at which a system can take one of several paths [IC]. Ervin Laszlo applies it to the present, as a choice point between breakdown and breakthrough [IC]. The Anabranch thesis places its Bifurcation in the past and calls the present moment the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]].

## Provenance

Written on 19 November 2026, when Michael Haupt named the arc of his consciousness thesis the Anabranch, with the Bifurcation and the Confluence as its two moments [MS]. On 20 November 2026 the date of the Bifurcation (the Neolithic Revolution, about 12,000 years ago) and the distinction between branching and leading were added. On 22 November 2026 the entry was rewritten to match the new wording of step T7, after Michael Haupt renamed the two streams. Later the same day the description was brought into line with T7. On 25 November 2026 the span was brought into line with the shorter wording of step T7, and the Garden of Eden reading was marked as the thesis's image, outside the claim.
