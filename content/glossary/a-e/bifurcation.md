In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], the Bifurcation is the moment at the Mythic to Material transition when human consciousness split into two streams [MS]. [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]] carried on as the main channel, and [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] branched away and drove the species up the steep part of the [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|S-curve]]. Michael Haupt reads the Garden of Eden story as one telling of this moment.

The word comes from the study of systems far from equilibrium, where Ilya Prigogine used it for a point at which a system can take one of several paths [IC]. Ervin Laszlo applies it to the present, as a choice point between breakdown and breakthrough [IC]. The Anabranch thesis places its Bifurcation in the past and calls the present moment the [[glossary/a-e/confluence|Confluence]].
