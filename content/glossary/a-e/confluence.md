In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], the Confluence is the moment, now beginning, when [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] rejoins the main channel of [[glossary/f-l/indigenous-knowledge|Indigenous Knowledge Systems]], and the two streams learn to work side by side towards place-based wholeness [MS]. Bioregional practice is how the thesis expects the joining to look on the ground.

In a river, the waters below a confluence carry what both streams brought. The thesis reads the flow below the Confluence the same way: what the Material stretch learned is kept and brought home to a place. That matches the position the network writes from: a new garden can be grown near the top of the curve, by people who keep what the climb taught them and bring it home to a place.
