In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/anabranch/index|Anabranch thesis]], the Confluence is the moment, now beginning as growth meets its limits, when [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] and [[glossary/m-s/municipal-consciousness|Municipal Consciousness]] learn to work side by side, rooted in place and connected across the planet, and braid into [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] [MS]. Together, the thesis holds, they can create the conditions for thriving. [[glossary/a-e/bioregionalism|Bioregional]] and [[glossary/a-e/cosmo-local-production|cosmo-local]] commons are two of the forms the thesis expects this to take on the ground [MS].

In a river, the waters below a confluence carry what both streams brought. The thesis reads the flow below the Confluence the same way: what the Material stretch learned is kept and brought home to a place. That matches the position the network writes from: a new garden can be grown near the top of the curve, by people who keep what the climb taught them and bring it home to a place.
