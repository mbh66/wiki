The private narrative through which a person explains to themselves what is happening, who is responsible and what comes next.

## Extended definition

Self-talk works on the upper rungs of the [[ladder-of-inference|ladder of inference]], where assumptions become conclusions and conclusions become beliefs. It carries the ladder's reflexive loop: self-talk remembers the conclusion, and the conclusion tells the [[lens]] where to look next time. It also absorbs the substrate. "I worked for what I have" is a Market-form conclusion experienced as a personal truth.

Cognitive biases are self-talk's working tools. Four do most damage when perspectives meet:

- naive realism, the conviction that one sees the world as it is, so those who disagree must be misinformed or acting in bad faith ([[ross-lee|Ross]] and Ward 1996);
- reactive devaluation, discounting a proposal because of who tabled it (Ross and Stillinger 1991);
- the ultimate attribution error, crediting a rival group's failures to its nature and its successes to circumstance (Pettigrew 1979);
- confirmation bias, the reflexive loop itself.

## Contrast with common usage

In counseling and sports psychology, self-talk means inner dialogue in general. The corpus uses the term more narrowly, for the narrative layer of reasoning that sits above the lens. The boundary leaks: self-talk also decides which lens a person reaches for in a given setting.

## Use in governance

Exposing self-talk carries risk, and in a room with power asymmetries the risk falls on whoever holds least power. Governance practice therefore works on it privately or indirectly: the left-hand column exercise done alone or in pairs, blind proposal rounds against reactive devaluation, and acknowledgment rounds, which reduce false polarization where restating one's own position does not (Sherman, Nelson and Ross 2003). Teaching participants about naive realism can open the least open among them (Nasie et al. 2014).

Lens and self-talk entered the model in a Zoom dialogue between Michael Haupt and Michael Keller (Ecology of Design in Human Systems) on 25 September 2026. This use of the term is Michael Haupt's development of it (working hypothesis).
