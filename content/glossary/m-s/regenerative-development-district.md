A Regenerative Development District is the area inside which [[life-benjamin|Benjamin Life]] places a [[bioregional-development-organization-bdo|Bioregional Development Organization]], working "inside a Regenerative Development District via a Bioregional Development Plan."

## Extended definition

The phrase appears once in *The Missing Middle of Bioregional Development* (Part II, October 2026). Life does not define it, say who designates it, or give it a legal, tax or regulatory regime of its own. The name reads as a counterpart to the US Economic Development District, the federal designation that nearly four hundred Regional Development Organizations hold, and that designation is a planning and funding status built around a regional strategy. That reading is the wiki's inference. Life does not state it.

In the founding suite, the area a plan covers is the [[bioregion|BioRegion]] as its BioRegion Definition sets it. Where a place wants a district with standing in law, the legal content is the place's own construction and its jurisdiction's to grant.

## Contrast with adjacent terms

**Special Economic Zone.** A designated area under its own customs, tax or regulatory regime. Life attaches no such regime to the Regenerative Development District, and the two should not be read as equivalents.

**Economic Development District.** The US federal designation for a multi-county region with an approved Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy. Its boundaries are administrative. Life requires bioregional boundaries.
