A geofenced digital layer on the Guardians of Earth platform associated with a real-world location (nature reserve, urban green space, school, resort, backyard, any location at all). Each Realm has its own BioScore composite metric and can accumulate biocultural evidence, species observations, and stories from the community engaged with the place. Realms compete in biannual BioQuest competitions each April and October.
