Mythic Consciousness is the third of the five [[glossary/m-s/structure-of-consciousness|structures of consciousness]] in the corpus's [[concepts/major-evolutionary-transitions|Major Evolutionary Transitions]]. It's the form that organises experience and coordination through shared story, symbol and cosmology [MS].

## Extended definition

In Mythic Consciousness, people make sense of the world through narrative. Authority, belonging and the order of things are held in shared stories, and in the rituals and symbols that carry them [MS]. It follows the Mimetic and Magical structures and comes before [[glossary/m-s/material-consciousness|Material Consciousness]] [MS].

The name is Michael Haupt's, as are the other four names in the sequence [MS]. It draws on three lineages, and each keeps its own terms.

- Jean Gebser calls it the mythical structure, one of five in *The Ever-Present Origin* [IC].
- Merlin Donald calls it mythic culture. He ties it to spoken language and narrative, and places it with archaic *Homo sapiens*, about 300,000 years ago [IC].
- Roger Briggs calls it the Mythic macro-stage. He dates it from about 60,000 years ago, and holds that spoken language gave birth to Mythic culture (*Emerging World*, ch. 5, pp. 164, 166) [TBV].

The dates differ widely. Michael Haupt's working page places Mythic Consciousness about 70,000 years ago [MS]. The Major Evolutionary Transitions page ties it to the great civilisations of antiquity, about 5,000 years ago [MS]. The [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]] reads these as answers to two different questions: when a capacity first appears, and when a structure comes to lead coordination at civilisational scale (inference).

Every structure stays alive in every person [MS]. Mythic Consciousness names a way of knowing that anyone can use, and it never places a people or a person behind anyone else.

## Contrast with adjacent terms

- **Myth as falsehood.** In everyday speech a myth is a story that isn't true. Here, "mythic" names a way of holding knowledge in story. It makes no claim about whether a story is true.
- **Material Consciousness.** Mythic Consciousness carries meaning in narrative. Material Consciousness carries it in measurement and analysis [MS]. The two tell the same events in different languages. The network's own example is the [[glossary/f-l/garden-of-eden|Garden of Eden]]: the Mythic structure tells it as a story of a garden, a fruit and a serpent, and the Material structure tells it as a growth curve [MS].
