Ilja Aleksandar Panić is a Czech designer who came to fungi through web technology, and who curates Cyberbiomes [IC].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Ilja Panić only through public roles and published work. He has not yet been invited to comment; any reply or correction will be recorded here with its date.

## Contribution

Cyberbiomes describes itself as an eco-speculative project exploring concepts, projects and initiatives where nature, culture and technology meet [IC]. It holds a lexicon of terms (among them biosemiotics, mycelium and solarpunk), a list of projects, a book list and references [IC]. In an interview with CzechCrunch, Panić described turning to mycelium from years of work in web technologies, while studying an experimental design course on emerging futures, and named the mycologist Paul Stamets as an inspiration [IC].

Michael Haupt's list of people bringing [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] into business and life names Panić for Cyberbiomes [MS]. The wiki reads his work as practice at the edge of biology and digital design, where fungal science and the mycelial metaphor meet; the [[sources/karst-2023-positive-citation-bias|Karst and colleagues]] critique is the reason the wiki keeps the two apart.
