Josie Watson is recorded as the founder of Mycelial Law, an Earth-law practice in the United States [TBV].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Josie Watson only through public roles and published work. She has not yet been invited to comment; any reply or correction will be recorded here with its date.

## Contribution

Mycelial Law presents itself as law as the mycelium practises it: a specific branch of Earth law, the legal practice of building a civilisation in service to all life, in the lineage of Thomas Berry, the Global Alliance for the Rights of Nature and the Earth Law Center [IC]. Its writing frames the work as grassroots Earth law, asking how the principles of representing the Earth's rights can be brought into ordinary communities [IC]. A Josie Watson has also written on Earth jurisprudence and Mātauranga Māori in New Zealand for the *Yale Environment Review*; whether this is the same person is unconfirmed [TBV].

Michael Haupt's list of people bringing [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] into business and life names Watson as the founder of Mycelial Law [MS]. The wiki reads the practice as an application of the mycelial metaphor to law; the metaphor is the practice's own.
