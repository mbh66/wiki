Ariana Bahrami is the founder of Hy, which describes itself as an incubator for a life-centric economy [IC].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Ariana Bahrami only through public roles and published work. She has not yet been invited to comment; any reply or correction will be recorded here with its date.

## Contribution

Hy runs local incubators built on self-discovery, close connection and the human capacities that technology will not replace [IC]. Its programmes are named The Forest (self-discovery), The Meadow (connection and co-creation) and The Mountain (turning what emerges into economic activity), and it has local nodes in the San Francisco Bay Area and Lisbon [IC]. People apply, join gatherings and co-create projects meant to give back as they grow [IC].

Michael Haupt's list of people bringing [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] into business and life names Bahrami as the creator of Hy Playground [MS]. The wiki reads Hy as one practice of [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-coordination|mycelial coordination]]: distributed, relational ways of building enterprise. That reading is the network's own; Hy's public pages use the language of life and nature, and do not use the mycelial metaphor [IC].
