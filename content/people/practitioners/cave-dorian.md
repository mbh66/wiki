Dorian Cavé is a researcher and facilitator of self-organised groups, and an associated researcher at the Weizenbaum Institute for the Networked Society in Berlin [IC]. He studied international relations and sustainable development at Sciences Po in Paris [IC].

> [!note] Right of reply
> This page describes Dorian Cavé only through public roles and published work. He has not yet been invited to comment; any reply or correction will be recorded here with its date.

## Contribution

Cavé's doctoral thesis, *Connecting to Make a Difference: Social Learning and Radical Collective Change in Prefigurative Online Communities* (2023), was completed through Lancaster University and the Institute for Leadership and Sustainability at the University of Cumbria, supervised by Jem Bendell, Tom Grimwood and Kaz Stuart [IC]. It reports two participatory action research projects in online communities that try to live out the change they seek; one set out to build a transnational, decentralised grassroots economic system as an alternative to global capitalism [IC]. The thesis ends by arguing for decolonial approaches to social change as responses to the global predicament [IC].

Michael Haupt's list of people bringing [[glossary/m-s/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]] into business and life names Cavé for this work on online communities aimed at radical change [MS]. His writing for the MADO collective calls for a mycelium of change initiatives [TBV]. The wiki reads his research as evidence on how networked groups learn, which bears on the claim that a networked, collective intelligence is emerging (step C3 of the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]]).
