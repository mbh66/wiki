Czech theologian, philosopher and educator (1592-1670), the last bishop of the original Unity of the Brethren (Unitas Fratrum), who argued for universal education regardless of sex or social standing [IC]. He is widely called the father of modern education.

## Contribution

Comenius held that learning is as natural as breathing, so education is part of nature's own formative process and a healthy society makes it the axis on which it turns. His *Didactica Magna* set out universal schooling and teaching through the senses and real-world content; *Orbis Pictus* (1658) was among the first illustrated schoolbooks for children. Jonathan Rowson names him as the most likely single intellectual forebear of [[concepts/bildung|Bildung]], and records the story that he declined an offer to lead Harvard College [TBV]. The wiki's research brief [[research/comenius-to-schmidt-chain|The Comenius-to-Schmidt Chain]] follows his pedagogy through the Moravian Church to the artisan-missionary model.

## Selected works

- *Janua Linguarum Reserata* (1631)
- *Didactica Magna* (written 1628-1632, published 1657)
- *Orbis Sensualium Pictus* (1658)
