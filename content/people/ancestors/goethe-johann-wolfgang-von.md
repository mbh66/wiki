German poet, novelist, statesman and natural scientist (1749-1832), author of *Faust* and of *Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship* (1795-1796), the model of the *Bildungsroman*, the novel of a young person's formation [IC].

## Contribution

Goethe joins both halves of the tradition on [[concepts/aesthetic-education|Aesthetic Education]]. In literature, *Wilhelm Meister* gave [[concepts/bildung|Bildung]] its narrative form: a young person leaves home, is changed by experience, and finds a place in society. In science, *The Metamorphosis of Plants* (1790) and the *Theory of Colours* (1810) studied nature through patient perception of form and change. Jonathan Rowson places Goethe, with [[people/ancestors/humboldt-alexander-von|Alexander von Humboldt]], among those who received the Third Earl of Shaftesbury's aesthetic view of nature, and credits him with perceptions Darwin later built on; that last claim is Rowson's [TBV].

## Selected works

- *The Metamorphosis of Plants* (1790)
- *Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship* (1795-1796)
- *Faust* (Part One 1808, Part Two 1832)
- *Theory of Colours* (1810)
