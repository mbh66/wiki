The South African San Council represents the three San communities of South Africa: the ‡Khomani of the southern Kalahari, and the !Xun and Khwe, most of whom live at Platfontein near Kimberley [IC]. Its chair and vice-chair have been drawn from the ‡Khomani and the Khwe [IC]. It was set up in 2001 within the Working Group of Indigenous Minorities in Southern Africa (WIMSA) [TBV]. This page describes the Council only through its published record; it has not been invited to comment, and any reply will be recorded here with its date.

## Contribution

In March 2017 the Council launched the San Code of Research Ethics in Cape Town, the first research ethics code from an Indigenous people in Africa [IC]. The code asks researchers to commit to respect, honesty, justice and fairness, and care, and sets a process through which San communities approve research and its outputs [IC]. The Council was also a party, with the National Khoi and San Council, to the 2019 benefit-sharing agreement with the rooibos industry [IC]. The wiki follows the code in how it reports San knowledge: through San institutions and published San testimony, never translated into the network's structure names, and with no location of rock art or burial sites beyond the official heritage record.
