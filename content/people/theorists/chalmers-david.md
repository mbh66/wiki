David J. Chalmers is an Australian philosopher and cognitive scientist, University Professor of Philosophy and Neural Science at New York University and co-director of its Center for Mind, Brain and Consciousness [IC].

## Contribution

In a 1995 paper Chalmers separated the easy problems of consciousness, which concern what the brain does, from the [[glossary/f-l/hard-problem|hard problem]]: why that processing is felt at all [IC]. *The Conscious Mind* (1996) argues that consciousness does not follow logically from the physical and proposes a naturalistic dualism, in which experience is a fundamental feature tied to information [IC]. With Andy Clark he proposed the extended mind (1998), and *Reality+* (2022) turns to virtual worlds [IC]. Dennett and other physicialists reject his central arguments [IC]. In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]] Chalmers states one side of the hinge, step H.
