Iain McGilchrist is a British psychiatrist and writer, a former Fellow of All Souls College, Oxford, and a former consultant psychiatrist at the Bethlem Royal and Maudsley Hospital in London [IC].

## Contribution

In *The Master and His Emissary* (2009) McGilchrist argues that the right hemisphere attends to the whole, the living and the unique, while the left attends to parts, categories and use, and that Western culture since the ancient Greeks has shifted toward the left hemisphere's mode [IC]. *The Matter with Things* (2021) extends the argument into philosophy and science [IC]. Some neuroscientists dispute the cultural history he builds on the hemisphere research [TBV]. Tom Morgan's *Anomaly Grid* draws on him for its claim that the left hemisphere blocks the signal. In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]] McGilchrist affirms steps A3, C1 and C4.
