South African forest hydrologist whose catchment studies at Jonkershoek, near Stellenbosch, measured how pine plantations reduce streamflow from fynbos catchments. His 1987 paper in *Water SA* is the base rate the wiki cites for the water cost of pine invasion in the fynbos biome.

## Contribution

Stellenbosch University holds an MSc thesis by Daniel Bartholomeus van Wyk (1977) on the effect of afforestation with *Pinus radiata* on the total annual runoff of the Jonkershoek streams. The wiki takes this thesis and the 1987 paper to be the work of the same researcher.

The 1987 paper documented a 55 percent reduction in streamflow, from 600 mm to 270 mm, in fynbos catchments 23 years after pine afforestation. The finding anchors the defensible water claim on [[research/bioprecipitation|Bioprecipitation]]: clearing invasive alien trees restores runoff and infiltration. It also underpins the business case for the Greater Cape Town Water Fund.

## Selected works

- Van Wyk, D.B. (1977). *Die invloed van bebossing met Pinus radiata op die totale jaarlikse afvoer van die Jonkershoek strome*. MSc thesis, Stellenbosch University.
- Van Wyk, D.B. (1987). "Some effects of afforestation on streamflow in the Western Cape Province." *Water SA*.
