Tomas Björkman is a Swedish social entrepreneur and author. He founded the investment bank IBP Fondkommission in 1990 and left finance in 2006 [IC]. In 2008 he established the Ekskäret Foundation, and in 2016 he co-founded the research institute Perspectiva in London with [[people/theorists/rowson-jonathan|Jonathan Rowson]] [IC]. He has been a member of the Club of Rome since 2014, and is a co-funder and the chair of the Inner Development Goals Foundation [IC].

## Contribution

With [[people/theorists/andersen-lene-rachel|Lene Rachel Andersen]], Björkman produced *The Nordic Secret* (2017), which argues that [[concepts/bildung|Bildung]], spread through the Nordic [[glossary/f-l/folk-high-school|folk high schools]], carried the Nordic countries into modern democracy, and which links Bildung to Robert Kegan's orders of consciousness. Some records list him as the book's editor. *The Market Myth* (2016) argues that the market is a social construction. *The World We Create* (2019) develops his account of how societies and their members develop together. Through Ekskäret and the [[glossary/f-l/inner-development-goals-idgs|Inner Development Goals]], he has carried the concern with inner development into the sustainable development field.
