Roger P. Briggs is an American physicist turned deep-time historian whose work synthesizes paleoanthropology, cognitive psychology, and evolutionary theory into a unified account of consciousness evolution across civilizational scale. He is co-founder of The Planet Project and author of two books that trace the full arc from cosmic origins to the present developmental threshold.

## Contribution to the BioConomy Corpus

Briggs provides two contributions to the corpus.

The first is a sequence of five macro-stages of culture and consciousness, built on [[sources/gebser-ever-present-origin|Jean Gebser]], Merlin Donald and Arthur Young together with the archaeological record. They are the Mimetic, from about three million years ago; the Magical, one million; the Mythic, 60,000; the Material, about 5,000 years ago with the first civilisations; and Planetary consciousness, now emerging, which he equates with Gebser's Integral (*Emerging World*, ch. 5, p. 166) [TBV]. The Mental, Mental Rational and Postmodern stages that Gebser and Ken Wilber describe are, for Briggs, sub-stages within the Material, which after Young he expects to hold seven (pp. 165-166) [TBV]. The BioConomy corpus adopted and extended this synthesis into its own [[concepts/major-evolutionary-transitions|Major Evolutionary Transitions]] sequence (Mimetic, Magical, Mythic, Material, Mycelial), with Briggs's explicit agreement that these shifts qualify as cognitive METs [MS].

The second is his reading of the present as the end of the Material era. He holds that after 5,000 years of domination and exploitation, Material consciousness has reached the limits of the planet's life-support systems, and that the one hope is a new consciousness emerging at large scale (ch. 5, pp. 170, 182) [TBV]. He reads the collapse of classical physics between 1905 and 1930 as a preview of that shift (pp. 171-178), and calls the present a technological bottleneck in a passage he marks as his own speculation (pp. 182-186) [TBV].

The civilisational S-curve the corpus uses is Michael Haupt's own reading, set out in the [[frameworks/s-curve-thesis|S-Curve Thesis]] [MS]. It underpins the [[frameworks/three-futures|Three Futures]] analysis and the urgency argument in Movement I of the Emancipation Architecture [MS]. *Emerging World* places no [[glossary/m-s/s-curve|logistic curve]] on civilisation. Its one growth graph shows the pace of evolution accelerating more and more steeply up to the present (ch. 6, p. 223) [TBV].

Michael Haupt describes Briggs as a friend, after numerous exchanges and calls, and credits *Emerging World* as a "beautiful synthesis" of the consciousness evolution literature that became one of the structural pillars of the thesis.

## Key Works

**Journey to Civilization: The Science of How We Got Here** (2013). Briggs's first book recaps the evolution of the cosmos, Earth, life on Earth, and humanity from three million years ago to the beginning of recorded civilization. A polymath tour incorporating the latest scientific discoveries and theories, it tells humanity's common origin story.

**Emerging World: The Evolution of Consciousness and the Future of Humanity** (2021). Eight years in the writing, this sequel picks up where *Journey* ended: at the beginning of civilization five thousand years ago. Briggs traces a progression of cultural mindsets through which humanity has evolved, walking the line between scientific and spiritual polarities. He encapsulates the common ground with a hybrid concept he calls "Planetary Consciousness." The book includes no political history and little technological progress. It identifies instead the progression of consciousness structures that the BioConomy corpus maps as the MET sequence.

## The Planet Project

Briggs co-founded The Planet Project (planetproject.io), which maintains a database of aligned change agents and publications tracking the transition ecosystem. He has done extensive mapping of what he calls "the landscape": the global network of organizations, thinkers, and initiatives working on civilizational transition. Much of the ecosystem mapping referenced in the BioConomy corpus's shiftscaping work draws on this effort.

As of May 2026, Briggs is working on the Positive Media Lab (positivemedialab.com) with Kurt Johnson and Amanda Sanders.

## Relationship to Other Thinkers

Briggs operates at the intersection of several lineages represented in the BioConomy corpus. His consciousness evolution work draws directly on [[sources/gebser-ever-present-origin|Gebser's]] structures of consciousness and extends them through Donald's cognitive archaeology, Young's process theory, and Wilber's integral framework. His account of the end of the Material era sits beside Joseph Tainter's research on the collapse of complex societies, Peter Turchin's cliodynamics and the Strauss-Howe generational theory, which the [[frameworks/three-futures|Three Futures]] analysis draws on together (inference).

Briggs was introduced to the broader network through [[sources/sahtouris-earthdance|Elisabet Sahtouris]], Louis Herman, and Brian Swimme, placing him within the living-systems evolution lineage that provides the biological grounding for the corpus's maturation thesis.
