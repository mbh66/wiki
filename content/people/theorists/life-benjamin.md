Author of the two-part *An Introduction to Bioregional Economics* (2026), published on his Substack, omniharmonic. Part I (August 2026) provides the discipline's fullest current specification, including the Two Machines diagnosis, the four pillars, and the missing-middle question. Part II, *The Missing Middle of Bioregional Development* (October 2026), proposes the [[bioregional-development-organization-bdo|Bioregional Development Organization]] and the bioregional learning center as the institutions of that middle. Life writes from the Front Range of Colorado, where he describes his involvement with the Regen Hub in Boulder.
