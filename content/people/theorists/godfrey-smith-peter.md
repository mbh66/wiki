Peter Godfrey-Smith is an Australian philosopher of science and professor in the School of History and Philosophy of Science at the University of Sydney [IC].

## Contribution

In *Other Minds* (2016) Godfrey-Smith uses the octopus, whose mind evolved on a separate branch from ours, to ask how subjective experience arose in animals [IC]. *Metazoa* (2020) traces the evolution of sensing and acting bodies from early animals to vertebrates, and argues that experience comes in graded forms tied to how an animal lives [IC]. He writes as a naturalist, with close attention to field observation and to the history of life [IC]. In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/library|library]] his work supports step A1, that minds evolved in graded forms across many lineages.
