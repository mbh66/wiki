Daniel Schmachtenberger is an American social philosopher and a founding member of The Consilience Project, which publishes on the conditions for better collective sensemaking [IC]. He also co-founded the Neurohacker Collective [IC].

## Contribution

Schmachtenberger's talks describe the metacrisis: risks driven by rivalry, exponential technology and the erosion of shared sensemaking [IC]. In "In Search of the Third Attractor" (Rebel Wisdom, August 2022) he argues that current dynamics pull toward catastrophe or toward dystopian control, and that a [[glossary/t-z/third-attractor|third attractor]] needs new collective capacities for sensemaking and choice [IC]. His work is published as talks, podcasts and essays, outside peer review [IC]. He is named on Michael Haupt's reading [MS]. In the [[research/evolution-of-consciousness/grid|evolution of consciousness grid]] he affirms steps C2, C3 and C4.
